# EGWEE corrected EuPPollNet: cross-study bee–hoverfly floral-use partition
**Outcome-blind quantitative estimand freeze: 2026-11-18.** Source schemas, guild response counts, network effects and signs have NOT been inspected in this branch at registration.

## Purpose and role

Empirically test whether **two co-observed functional guilds use the same flowering plant spectrum within a network**, after controlling for the observed (pooled-guild) floral-use frequencies and finite insect sample size. This is a *resource-use interaction* macroecology test in empirical \`egwee\`, not an analysis of reproductive fitness, genetically compatible pollen, fragmentation g, the UK PoMS method comparison or theoretical \`egwe\`.

## Biological estimand (frozen before guild results)

Within **one source-defined network** (\`Study_id\` + \`Network_id\` + year where required by native data), classify focal pollinators only:
- **Wild bees**: source family in {Apidae, Megachilidae, Andrenidae, Halictidae, Colletidae, Melittidae, Stenotritidae}, with \`Apis mellifera\` excluded at species name; source Hymenoptera family needs to be verifiable.
- **Hoverflies**: source family Syrphidae (not all Diptera).
- Exclude other/ambiguous pollinator groups, incomplete family/species labels, and records without a source-accepted plant taxon. Whitelist exact normalized family names only; report all exclusions. Source taxonomy fields must pass documented schema-identity gate before numerical analysis.

For each eligible network: at least **20 wild-bee and 20 syrphid records** in the same method/network, at least **3 unique plant taxa in their pooled interactions**. Observed plant-use distributions \`b_p/B\` and \`h_p/H\` define Jensen–Shannon divergence (\`JSD\` in natural-log units; half KL to their mean). No adding zeros, no pseudocount; use 0 log 0 = 0. For finite-sample correction, simulate **199 draws** from the exact multivariate hypergeometric distribution with fixed network plant visit totals \`n_p=b_p+h_p\`, total wild-bee visit count \`B\`, total syrphid count \`H\`. This null assigns guild labels randomly among observed visits **conditional on their pooled plant-use availability and sample sizes**, without importing independently measured floral abundance (which may not be harmonized across studies). Fixed RNG \`20261118\`, stable sorted network order. Primary network effect \`E = observed_JSD - mean(null_JSD)\`; 0 compatible with no detectable guild floral-use partition after finite-count correction.

Summary estimators:
1. Compute \`E\` for every eligible network, then average networks equally *within each original study programme*, then average the study means **equally** across programmes. This is the primary cross-programme estimand; a study with 100 networks is not 100 independent studies.
2. Whole-programme bootstrap **2,000 draws**, retaining all networks within each sampled programme and resampling programme means, seed \`20261119\`; percentile 95% interval. Report % programmes with positive effect, median, and leave-one-programme-out means. Predeclare directional support only if bootstrap CI excludes zero and >=70% programme means positive; otherwise describe as unresolved. Do not test each network then count only significant networks.
3. Secondary descriptive equal-network mean and survey-method stratification (no source-method types merged to infer population response). If co-observation across groups is insufficient, return \`COVERAGE_STOP\`, not a selected subset after reading signs.

## Predictor coverage and additional bounds

- Independently measured flower counts, \`Flower_data_merger\`, may permit a later resource-supply comparison but are **not** the null's availability denominator: its null uses pooled observed visits. Do not mislabel \`E\` as distance from floral abundance or resource partition after correcting supply. Cross-study floral *quantity/cover* measurements are heterogeneous.
- Only after this main result is frozen and reported may one undertake a separately marked exploratory independent flower count association; no moving to a resource predictor to chase a significance result.
- Identification limits: this may reflect taxon-specific flower choice, phenology, morphology or heterogeneous field detection. No proof of competition, reproductive success, successful compatible pollen, seed quality, pollinator abundance or fragmentation causation. Existing research already knows guild differences; novelty depends on a source-validated, study-weighted, finite-sampling-corrected **magnitude and generalization** (not merely rejecting random choice).
