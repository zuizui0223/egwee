For each offspring with **at least four jointly typed** of the six microsatellite loci, the prespecified indicator equals one when any called offspring allele was absent from both maternal alleles at the corresponding locus. Its **mother-level fraction** is a **lower bound on detectable other-parent alleles**, *not* a full MLTR outcrossing rate or an assigned father. Some genetically outcrossed offspring inherit only markers already observed in the mother, while sequencing/typing errors can mimic nonmaternal alleles. Mothers sharing a family and 2014/2015 nursery ancestry are not treated as independent species/landscape studies.

## 2. Frozen whole-site predictive test

**M0:** 2015, SC/SI, year×SC, log(1 + lifetime flower number), days to flower, and maternal heterozygous microsatellite locus fraction.

**M1:** M0 plus log(1 + source-defined AH/BF visits per observed open flower-hour), computed from the same original positional visit records as frozen LA001. The official Dryad file description defines `BF` as bee-fly and `B` as honeybee/carpenter bee, while a separate 2014 temporal worksheet contains an inconsistent `B = Butterfly` label; this is recorded as a source-codebook limitation. Source visit-token denominators are **not** equated with the paper's unresolved 3,748 published visits.

Each mother has (k) offspring with a detectable nonmaternal allele among (n) eligible offspring. Fit mother-level `log((k+.5)/(n−k+.5))` by an unweighted train-only-standardized ridge model (lambda=1); evaluate heldout sigmoid probability against the raw maternal `k/n` using one Brier error per mother. Each of four folds leaves out **an entire site including both years**. Positive support was prespecified only when pooled M0−M1 error is positive *and* at least three of four folds improve; no extra seeds, loci, site exclusions or penalty tuning after results.

| Held-out site | Mothers | Genotyped offspring | M0 Brier | M1 Brier | Gain M0−M1 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Waco | 56 | 421 | 0.067985 | 0.068745 | **−0.000760** |
| Quarry | 63 | 485 | 0.044796 | 0.043821 | +0.000975 |
| 24-43 | 51 | 363 | 0.094424 | 0.094419 | **+0.000005** |
| Landersville | 63 | 473 | 0.059481 | 0.058089 | +0.001392 |
| **Pooled** | **233** | **1,742** | **0.065203** | **0.064744** | **+0.000458** |

**Registered decision: `BOUNDED_WITHIN_STUDY_VISIT_TO_DETECTABLE_OUTCROSSING_INCREMENT`.** The two-part test passes (positive pooled score, 3/4 site-fold scores), but the absolute improvement is only **0.000458 Brier**, or **0.70%** relative, and one nominal positive site has a practically negligible increment (+0.000005). No credible out-of-study generality or causal function can be inferred from four sites.

## 3. Parent-genotype source check and relation to the original article

The direct marker indicator finds the following mean mother-level fractions (not allele-frequency-weighted MLTR estimators):

| Year | Maternal type | Mothers | Admitted genotyped offspring | Mean fraction with a detectable nonmaternal allele |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 2014 | SI | 54 | 373 | 0.958 |
| 2014 | SC | 55 | 403 | 0.365 |
| 2015 | SI | 64 | 495 | 0.974 |
| 2015 | SC | 60 | 471 | 0.303 |

**This SC/SI differentiation is NOT a new finding.** Original authors used MLTR v3.3 to estimate genuine multilocus outcrossing rates: SI `0.877–0.992`, SC `0.186–0.397`, and they also estimated paternity and seed discounting. See Layman et al. (2017), Results, Fig. 4. The current simple marker indicator is different, lower-bound in interpretation, and was selected to permit a transparent exact mother-source pairing/quality audit rather than to supplant the published MLTR estimates.

## 4. Ecological meaning and scientific limit

LA001 and LA002, using lifetime seed **count** as the outcome, failed their predeclared four-site visit-rate incremental prediction tests (M0 had lower pooled MSE). LA005 measures a different and more directly interaction-related response: **detectable other-parent marker inheritance among a subset of offspring**. It offers a **small** positive incremental association after SC class and flowering covariates, but does **not** prove a mechanism for the divergence between seed quantity and genetic mating state: the outcomes, denominators, sampling windows and potential confounders differ. Visit counts are not measured compatible pollen deposition, and S-locus genotype class is perfectly confounded with SC/SI in field subjects.

**The stronger and still open problem** is to test whether measured pollen compatibility/donor diversity and same-cohort *viable offspring recruitment* explain functional fate in **independently replicated systems** under harmonized observations. Neither the first finite EGC/EGWE sorting-plus-repair model nor a general island/urban functional-fragmentation law has been validated by LA005.
