# LA001 — individual visit exposure to lifetime seed production (prospective within-study reanalysis)

This is a narrow, **one original-programme** holdout prediction audit of Layman et al. (2017), *Evolution* 71:884–897, DOI [10.1111/evo.13167](https://doi.org/10.1111/evo.13167), original archive [10.5061/dryad.2pj0j](https://doi.org/10.5061/dryad.2pj0j). **This is not a new natural eco-genetic mechanism validation or an independent reproduction of the original authors' discovery.**

### Source and exact pre-outcome linkage

Original user-supplied ZIP SHA256 `82bcaa1c3a504cc779bc9c2aa95199a657dd404b3fdf62f7059e4ff5028b39c1`. Nine original Excel workbooks and three original model/code files. Original arrays: 2014 and 2015 × Waco, Quarry, 24-43, Landersville, exactly 32 plants/site/year (16 SC and 16 SI). Field rows 256; source `First Flower` mappings give 256/256 unambiguous year×site×plant identifiers; each field key also matches S-locus original genotype. Field S locus calls `A` for SC and `D` for SI without residual within-genotype genetic variation; **genetic mate compatibility cannot be separately identified**. Greenhouse Year×Family×Individual IDs are not equivalent to field record IDs (only one literal overlap) and must not be joined.

The raw array visit sheets are event sequences rather than plant-by-column counts. Their 121 **completed** 30-min observation periods (2014 45; 2015 76) match the Methods count; 125 `Open Flowers` rows include four 2015 sessions with no completion `TOTAL`. Within the 121 complete periods 6,701 valid 1–32 position tokens occur, with 38 tokens targeting positions whose logged open flower count was zero, and one additional token `34` outside 32 array plants. Primary exposure excludes these 39 problematic tokens; all are retained as provenance/quality counts, and sensitivity may include the 38 zero-flower tokens without assuming missingness is random. One recorded `TOTAL` disagrees with valid token sum by one due to the `34` token. No signed seed association has been examined to choose these exclusions.

### Frozen comparison

**Primary** same individual × site × year reproductive endpoint `log1p(seed total)`, excluding source 4 missing seed totals while retaining zero counts. Baseline ridge prediction includes year, field SC vs SI, year×SC, lifetime flower number and days to flowering. Added predictor is `log1p((AH+BF visits)/(observed open flower-hours))` because the original paper's single-visit experiment identified solitary bees and bee-flies as effective groups; visit data alone are not compatible pollen. Compare against baseline by leaving both years of each of **four complete sites** out. A nontrivial, cross-site predictive increment requires positive pooled MSE gain and positive fold gain for at least 3/4 sites. Secondary comparator uses total visits under the same exposure rule.

**Do not** refit with selected pollinator categories, switch seed outcome to fruit set or seed/flower, add years/sites or tests, tune ridge penalty, or treat 252 plants as independent landscapes if the primary fails. This is a **one-study predictive adequacy pilot** only; no causal effect of S-alleles vs visitation can be identified because source marker type is fully confounded with SC/SI, and there is no direct pollen-compatibility measurement per experimental mother.
