# LA002: can separately assayed per-visit seed efficacy improve spatial transfer?
**Frozen before opening any LA002 field-seed predictive outcome, 2026-11-17.** See `LA002_FROZEN_SINGLE_VISIT_PROTOCOL_20261117.json`. This is a single original study's model-transport test, NOT independent replication or evidence for a new eco-genetic law.

The prior LA001 analysis linked 256 field subjects to site-year visit positions and source S-locus type, with 252 complete lifetime seed counts. 4 whole-site holdouts showed no stable improvement from AH+BF visit-count rate. Its negative decision and `M0=0.670754`, `M1=0.701567` MSE stay fixed.

New, **separate** source workbook `Emasculation+experiment.xlsx`, Final pass contains 72 emasculated single-visit or bagged control observations (22 solitary bee, 9 bee-fly, 17 other fly, 2 honeybee, 1 ant, 21 control). The original authors' efficacy assay is a previously published result, not a newly run ecological experiment. Fixed raw expected seed-per-visit weights including zero-fruit flowers: AH=57/22≈2.591, BF=23/9≈2.556, F=7/17≈0.412. Honeybee/ant/unassayed codes have inadequate matching experiment replication, so are *excluded from the calibrated rate*, **not assigned biological incompetence**. The 21 control flowers have 1 total seed, an unmodeled low background.

Apply these separate, frozen efficacy weights to observed **position-decoded** field visit counts and divide by measured flower-hours. Primary new feature is `log1p((AH*(57/22)+BF*(23/9)+F*(7/17))/flower-hours)`. Never use 252 field plants' seed outcomes to estimate those weights.

Original outcome remains `log1p(total lifetime seeds)` and M0 ridge parameters remain year, SC, year×SC, lifetime flowers, flowering days, lambda=1. Compare M3 quality weight against M0 with 4 whole-site folds (both years excluded) on all 252 plants, reporting all fold errors. Secondary M3 vs original M1; cannot rescue primary by winning that one. Prespecified win is pooled MSE gain>0 AND at least 3/4 site-fold wins. Even then this would only be a **within-study candidate** because the efficacy assay was conducted near Waco in 2015 and neither paternal compatibility nor viable progeny were observed.

The main scientific distinction: calibrating pollinator-visit **quality using a separate manipulation** is more mechanistic than calling AH/BF visits inherently effective, but it does not resolve missing actual compatible pollen, pollen-donor matching or ecological convergence. Rigor requires preserving both success and failure without optimizing taxon weights post hoc.
