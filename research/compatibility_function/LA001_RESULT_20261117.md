# LA001 — individual visit exposure does not robustly improve held-out seed-production prediction

**Date:** 2026-11-17. **Decision:** `NO_ROBUST_SINGLE_PROGRAMME_INCREMENT`. Original public-source reanalysis of *Leavenworthia alabamica* field arrays; **not independent replication of the original study, not causal inference, not natural validation of the EGC/EGWE finite models**.

## Actual source grain and joining integrity

- The field sheet has **256** site×year×plant records: 4 sites × 2 years × 32 plants; exactly **16 SC and 16 SI** at each site-year.
- Site IDs: 1 Waco, 2 Quarry, 3 24-43, 4 Landersville. The year codes 1/2 map to 2014/2015 and the `First Flower` position→plant crosswalk yields **256/256 exact identifiers**.
- Independent year-matched S-locus genotype sheets link to **256/256 field plants**, but marker calls within those field plants are **A for every SC and D for every SI**: no independent genetic compatibility variation at fixed genotype. Do **not** fit a separate S-marker coefficient. **201 greenhouse records** produce only **one literal Year×Family×Individual match** to the field IDs and cannot be cross-joined as if they were the same mother/cohort.
- The original paper specified **30-minute pollinator observation periods**. The raw sequence-of-plant-positions encoding produces **121 source-completed periods** (2014:45; 2015:76), matching the published Methods. The additional four 2015 `Open Flowers` rows lack `TOTAL` completion and contain zero insect event rows; they are not counted as completed 30-minute observations.
- Source position sequences contain **6,701 valid plant position tokens (1–32)**, **38** visits to positions logged with zero open flowers in their particular period, and **one invalid position `34`** (which the original summary total included). For the visit-rate numerator, the 38 inconsistent tokens and invalid 34 are excluded by a **pre-outcome source-quality rule**; all flags remain recorded. Flower-hours are `0.5 × sum(open-flower counts over complete periods)` at each specific site-year/position. All 256 field plants have positive source flower-hour exposure.
- Lifetime seed count is present for **252/256 plants**; four missing source counts were excluded without interpreting as zero. The numerical original model compares those 252 plants across **four independent sites**, not 252 independent ecosystems.

## Frozen predictive comparison

Outcome: individual log(1 + **lifetime seed total**). Controls for year, binary SC, year×SC, log(1 + lifetime flower number) and days-to-flowering / 30; intercept unpenalized. Fold-internal standardized ridge `lambda=1`, leave **both years of each complete site out** in turn. Predictors:

- **M0 baseline:** phenology, flower count, SC status and year.
- **M1 quality-aware source visits:** M0 + log(1 + flower-position visits by `AH` (Andrenidae/Halictidae) or `BF` (Bombyliidae) / individual observed flower-hours). Those guilds were specified **using the original article's single-visit efficacy evidence**, not by selecting groups according to current seed outcomes.
- **M2 total source visits (secondary):** M0 + same normalized visits from all recorded taxa.

The frozen M1 success criterion required a positive **pooled held-out MSE gain** (M0 − M1) **and at least three positive site-fold gains**. No post hoc changes to guilds, flower covariates, outcome, penalty, site folds or classification are allowed.

## Original complete four-site holdout result

| Whole site left out | Eligible plants | M0 MSE | M1 MSE | M2 MSE | Primary gain M0−M1 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Waco | 62 | 0.87010 | 0.81793 | 0.87308 | +0.05217 |
| Quarry | 63 | 0.27731 | 0.26630 | 0.27846 | +0.01101 |
| 24-43 | 64 | 0.99514 | 1.20718 | 1.14888 | **−0.21204** |
| Landersville | 63 | 0.53848 | 0.50868 | 0.56096 | +0.02980 |
| **Pooled 252 plants** | **252** | **0.67075** | **0.70157** | **0.71644** | **−0.03081** |

All error units are squared `log1p(seed total)`. M1 improves **3/4** site folds but **worsens the pooled held-out error**, driven by 24-43. Thus **the prespecified primary criterion fails**. The secondary M2 count-based addition also underperforms M0 (pooled MSE 0.71644). M1 beating M2 does **not** rescue the primary failure or prove pollinator quality causally determines reproductive fate.

Original source-level four-site cross-validation contains **no new species-level replication** and is too small for credible general p values across independent ecosystems. Numerical prediction was independently checked with a NumPy ridge implementation for all 12 site×model fits (maximum absolute fitted-prediction deviation `<1e-8`).

## Biological interpretation ceiling

This is **not a rejection of pollination**, or an empirical claim that seed-set limitation is entirely driven by mating or entirely driven by resources. Observational visitation-rate/count has incomplete temporal information, does not measure individual compatible pollen or paternity, and is not independent of the lifetime floral display. Source S-marker category is fully confounded with SC vs SI. Seed **counts** are not confirmed viable recruits. Flower total is a **whole-season** covariate, so this is site-held-out *same-season prediction*, **not** an early-warning forecast measured before the outcome window.

For an eco-genetic process test that might be genuinely novel, the missing natural data are **compatible pollen deposition/paternity and effective next-generation recruitment measured in the same maternal site-year as visitation**, ideally in replicated systems with comparable cohorts. These cannot be invented from this archive.
