# LA002 — independent single-visit efficacy calibration does not repair site-transfer failure

**2026-11-17, final within-programme result:** `NO_ROBUST_ASSAY_CALIBRATED_SITE_TRANSFER_INCREMENT`. The claim is a **source-linked original-data reanalysis** of one published programme, not a newly discovered pollination mechanism, natural eco-genetic law, or replication across species.

Original Layman et al. (2017), *Evolution* 71:884–897, [DOI 10.1111/evo.13167](https://doi.org/10.1111/evo.13167), original Dryad [10.5061/dryad.2pj0j](https://doi.org/10.5061/dryad.2pj0j). The original LA001 negative site-out result and all its M0/M1/M2 model coefficients/test folds were not redefined.

The original 2015 single-visit emasculation assay examined solitary bees and bee-flies at one source site (Waco) and a smaller group of other flies. It was **not** executed as a new independent experiment here. Unlike LA001's equal effectiveness assignment to AH and BF, this analysis calibrates a new field visit index using **seed output per experimental visitation**, measured in that separately sampled assay, without seeing each field plant's lifetime seed outcome.

The primary prospective rule required **positive pooled M0−M3 MSE gain and at least three of four site-fold gains**. The gains were 3/4, but pooled **−0.030932**, hence **failed**. Secondary M1−M3 pooled difference was **−0.000119**; the calibrated weighting was practically the same as simply counting the two effective guilds across held-out sites, and cannot rescue the preregistered failure. Its slightly lower 24-43 MSE than M1 is descriptive and not an independent success.

## Scientific meaning and decisive data gap

This result makes the preceding failure more interpretable, but not causal: **an explicit, within-study independently measured per-visit seed calibration did not make site-to-site seed production transferable**. A single global taxon×seed yield factor cannot substitute for site/year-specific pollinator effectiveness, compatible pollen receipt, differences in female resource/phenology, genetically compatible mates or reproductive compensation. Which of these actually caused the transfer failure is **not identified**; this dataset has no same-mother mating/paternity or verified viable F1 recruitment. The source field S marker is completely confounded with SC/SI state.

Layman et al. (2017) already established reproductive assurance and seed discounting in this system. The current output is **a negative predictive transport and measurement-boundary analysis**, not a new natural ecological law. All earlier EGC/EGWE finite-closure effects, EGWEE natural pooled syntheses, LA001 negative primary, and journal submission artifacts remain unchanged.
