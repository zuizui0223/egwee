# Genetic-state versus ecological-function recovery — new public-source decision ledger
## Source screen (2026-11-15), appended without rewriting frozen 5-cluster EGWEE meta

**Source and outcome classification, NOT a pooled effect or an eligible-study claim.** All source programmes below are screening candidates. Publication records, genotype rows, plant/census records, treatments and repeated time points **must not be counted as independent meta-analytic biological systems**. The primary estimand remains direct intervention-associated recovery of a specified ecological function, distinct from genetic diversity improvement or general survival.

## Nonindependence and what is *not* novel

- `Dianthus` and `Arnica` each have primary botanical papers but share Belgian LIFE Herbages translocation infrastructure and some study participants; treat programme/management overlap as a possible higher-level dependency, not proof of independent ecological shocks.
- Four species in Bürli 2025 are **four responses within one planned experimental programme**, not automatically four independent meta trials; F0 flowering, fitness and survival were reported, but the experiment does not measure translocation-induced genomic diversity per individual.
- *Primula*, *Cirsium* and *Pleuropogon* can document demographic–genetic discordance, but may have no synchronized recipient-cohort **interaction-dependent F_direct**. They cannot be silently promoted into controlled genetic-rescue efficacy meta-analysis.
- The existing Clarke 2024 `ΔH_e→fitness` meta already handles 19 original studies / 27 comparisons, largely population size/survival. A replay of that regression is not the current novelty.
- Pseudoziziphus (PRs #54, #56): 1,120 real genotyped transplants in 12 populations and 1,185 long survival records; no exact individual ID crosswalk, only 3/12 literal population labels. Its original authors already tested sibship survival; repeating their model is not a new meta-analysis.

## Next pre-outcome decision contract

1. Validate the DOI, file digest, sheet-header/row schema, and usable cross-file identifiers in original Dianthus and Arnica archives **before opening effects**.
2. If F0 maternal IDs and F1 seed IDs match published genotypes, freeze individual/cohort and study-level effects and test molecular/cohort fitness under controls. If not, record `ID_LINKAGE_STOP`, not an ecological null.
3. Source-recover a **separate set of genuinely independent interventions with measured function** to avoid one-system mechanism cherry-picking. Reproduction/pollination, establishment, persistence, and neutral markers are **separate estimands**, not a single numerical meta effect size.
4. Only pool study programmes after coverage, biological unit, matching, dependency and effect-scale gates; otherwise publish evidence-map diagnostics rather than a spurious meta-analytic rescue coefficient.
