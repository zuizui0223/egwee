# Rescue coupling — genetic change versus realised ecological-function recovery
## Prospective data-screening protocol v0.1 — 2026-11-15

**Development programme only, not a completed meta-analysis and not a new active manuscript.** It is separate from the frozen EGWEE five-cluster/17-effect fragmentation state-separation synthesis and the finite-model *Theoretical Ecology* flagship. Do not pool those previous effects into this project unless their exposure/outcome designs independently pass the new eligibility rules.

### Scientific question and terminology

**Do genomic improvements after gene flow or translocation translate into improvements in interaction-dependent function, and does the answer depend on the initially limiting ecological or mating process?**

We distinguish four biological layers:

1. **Genetic-state improvement (G):** measured change in heterozygosity, inbreeding, harmful genomic burden, effective donor/mating diversity, or validated functional-allele availability; analysis must not assume all metrics are equivalent
2. **Population fitness (W):** individual survival, fecundity or population growth attributable to introduced genetic variation versus demographic supplementation; a causal **genetic rescue** claim needs this additional support
3. **Realised ecological function (F):** direct compatible pollen deposition, pollination efficiency, successful seed production and recruitment, effective dispersal, or partner-mediated ecosystem function, with a prespecified observation window
4. **Demographic state (D):** abundance, temporary additions of individuals or persistence not automatically assignable to genetic improvement

**Terminology firewall:** `ΔH_E>0` is **genetic-marker improvement**, *not by itself genetic rescue*. Increased offspring fitness is not automatically regained **interaction-dependent ecological function**. The term “functional rescue” has a **different established use** in genetic mixing/provenancing literature (functional species replacement); use *ecological-function recovery* here, not a claim to originate the term.

The four non-exclusive mechanisms of population rescue (demographic, genetic, evolutionary and ecological/process restoration) must be separately tracked. A single translocation can affect multiple pathways. Christie, Fitzpatrick & Searle (2026), *Trends in Ecology & Evolution*, https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S016953472600220X, emphasize rescue as a **population-level outcome**, not the intervention label.

### Existing research that prevents a redundant meta-analysis

- **Clarke, Smith & Cullingham (2024)**, *Molecular Ecology* 33:e17532, https://doi.org/10.1111/mec.17532, already meta-analyzed **19 pre/post translocation studies** with both genetic and fitness data. It found mean improvements but not universal success. Original analysis dataset and code exist at https://doi.org/10.5683/SP3/9VKJI6 and https://github.com/AdamCSmithCWS/Genetic_rescue_meta. We must **not** republish simply the `ΔH_E → Δfitness` association as if novel.
- **Frankham (2015)**, *Molecular Ecology*, https://doi.org/10.1111/mec.13139, surveyed 156 outcrossing cases, including plants; the benefit of outcrossing is already well documented under low outbreeding-depression risk.
- **Hufbauer et al. (2015)**, *PNAS*, https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1504732112; public experimental dataset https://doi.org/10.5061/dryad.p96b7, **244 flour-beetle experimental populations** distinguishing demographic and genetic contributions. It records treatment arms and populations, not necessarily neutral genomic marker and partner-dependent function on the same time points.
- **Genetic and Habitat Rescue Improve Population Viability in Self-Incompatible Plants (2024)**, *Evolutionary Applications*, https://doi.org/10.1111/eva.70037, explicitly compares genetic, demographic and habitat rescue **in a genetically and spatially explicit simulation**, including their combination. Its S-allele / mate-availability mechanism is unusually close to the concept in EGC–EGWE, but this synthetic study **is not an independent empirical field replication**.
- **Genetic mixing for population management: From genetic rescue to provenancing (2020)**, https://doi.org/10.1111/eva.13154, already assigns “functional rescue” to a type of replacement of functionally important species.

### Separate, nonpooled evidence streams

**Stream A — real genetic intervention and function (causal primary).** Experimental or quasi-experimental genetic mixing, assisted gene flow, pollen donor rescue, or restoration with a genetic-control/comparator and at least one direct function endpoint measured in recipient units. Accept explicit randomized factorial or matched-control BACI designs. Genetic causal claim cannot be assigned from pre-post markers alone. Track intervention amount, genotype provenance, ancestry, post-intervention generations, baseline habitat and control composition.

**Stream B — observational synchronized joint-state (conditional association only).** Co-measured genetic state and direct function in the **same population/site×year×cohort** without any documented rescue intervention. Eligible for **prediction/discordance**, not for a causal `genetic rescue` effect. Existing EGWEE fragmentation data can enter only with a separately frozen, nonoverlapping target and exact independent unit.

**Stream C — mechanistic simulations and theoretical counterexamples.** Citable mechanism/design scaffolds only. The 2024 SI plant model and the EGC–EGWE finite closure belong here. Do not count simulation seeds as independent field systems or pool simulated outcome scales with Stream A/B.

### Strict primary inclusion and outcome rules

- Independently identified biological recipient population, maternal family or experimental line; maintain study/programme as a grouping variable
- Genomic/marker or pedigree data AND direct functional outcome co-measured in a compatible time/cohort frame, OR a controlled genetic/habitat factorial with biologically justified functional offspring readout (explicitly mark molecular data absent)
- Direct comparison arms, timing, baseline genotype/ancestry, process-related moderators and denominator present or recoverable
- Reserve `F_direct` for ecological-function endpoints (pollinator effectiveness, compatible fertilization, viable seed set, actual recruitment, partner-mediated service). General `W_fitness` includes survival/growth; do not silently rename `W` as `F`.
- Preserve inbreeding, heterozygosity, allelic richness, homozygosity, fitness, function and demographic endpoints separately. No composite “rescue” indicator before predeclared interpretation
- One biological study/programme—not row, site nested in one programme, life-stage repeat, outcome type, or locus—is the independent meta-analytic cluster
- No outcome-driven dataset additions; archived raw source pinned by DOI plus source GitHub commit/digest

### Two primary estimands, contingent on data feasibility

**A1. Intervention × function:** `θ_F^G = [F_post - F_pre]_(genetically introduced) - [F_post - F_pre]_(control)` at a common horizon; otherwise a randomized follow-up difference may be appropriate. Separate `θ_G^G` (measured genomic-state change), `θ_W^G` (fitness) and `θ_D^G` (population growth). Use paired likelihoods or a multivariate model retaining sampling covariance; no independent endpoints count.

**A2. Ecological moderator / interaction:** With matched genetic and habitat treatment arms, `θ_F^(G×E)=F_GE-F_G-F_E+F_0` at the same biological unit/horizon. Only within coherent factorial contrasts; comparisons across unrelated experimental systems are not a valid causal interaction.

**B1 (separate observational estimand):** incremental held-out prediction for `F_future` from synchronized functional/mating/interaction state over and above neutral population structure, adult heterozygosity, census, habitat geometry and ancestry.

**Classification is secondary:** “genetic-marker improvement without direct functional recovery,” “both,” “function without marker gain,” “neither,” or “not identifiable.” Do not classify a near-zero or missing result as a true zero or equivalence; use uncertainty and a prespecified smallest meaningful change when estimable.

### Candidate ecological moderators

- mating system including self-incompatibility `S`-allele availability / compatible mate diversity
- pollination/interaction or pathogen/herbivore process actually limiting function
- genetic load/inbreeding and donor-recipient genetic divergence; outbreeding depression and ancestry replacement risks
- preexisting population size, density and demographic supplementation
- initial interaction quality (not merely pollinator species richness)
- time since translocation; F1/F2/F3+, juvenile versus adult cohort
- landscape habitat quality/connectivity and partner/seed/pollen movement operators

### Analysis gates (stop instead of overclaim)

**Gate 0 — public-data audit:** harvest original data *metadata/variable schema* without looking at effect signs. Confirm the 2024 Clarke data have independent studies, data-type labels and recoverable source lines; do not assume `fit_par` means interaction function. Check Dryad Hufbauer factorial table separately.

**Gate 1 — endpoint codebook (blind to effects):** classify each published fitness variable as `W_general`, `F_direct`, `D_abundance` or `not_identifiable` based on its original definition; record control type and cohort alignment. Lock classifications before directional outcome review.

**Gate 2 — event/contrast recoverability:** compute valid within-study effects, paired sampling variances and dependent structure. If fewer than a defensible number of independent Stream A studies have **both** G and F, report a quantitative **evidence-map/feasibility finding**, not a pooled primary meta-analysis. An empty eligible denominator is a result of measurement coverage, not evidence that rescue fails.

**Gate 3 — validation:** leave-study/programme-out, taxon/mating-system subgroup sensitivity only if sufficient independent clusters, publication/reference overlap audit, non-event denominator; no no-habitat-control design promoted to gene–habitat interaction.

### Source and reproducibility contract

The initial public Clarke screen must be **schema-only**, before publishing outcome-driven inclusion. The GitHub action downloads the pinned public repository file in runner memory, verifies ZIP/XLSX structure and produces only anonymized aggregate schema and category counts; it does not commit or redistribute third-party raw workbooks. Summary JSON files are a reproducibility receipt, not an effect-size meta-analysis.

The output of this programme can later become (i) a proper independent multilevel meta-analysis of gene-flow/functional recovery couplings if the same-unit data denominator supports it, or (ii) a systematic evidence map with a falsifiable target for new paired genotype×interaction field work.
