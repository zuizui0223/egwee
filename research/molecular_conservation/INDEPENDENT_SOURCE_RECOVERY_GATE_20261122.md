# Independent molecular–mating–function source recovery gate — 2026-11-22

Status: original-source candidate triage and acquisition specification only. **No new natural effect estimate, no independent field validation, no manuscript revision.** Builds on the existing 2026-11-15 plant primary screen and the LA001/LA002 correction.

## Decision
Stop re-fitting the existing *Leavenworthia alabamica* LA001/LA002 seed outcomes. Although the user-supplied original ZIP permitted 256/256 field S-marker joins and 252 lifetime seed outcomes, its field S-locus A/D classes are fully confounded with SC/SI, so within-class genetic mate-compatibility variation is absent. Original whole-site held-out additions of visits and independently calibrated efficacy failed to improve pooled prediction. This is not evidence that genetic compatibility is unimportant.

## Strict evidence separation
- **B observational joint state:** simultaneous pollen supply, compatible-mate diversity and seed set in the same natural population/time. SI001 already published the biological association; novelty requires a distinct held-out question or independent study.
- **A intervention:** pollen-donor provenance randomized or otherwise controlled, with direct fertilization/seed output; genetic-marker improvement, multigeneration persistence and ecological-function recovery are separate endpoints.
- **C theory:** exact EGWE finite-patch closure and 2024 S-allele simulation; never counted as empirical study.
- Do not equate diallel-estimated S-allele richness with a genome-sequenced S-locus haplotype; do not equate seed count with viable recruitment; do not treat pollen deposition as genetically compatible pollen.

## Required original-data intake contract (before outcomes)
1. DOI, original version/file names, archive SHA256 and access provenance.
2. Exact recipient population, donor population, maternal plant/family, year, ploidy, crossing arm and experimental block keys where present.
3. Source-native treatment assignment, response definition and response denominator, including non-events.
4. Whether genetic information is directly sequenced, pedigree-estimated, diallel-inferred, or a simple SC/SI class; flag non-identifiability if variation absent.
5. Overlap of SI001/SI002 populations; nesting and repeated donor-recipient pairs. Never add pairs to independent study count.
6. A source-only gate decision **before** reading response signs or fitting outcomes.

## Fixed test once genuinely independent source passes
- Mechanistic contrast: direct fertilization/seed output in recipient under locally sourced versus genetically compatible donor pollen, preserving within-recipient blocks and ploidy.
- Factorial G×E requires an actual independent ecological/pollinator/habitat manipulation. Different studies or simple pollen addition alone cannot identify G×E.
- Prospective prediction baseline: local pollen service + recipient density + mating system + ancestry; additional state: independently varying functional mate compatibility / donor-specific paternity. Hold out whole recipient populations; do not claim portability from 7 original populations alone.
- Predeclared failure: if functional compatibility does not add held-out prediction, or only repeats published within-study correlations, the theory-to-nature transfer is **not supported**; no parameter search to rescue a positive result.

## Conservation interpretation
The actionable distinction is whether local function is limited by *pollen delivery*, *compatible mates*, or *post-fertilization recruitment*. Match intervention accordingly; genome-wide heterozygosity alone cannot select a mechanism. None of the current candidates proves a universal warning threshold or that urban and island fragmentation converge.
