## Primary source rationale and prior art
- ECH01 Waananen et al. 2025, New Phytologist 247:968–978, DOI 10.1111/nph.70240, official DRUM handle https://hdl.handle.net/11299/271643. Same 2005 parentage cohort as Ison et al. 2014 (PMID 24388964: 37 maternal plants, 832/927 seedlings assigned, 11 microsatellite loci). Original study tracks offspring for 16 years, parents drawn from 13 source populations; 436 offspring included in a published phenology figure. Authors already show parental provenance distance interacts with ordered flowering asynchrony. No new discovery claim. Realized paternity does not represent all failed or incompatible mating opportunities; source-population distance is not genomic relatedness.
- ECH02 Waananen et al. 2024, American Naturalist 203:E188–E199, DOI 10.1086/730112, DRUM DOI 10.13020/b74k-7f48 / handle https://hdl.handle.net/11299/227155. Authors report 1,278 mating bouts over 7 years and 14 sites, with 8-year offspring followup; early emergence improved with mating potential but 8-year offspring counts did not. Repository metadata earlier refers to 1,279 plants; reconcile with final inclusion rather than equate them. Important prior art on erosion of short-term reproductive advantages. Publication explicitly withholds exact coordinates because of poaching. Never retrieve, export or reconstruct exact plant locations.
- ECH03 Beck, Waananen & Wagenius (2023), Figshare DOI 10.6084/m9.figshare.23994528.v1, Habitat fragmentation decouples fire-stimulated flowering from plant reproductive fitness. Official source listed by https://echinaceaproject.org/datasets/. Potential distinct ecological exposure comparator; independence from ECH01/ECH02 unknown. No claim before original source and study unit audit.
- Separate published pollinator effectiveness and pollen limitation studies exist; don't substitute visits for compatible pollen. ECH01 and 2014 are a shared cohort; do not count publication count as independent replication. ECH02/ECH03 overlap to be checked by actual study cohorts.

## Outcome-blind acquisition and admission
Use official public institutional DSpace REST item via handle resolver or publicly advertised bundle links, and official Figshare article metadata. No auth bypass, no execution of third-party scripts. Capture source file names, reported sizes, archive checksums where present; bounded download only of public data archives/tables; output headers and archive member names/row counts only. For protected sites never display raw geographic coordinates, plant IDs or values. Stop on unauthorized files, unknown source or missing key semantics.

Priority ECH02: verify maternal mating-bout ID, time/site unit, mating-opportunity measure and ascertainment of emergence + 8-year surviving descendants, nonemergent and lost-to-followup denominators. ECH01: check offspring ID, both parents, flowering start difference, parental provenance distance, offspring years and seed output or survival; verify which 2005 parentage subset remains. ECH03: check same head/plant/year mating opportunity and reproductive endpoint; no causal fire effect without design.

If original tables can be recovered, freeze a new source-defined endpoint and independent population/year or maternal holdout BEFORE fitting any effects. No artificial join of mothers, realized offspring, and mating opportunity across the papers. Do not conflate within-cohort offspring fitness with recruitment per originally available seed/ovule. Cross-layer G×I×F remains not identifiable unless functional compatibility, effective transfer and future endpoint are aligned. Source-only results are not a biological null.
