# ECH03A — preread joint spatiotemporal mating opportunity prediction (2026-11-26)

Source: Beck, Waananen & Wagenius 2023 PNAS DOI 10.1073/pnas.2306967120; public original Figshare 10.6084/m9.figshare.23994528.v1, burnRemsPopData.csv SHA256 28911e8c2a83bddbb75124dfcb0bf12add21c199b54bc3e308a41c47bf3fa675; metadata SHA256 135c13432a6d31b5249bc527ecc7f20a87ccab4e5b674a005650a4e6265b2a8e. Source-only runs 45769322577 and 45769477942 verified 210 unique site×year rows from 35 sites and six years, 152 nonmissing seed-set scenes, 182 OMP/synchrony/distance entries. The original article already reported the association of seed set with OMP, style persistence and fire×density; those are not new findings.

**Question:** does the recorded joint spatiotemporal OMP improve complete-site-held-out prediction of observed mean seed set beyond separate spatial and temporal summaries and their interaction, while allowing the original burn×census relationship? This is an ecological joint-state analogue, not a test of genetic compatibility or G×I×F. OMP combines distance to contemporaneously flowering neighbors; it is not observed realized paternity, sequenced S alleles or direct pollen transfer. Seed set (proportion of achenes containing embryos) is not established recruitment.

## Fixed outcome and comparison
- One scene = source-defined unique burnSite × year. Verify source hashes, 210 unique scenes, 35 sites, six observed years (2014–2016, 2020–2022). No exact locations or identifiers in outputs.
- Use only scenes with finite nonmissing census, propFl, meanSync, nn7.hmean, meanOMP, and meanSdSet; known burn and year codes; no missing-as-zero. Validate proportions in [0,1] where appropriate, census nonnegative integer, distance nonnegative. Require at least 100 complete scenes, at least 25 sites and at least two distinct outcomes. Count all missing reasons separately, preserve all eligible scenes (including published influential scenes); no outcome-conditioned exclusions.
- Target raw observed meanSdSet; equal scene weights. M0: intercept, burn indicator, training-standardized log1p(census), burn × standardized census, standardized propFl, standardized meanSync, standardized log1p(nn7.hmean), product of standardized meanSync and standardized distance, five year indicators (2014 reference). M1 adds standardized meanOMP. Training-only means and population SDs; constant features become zero. Ridge penalty lambda=1 on all nonintercept coefficients; no tuning. Clip predictions to [0,1] for both. Whole-site leave-one-out; all six years at a site withheld together; no site-specific intercept.
- Primary pooled held-site-out MSE gain = MSE(M0)-MSE(M1). Fixed operational gate: gain > 0 AND >=70% of represented sites have positive gain. Also report equal-site mean gain, per-fold anonymized scene counts and gains, 1000 deterministic cluster bootstrap of fixed out-of-fold errors (seed 20261126), no independent-system inferential p-value. If gain or gate fails, report no supported incremental transfer; do not change predictors, outcome, folds, distance measure or data weights. No signed biological coefficients needed.
- This comparison is not a test of a nonlinear universal law, nor a proof of covariance's causal role; OMP is a distinct, richer pre-existing metric and may improve prediction because of its nonlinear construction. A negative finding means this added summary did not meet the specified transport criterion, not that temporal/spatial mating opportunity is irrelevant. Only within-programme held-site-out evidence. No independent species replication, intervention causal inference, universal warning threshold or molecular validation.
- Preserve all earlier EGWE and EGWEE results. ECH01 and ECH02 are separate source audits, do not combine numerically with ECH03A. Freeze this protocol commit before any seed outcome association is calculated.
