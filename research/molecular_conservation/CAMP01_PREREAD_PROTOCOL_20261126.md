# CAMP01 — preread cross-site functional-autonomy × visitation test

Frozen 2026-11-26 before accessing seed outcome values. This is one independent experimental programme, not molecular S-locus or genetic rescue evidence.

Source: Makowski, Lamb & Galloway (2024), DOI 10.1002/ajb2.16351; CC BY 4.0 data DOI 10.5281/zenodo.10814705, Zenodo v1. Original source describes 53 experimental populations at 10 repeatedly used sites, four source populations (two high, two low autonomous selfing ability), single/small population manipulation, control and supplemented flowers, and two days of visitation observations. Their already-published three-way pollination treatment × autonomy × population size result (Wald chi-square 16.76, p<.001) is PRIOR ART. The original paper explicitly discusses that more per-flower visits in solitary plants need not represent effective pollination. Do not claim a novel discovery of Baker's law.

New question: can an autonomy-by-observed-visitation interaction improve leave-site-out prediction of the pollen supplementation gap, beyond the already-published autonomy-by-size structure? This is same-experiment transport, not causal variation in visitation, verified compatible pollen, genotype or future recruitment. Autonomy is a source-population phenotype, not sequenced genotype; only four source populations and ten sites.

## Exact source rules
- Official Zenodo record 10814705 only, two original CSVs PLdataindividual.csv (MD5 b84fa5c83513dbe75c0bf7840d1c74aa) and pollinator.csv (MD5 81e0deaa78a6a97e1211484cb9d0d3b3). No other records used. Check hashes; do not redistribute raw rows. Download via published links only; on 401/403 stop. Source catalog describes seed file under slightly different name, use listed file name.
- Source tables contain experimental.population, site, source.population, autonomy, size; seed table individual, treatment, seed number; pollinator table visits.per.flower. Parse case/whitespace/punctuation normalization only, require unique exact semantic matches. Stop on unknown categories; do not guess abbreviation mappings. Verify same experimental population ID and site, source population, autonomy and size across files. Site IDs may be repeated; enforce one observation row per experimental population. Only if source identifiers are consistent and same experimental population observed, join. Report denominators and dropped/missing counts, no individual labels exported.
- Seed response must be nonnegative integer recorded seed count, blanks/NA missing, zeros retained. Individual key = (site, experimental population, individual). Within each experimental population, take only plants with both natural control and supplemented recorded numeric seed outcomes; average within arm if repeated flowers, then average the per-plant log1p(mean supplemented seeds) minus log1p(mean control seeds) across matched plants. No pairwise hand vs natural pollen causal effect without original randomization details; call this recorded pollen-supplementation contrast. Each experimental population gets equal weight. Do not silently impute missing.
- Require at least 30 matched experimental populations, at least eight distinct sites with >=2 heldout groups each, both autonomy and size levels present in each training set and all four original source population levels present in each training set. Site-level leave-one-out splits, no data-selected fold changes.
- M0: intercept, one-hot source population (first sorted reference), binary autonomy high, binary small size, autonomy×size, log1p(visits per flower) standardized on training data. M1 adds exactly autonomy×standardized visitation. Ridge lambda=1, unpenalized intercept. Fit the models on training experimental populations only; compare pooled MSE and individual site gains. Because source population and autonomy may be structurally collinear, use shrinkage for prediction only, no independent ancestry/autonomy causal effect estimates.
- Criterion: positive pooled M0−M1 MSE, at least 70% held-out site folds with positive gain; otherwise no supported transport increment. No tuning, nonlinearities, additional outcomes, alternate visit metrics or dropping sites. Report actual effect direction only if existing predeclared model fitted; no causal sign, p-value, meta-analysis claim, or molecular genotype inference. Site holdout does not validate outside the experimental region or across source populations.
- Preserve LUPF1 STOP and LUPF2 exploratory result unchanged. Same-source author conclusions must remain author-attributed. If no join/support, retain source gate only.
