# CropPol guild-balanced Q→crop yield eligibility and model freeze

**Date:** 2026-11-18. This protocol is authored **after source-only 3448/54191 structural headers and missingness, but before inspecting any numerical associations between yield and any guild visit variable**. Targeted CropPol 2.1.0 author source commit and its two original Git blobs are locked in `SOURCE_PREOUTCOME_PROTOCOL_20261118.md`.

## Source-based visit and zero qualification

CropPol's original `visitation_rate` and `visit_*` columns are rates in source `visitation_rate_units`, NOT nested raw capture counts. The `taxa_recorded` field provides whether syrphid visitors were within survey scope. A numerical `visit_syrphids=0` is **eligible only** if `taxa_recorded` is source-labeled exactly `all visitors`, `bees and syrphids` or `bees, syrphids and wasps`. Records sampled as bees only, wild bees, bees/leps or bumblebees cannot contribute Syrphidae zero, even if a numeric guild field is populated. Strictly require `use_visits_or_abundance="use visits"` and `sampling_visitation` in `{"focal observations", "transects"}`. This is a conservative source-semantic subset; excluded sites remain missing/unknown, not ecological zero.

Primary same-field outcomes must be original `yield` with an explicit original `yield_units` category, not `yield2` selected for significance. Site fields must be exactly one row per `study_id × site_id × sampling_year × crop`, with finite, nonnegative numeric `yield`, `visitation_rate`, `visit_bombus`, `visit_wildbees` and `visit_syrphids` (plus honeybee to support total-guild audit). A legitimate zero yield is retained. Exclude source units containing `z-score`, odds or treatment/control **difference** as the given yield unit; the primary is a natural production value. Use exactly the source `visitation_rate` for total Q, and composition of `bee=visit_bombus+visit_wildbees` vs `syrphid=visit_syrphids`; no sum of passive abundance or invented survey effort scaling. These guild rates are already scaled to the source's visit unit and are comparable *only within exact source study-crop-year-unit panels*.

Panel definition: `study_id2` when nonempty else `study_id`, crop, calendar `sampling_year`, exact source normalized `yield_units`, exact original normalized `visitation_rate_units`. A panel is admitted if it has at least **10 independent field keys**, >=3 fields with positive recorded bee visitation and >=3 fields with positive Syrphidae visitation, and >=6 fields with nonzero *bee+Syrphidae* subtotal. Each eligible field has `0<=bee_share<=1` and `0<=syrphid_share<=1`; fields with bee+syrphid=0 do not identify that share and are excluded **pre-outcome**, not zero-filled. The user of these fields must not treat repeated site-year fields as independent original study programmes.

**Minimum global prediction gate:** >=10 independent `study_id2` source programmes, >=12 independent eligible panels, and >=200 source original field keys across panels. Failure gives `COVERAGE_STOP_NO_QF_MODEL`, not a discarded null or model repair.

## Predetermined CV question conditional on passing coverage

For each admissible panel (same crop+year+yield unit+visitation unit and original study programme), predict the observed per-field **log1p(yield)** from two train-only-standardized, single-stage ridge models with lambda=1 and unpenalized intercept:

- `M0`: `log1p(total source visitation_rate)`.
- `M1`: same + `syrphid share = syrphid / (syrphid + all recorded wild-bee/bumblebee visits)`.

5fold CV by deterministic SHA256 grouping of exact `study_id/site_id/year/crop` field key, stratification not allowed; if a panel's fold has fewer than 2 training fields or a zero-variance source predictor or yield, mark the panel `NOT_ESTIMABLE`, not repair. Transform log1p(yield) into a train-only standardized z score in each fold; score test MSE on that scale. Do not standardize on the heldout target. Use the same five test folds for M0 and M1. The difference `MSE0−MSE1` is positive for better guild-composition prediction.

Within each original programme, average eligible panels equally. Compare equally across programmes; preserve distribution, leave-one-programme-out, 2,000 **whole-source-programme** bootstrap CIs, seed 20261118. No p-value from per-field test rows. Positive evidence only if global programme bootstrap CI strictly >0 and >=70% independent programme means positive; any other result is unresolved or no incremental support. Also report total number of studies, crops, panels, units, and site-fold signs. A negative mean is retained without tuning penalty, share transformation, target, eligibility, fold or unit. A field's nesting crop/year or site remains explicit.

**Important test limit:** within-programme CV is not independent out-of-programme transport. No generality to wild ecosystems, because source crop yield responds to multiple agricultural conditions beyond pollination. This does **not** identify pollen quality, cross-guild niche competition, actual bee/hoverfly floral preference, viable recruitment, or a fragmentation-specific mechanism; user study and experiment decisions may confound Q and F. Already published CropPol papers reported abundance/richness-yield associations. The novel target (if any) is exact-source Syrphidae-versus-wild-bee *incremental predictive adequacy* under the conservative surveyed-guild filter. Either sign must be reported.

**Provenance/firewall:** The preexisting source-only gate already showed only general missingness, original source metadata and not a single signed yield result. This freeze precedes access to any yield×guild covariance. Do not redistribute raw rows, sites, source yield values or field-level predictions. This is not a replacement for EGWEE's historical fragmentation 5 clusters, PR #72/#74 guild-use data or theoretical `egwe`.
