# EGWEE PoMS dual-observation macroecology — frozen feasibility protocol (2026-11-17)

**Status: prospectively registered measurement-channel test; no signed insect responses or flower-context contrasts opened for this branch.** This is an original-observation database analysis in `zuizui0223/egwee`, not a literature effect-size meta-analysis and not theoretical `zuizui0223/egwe`.

## Question

**When a flowering community changes, do flower-visitation counts and passive pan-trap catches give the same bee-versus-hoverfly response?**

This directly audits a major alternative explanation to an earlier exploratory PoMS within-site flower-change / bee-versus-fly result (v0.9, `+0.241` with 95% interval `[+0.015,+0.466]`, 72 sites, 311 panels): trap attractivity may change with surrounding flowers. The old result is a *provisional comparator*, not a pre-validated estimate of true pollinator abundance or reproduction. Its previous local SQLite/ZIP is not part of this GitHub branch, so no numerical reproducibility or direct one-to-one model claim is made here.

## Publisher datasets (do not redistribute raw archive)

- UK PoMS (2026), **pan-trap survey, 2017–2023**, EIDC DOI `10.5285/5841bfae-471b-498c-af89-c30e5140d6c5`, https://catalogue.ceh.ac.uk/documents/5841bfae-471b-498c-af89-c30e5140d6c5 .
- UK PoMS (2026), **Flower-Insect Timed Count survey, 2017–2023**, EIDC DOI `10.5285/d706088c-9d48-4be5-b156-6ed6ad5e0618`, https://catalogue.ceh.ac.uk/documents/d706088c-9d48-4be5-b156-6ed6ad5e0618 .
- Both EIDC records declare Open Government Licence and required acknowledgement to UK PoMS, UKCEH and JNCC/funding partnership. Record source ZIP SHA256, file size, filenames, headers and row cardinality in a **schema-only** CI artifact; do not commit publisher raw CSVs.

The 1-km-square FIT survey is chosen because it is collected in the **same UK PoMS 1-km-square network** as pan traps. The self-selected *public* FIT dataset must be excluded from the paired design. PoMS pan traps observe ~6-h passive captures, while FIT counts observe 10-min insect landings on one target flower. These are *different observation mechanisms*, not identical independent measures of pollinator population size.

## Stage 0: pre-outcome source feasibility gate

Download the two exact current 2017–2023 source ZIPs from EIDC. Inspect **only ZIP bytes/checksums, CSV member names, row counts, header schemas**. Avoid numeric insect outcome, floral abundance or environmental-effect values before the crosswalk contract is frozen. If an EIDC URL returns HTML/401/403, log a source ACCESS_STOP rather than guessing CSV rows.

After Stage 0, document explicit source-native:
1. Exact 1-km-square ID and survey date keys on both methods (including duplicate semantics).
2. Which occurrence count fields define **bees** and **hoverflies** in each method, and whether complete sampling allows a true zero. Do not equate generic `other flies` with hoverflies.
3. Site×date observation effort, time, source design and pan station count; source-native flower taxon/station occurrence identifiers.
4. Any source-coded "public FIT" vs "1-km FIT" marker. Do not join on rounded coordinates, location names, or fuzzy dates.

A source field's absence closes the matched-pair test. **No source outcome signs may guide key choice.**

## Stage 1: primary estimand (only if source-native keys pass)

**Independent cluster:** 1-km monitoring square; repeats of dates, stations, individual traps and multiple FIT counts are nested. Match within exact `square × date`, not merely year or spatial proximity. If source-native survey-event key exists on both methods, require that too. If 1-km FIT counts cannot be isolated, STOP.

**Common floral-context exposure:** station-normalized occurrence incidence of distinct flowering plant taxa recorded within 2 m of *pan stations*, aggregated to square×date, rather than an unverified true number of flowers. This same measured landscape-context predictor is assigned to **both** methods in matched square×date blocks; FIT target-flower count is not substituted. The exposure proxy is only admissible if flower occurrence records can be uniquely assigned to station/date and sample effort. Avoid treating absent source records as ecological zeros where sample incompleteness is unknown.

**Outcomes:** In each matched square×date, count (A) pan-captured bees and hoverflies; (B) FIT-observed bee landings (bumblebee + solitary bee + honeybee) and hoverfly landings. Include all supported source bins exactly once. A FIT count's multiple insect-group cells are **not** independent experiments. Both-zero bee+hoverfly visits yield no relative-composition information and are excluded from the binomial-composition outcome (but retained in coverage ledger).

**Primary target:** method×flower-context interaction on the log odds of bee relative to hoverfly count, `delta = beta_FIT - beta_pan`, adjusting site and calendar month/year. Report separate within-method slopes and their difference. If method-dependent floral-context slopes diverge, the causal conclusion is only **measurement-channel non-equivalence conditional on observed context**, not proven bias magnitude, true abundance change or pollination success. Treat site-block bootstrap (>=1000 re-samples when feasible), per-site influence and leave-year-out as mandatory sensitivities. Investigate observed method-specific effort/visit target as nuisance factors without converting to equivalent catch rates.

**Gate before opening signed outcomes:** >=20 distinct same-key sites, >=80 square×date pairs, >=3 survey years, variance in floral incidence, and both methods with nonzero bee+hoverfly denominator. These are *coverage* thresholds, not significance gates. On failure publish coverage and `NOT_IDENTIFIABLE`, without moving the threshold or exposure.

Do not bin "statistically non-significant" as ecological equality. A result where the estimated delta is zero within uncertainty is inconclusive without an equivalence margin fixed before outcomes.

## Biological novelty and nulls

Flower-richness effects on coloured pan-trap catch are documented (Westerberg et al. 2021, doi:10.1002/ece3.7252; Krahner et al. 2024, doi:10.1002/ece3.11157), and a UK PoMS environmental trend analysis appeared in 2026 (doi:10.1002/2688-8319.70327). Therefore **neither** "floral context affects traps" **nor** "FIT differs from pan counts" is itself claimed as new. The candidate contribution is an **within-network, same-date, multisite test of whether bee–hoverfly response to a common source-measured floral context translates across observation methods**. Check whether the recent UK PoMS study has already tested this exact interaction before framing novelty for a paper.

Potential outcomes:
- `delta` robust and nonzero: method-specific relative bee–hoverfly signals differ; past pan-trap interpretations require calibration.
- `delta` unresolved: insufficient evidence for divergence; does NOT establish equivalent true communities.
- same direction across methods: candidate observation-mode robustness, not pollination/seed-function confirmation.
- matched units unavailable: useful source-measurement boundary; do not manufacture a cross-method effect.
