# AA001 alternative two-stage orchard P→fruit-F predictive gate — after Q-P-F coverage STOP, before any P-F coefficients

**2026-11-18, outcome-blind for this new comparison. Explicitly secondary, source-dependent, NOT a third same-flower Q-P-F programme.** The original pre-set Q+P+F orchard gate failed: only 11 same-orchard 2023 Q+P+open-F sites from 3 regions, below the pre-fixed 12 minimum. That negative gate is frozen and **will not be relaxed**.

This distinctly narrower analysis asks whether independently collected **2023 germinated pollen** measured from open-pollinated apple flowers adds **cross-orchard predictive information** for natural-open final fruit set beyond orchard temperature alone. Q/visits are **not** used because their original site+year coverage failed the independent three-stage gate.

Original source Riva et al. 2026, Zenodo 10.5281/zenodo.19403906, 4 original SHA256-listed files verified [run 44290503422](https://github.com/zuizui0223/egwee/actions/runs/44290503422). Biological sampling: sampled styles for P were **excised**; final fruit data come from **other flowers**. The common valid grain is `Region×Site×2023×Treatment O`, aggregated over plots. It is an ecological *orchard-context association*, not a same-flower causal P→F conversion or paternity/compatible pollen indicator. Germinated pollen is not confirmed compatible pollen, and wind-mediated effects are unmeasured.

## Predeclared eligibility, no effect-sign inspection

- Only treatment `O` = natural open; **no** comparison with excluded E/OH selected after inspecting effect signs. P 2023 from original paper Methods, F Year exactly 2023.
- Source `Region×Site` must be present in both original `GerminatedPollen.txt` and `initial_final_fruitset.txt`, with `Plot` and source treatment, and **at least one exact same-orchard+plot O match**. Do not join an individual style P to a final fruit F by an apparent `Inflo_ID` from a different experiment.
- All admitted P `Nbr_germinated_pollen` must be finite, nonnegative, F `Final_nb_fruit, Nb_Failure_Final` must be finite nonnegative and sum>0. No replacing NA with zero. Keep distinct plotted styles and F rows nested within orchard and aggregate **once to one orchard**. P = arithmetic mean of original style P values in eligible plot O cells; F = sum final fruit successes / sum original final success + failure counts in those cells.
- Source `Temp` must be finite and available as original covariate in **both** P and F measurements, with no >1.0°C difference across two source orchard streams and no >1.0°C within orchard from original rows (large violations STOP). Baseline orchard temperature = source F unweighted orchard average of its rows; unit °C. Do not append posthoc vegetation/crop management covariates.
- Require at least **12 unique original orchards** with complete P/F/O common plot support, **>=3 original Region groups**, and each region supplying **at least 2** included orchards; otherwise `PF_CONTEXT_COVERAGE_STOP` with no prediction. This is not the original QPF gate (still failed). Dataset is one original paper with clustered regions.
- Correctly distinguish `Site` identifiers that repeat across regions. Each orchard is one prediction observation, not individual styles/fruits.
- Baseline M0: train-only standardized `Temp`; enriched M1: same + train-only standardized `log1p(mean germinated P)`. Response: source fruit final proportion converted to the smoothed log-odds `log((Fsuccess+0.5)/(Ffailure+0.5))`, standardized on training orchards only. Ridge λ=1 with unpenalized intercept; no feature selection or tuning.
- **Primary leave-one-whole-region-out**. Each region held out with all its orchards; if model feature variance is zero, return `NONESTIMABLE` rather than removing the region or changing λ. Score one test MSE per orchard on train-standardized target, pooled orchard-equal across original regions; also report region-equal MSE gain, and fold signs. Δ=MSE(M0)−MSE(M1), positive if measured P improves forecast.
- With only a few original regions, show all region-fold scores and leave-one-region result; do **not** compute pseudo-precision or a species-level meta-analysis p-value by treating flowers, inflorescences or fruits as independent. Positive support is **only** a descriptive within-one-study status if pooled Δ>0 and at least 3 source-region folds improve. Negative or unresolved retained, no alternate outcome or eligibility rescue. No cross-study generality from one apple programme.
- All individual source orchard values, region labels, pollen counts and fruit outcomes remain private to original author Zenodo. Output only source counts, fold/aggregate diagnostics. The original parent frozen natural QPF two-programme count and old negative heldout results are unchanged.

This is an *observational predictive transport exercise*, not independent experimental identification of flowering physiology. The paper already demonstrates pollination treatment and temperature effects on pollen germination and fruit set, so these effects themselves cannot be claimed as newly discovered. The distinct prospective contribution, if any, is whether an *additional measured stage* transports useful information across held-out climate regions within the source.
