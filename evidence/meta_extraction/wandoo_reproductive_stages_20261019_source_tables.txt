## Source notes

Table 1 isolation: inverse of percentage remnant vegetation within a 3 km radius. Shape: Patton diversity index, greater means more edge-dominated. Remnant-type note [c]: large reserve. EC is electrical conductivity in water, used as a salinity proxy. Soil chemistry was measured in bulked samples from eight nested quadrats; these are not independent populations.

Table 6 values in parentheses are source SE; N denotes maternal plants sampled. Year labels y1, y2, y3 are source labels; exact calendar years are not inferred. Pollen tubes were collected in y2. Seven populations had no reproduction measurements owing to logistical constraints. Population L produced no fruits or seeds; its seeds-per-fruit entries are undefined, not observed zeros. The source has one reproductive N column rather than separate effective N columns for each year.
