# Quercus ilex 2018–2019 experimental pollen-addition data provenance

Original study: Bogdziewicz et al. (2020), *Ecology Letters*, https://doi.org/10.1111/ele.13609. Original dataset: https://doi.org/10.5061/dryad.sn02v6x2b (Dryad API: CC0 1.0). Public archived copy: https://zenodo.org/records/5306693/files/pollen_addition_results.csv?download=1.

The Zenodo metadata lists **original file MD5 `9bb924a2d0739e78e6823f1df6878e6a`**. The source material was recovered using a public webpage CSV text extractor. The **stored text copy** in this repository has MD5 **`ec3ccf2901ab6f6de0bca9b90eafec86`**, despite the same 31,609-byte text size. **It is NOT certified byte-identical to the Zenodo file.** The difference may reflect extractor line-ending or textual transformations and must not be silently hidden. Numeric source recovery is separately checked by count, schema and biological identities, but full publisher-MD5 validation remains pending until original binary access succeeds.

Text-copy data: 960 semicolon-delimited shoot records, 80 distinct tree IDs recorded in 2018 and 2019 (paper reported 81 originally marked). Three shoots per tree × year × each of two *different branches within the same tree*: `Control` versus `Pollen-addition`. No absent shoot, empty final entry or `NA` is imputed as zero. The paper says haphazard branch assignment, not a randomized tree-level pollen treatment.

`flowers`, `acorns_june`, and `acorns_final` represent female flower exposure, acorns before the major summer drought window and acorns in September after it. The raw data provide no species-independent drought covariate, tree synchrony series or regional microclimate. The original paper already established flowering synchrony effects on pollen limitation; source-level year response analysis here is a sensitivity, not a new biological law.

This is an exploratory unfragmented **one-programme** experiment distinct from theoretical egwe, and does not alter empirical egwee's frozen fragmentation denominators, publication claims or `submission_ready=false`.
