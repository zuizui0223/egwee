# CF01 target-pair full-text gate — Hulting and Sevenello — 2026-09-24

## CFTQ0002 — Hulting et al. 2026

The 2026 Ecology paper is **not a new independent EGWEE programme**.

Its experiment is the Savannah River Site fragmentation system already registered as PS014 / ML007. The 2026 study uses seven experimental blocks from the same long-term fragmentation architecture. Each block contains connected and unconnected patch types and the planted Carphephorus system. The Dryad dataset exposes block, patch, plant, repeated arthropod surveys and seed-head records.

Therefore:

- programme identity remains PS014 / ML007;
- programme increment = **0**;
- raw access is now legitimately available for the linked 2026 Carphephorus campaign;
- patch/plant rows are not independent fragmentation replicates;
- the 2026 connectivity/edge-ratio exposure does **not** silently replace the previously frozen ML007-2025 edge-severity exposure after outcomes are known;
- quantitative status = **linked_programme_raw_reanalysis_required_no_new_K**.

This linked campaign is useful because it removes the old blanket claim that the Hulting programme has no accessible raw data at all. It does not by itself make the original 2025 locked effect estimable.

## CFTQ0001 — Sevenello et al. 2026

Sevenello is a distinct programme candidate.

The source reports nine reserves with 11 independently sampled remnant areas and 21 transects. Edge transects are <50 m from the agricultural boundary and core transects are >150 m from the edge. Four focal annual plant species were sampled, but species occurrence means the exact transect set differs among species.

The study contains:

- I: bee abundance/pollination-service information at the transect scale;
- F: species-specific total seed production and pollen-limitation experiments;
- fragmentation components: edge/core and remnant area;
- non-fragmentation context: adjacent crop type.

The primary binary fragmentation component for any future direct representation must be frozen before numerical extraction; edge/core is source-defined, whereas crop type is not fragmentation. Remnant size remains a continuous secondary fragmentation component.

Because responses are hierarchical and source analyses use mixed/SEM models, Sevenello is **model_contrast_pending_standardisation / raw_reanalysis_required**, not immediately Hedges-g admissible. Plant/quadrat counts cannot be paired with remnant-level fragmentation n.
