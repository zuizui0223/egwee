# CFTQ0121 Plectritis site-level recovery gate — 2026-09-24

## Frozen target

Programme: `P2_CF01_PLECTRITIS_ISLAND_CONNECTIVITY_2015`.

The frozen recovery contract requires a common site-level frame containing:

- source-defined habitat connectivity within 1 km of each focal patch;
- total *Plectritis congesta* floral visitation rate;
- direct seed production / maternal female fitness.

Independent unit = site/population. The source has 12 named localities.

## Public-source audit

The publisher page identifies an online appendix and the public article/appendix material exposes:

- the 12 site identities;
- the 1-km connectivity design;
- visitor sampling dates/effort and visitor composition;
- model-selection/model-summary material for visitation, mating and seed production.

The accessible material does **not** expose one reproducible site-level vector containing all three
frozen variables required for EGWEE: connectivity, total visitation and seed production.

No numerical EGWEE effect was calculated.

## Decision

Status: **design-valid, quantitatively blocked by site-level effect-unit recoverability**.

This is not an ecological null. It does not show that connectivity fails to affect interaction or
reproduction. It means the public reporting surface available to this recovery does not permit the
registered site-level Fisher-z pair and its covariance to be reconstructed without post hoc data
creation.

## No rescue

Do not:

- digitize figures to invent the 12-site vectors;
- back-calculate site values from AIC/model coefficients;
- substitute visitor diversity, phylogenetic diversity or functional-group composition for total visitation;
- dichotomize Vancouver versus Gulf Islands;
- use individual visitors, plants, flowers or seeds as fragmentation n.

Reopen only if an authoritative public source or author-provided dataset supplies the frozen
site-level variables.
