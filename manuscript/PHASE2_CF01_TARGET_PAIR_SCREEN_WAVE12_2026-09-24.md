## Full-text decision

### CFTQ0119 — Acer mono

The paper directly compares continuous and fragmented forest contexts and provides contemporary
pollen-dispersal / mating-connectivity information from microsatellite parentage and mating-system
analyses.

It does **not** provide a current-study fragmentation-level reproductive-function F endpoint.
The authors explicitly distinguish the 2015 analysis from earlier work on seed reproduction and
focus this paper on seed quality / mating-system genetics. Modelled pollen-donor siring success is
part of the C/mating-process model and is not recoded as F.

Decision: retain as C/G_mating fragmentation evidence and a mechanistic example of long-distance
pollen compensation; close the direct C-F pair under the present common-effect-unit contract.

A prior seed-reproduction paper may be considered only through a separately frozen linked-publication
contract before its outcome values are opened. It is not joined post hoc here.
