# ML018 / PS023 Spondias mombin recovery closure

## Decision

ML018 is **not admitted** to the primary multilayer Hedges-g stream from the currently recoverable public evidence.

Status: `population_level_F_vector_or_C_F_pairing_not_reconstructable`.

## Passed gates

- Independent species/system relative to the four canonical primary clusters.
- Source-defined fragmented-versus-reference landscape comparison.
- Population / forest fragment is a defensible fragmentation unit.
- Direct movement/connectivity information is available at the correct unit: Table 3 reports population-level apparent and total pollen-flow estimates (with SE) for FDP, LC, DL and four small island populations.
- The source demonstrates the biological state-separation pattern qualitatively: reproductive function is reduced in small island fragments while pollen immigration is high.

## Blocking gate

The prelocked C endpoint is recoverable as a population vector, but the prelocked F hierarchy is not recoverable on the same population frame.

The reproductive-success analyses expose fruit production primarily as tree-level observations/treatment curves for FDP, LC, DL and the **small-island treatment pooled across islands**. The germination figure likewise reports treatment-level FDP/LC/large-island/small-island summaries. The public article therefore does not expose one fruit-production or germination value for each of the individual small island populations represented in the pollen-flow table.

Consequently the primary requirements cannot be met:

1. small-fragment F replication cannot be represented by the same independent population units used for C;
2. exact C and F vectors cannot be aligned by population ID;
3. the predeclared paired-population covariance cannot be reconstructed; and
4. tree-, fruit-, seed- or progeny-level observations cannot be promoted to fragmentation replicates to manufacture a Hedges-g variance.

The strong direction of the published result is not an exception to the effect-unit contract. In particular, the fact that small fragments have lower reproductive output but higher pollen immigration is scientifically interesting, but it cannot be entered into the canonical primary Fisher synthesis without a valid same-frame population-level F representation.

## Reopening condition

Reopen ML018 only if source/raw data provide island-specific fruit-production or germination summaries for the same small-island population IDs used in the pollen-flow analysis, with enough information to construct population-level Hedges-g effects and C–F dependence.

No hypothetical ML018 p-value is inserted into the synthesis.
