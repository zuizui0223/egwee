# Fresh ecological validation amendment — effect-scale-aware propagation test

**Frozen:** 2026-09-29

## Status

This amendment is written after the current-corpus Hedges-g versus lnRR audit but **before any fresh validation outcome has been opened**.

It does not change the burned discovery set. It changes how future Q→E→F response amplitudes are represented so that the new validation programme does not repeat the current manuscript's endpoint-specific SD-standardization ambiguity.

## Why the amendment is necessary

The current direct corpus shows that Hedges g and lnRR can give different endpoint rankings, different within-system magnitude-separation decisions and different leave-one-system robustness classifications.

Therefore a fresh test of propagation, buffering or bottleneck position must declare its response-amplitude estimand before outcomes are inspected.

## Fresh direct-programme effect scale

For positive-valued Q, E and F endpoints measured under a fragmented-versus-reference design:

1. **primary amplitude estimand = oriented lnRR**, `orientation × log(mean_fragmented / mean_reference)`;
2. **mandatory sensitivity = Hedges g** using the same independent units;
3. paired Q/E/F covariance for lnRR is reconstructed from aligned independent units by a prespecified multivariate-delta rule or by a joint hierarchical model fixed before outcomes;
4. Hedges-g covariance is estimated under the separately frozen dependence rule;
5. no effect scale may be selected after observing which gives the preferred biological ordering.

lnRR is chosen prospectively here because the fresh Q/E/F endpoints are intended to be positive biological quantities and the hypothesis concerns proportional propagation across stages. This does not retroactively make lnRR the primary estimand of the current manuscript.

## Fresh gradient programmes

Continuous-fragmentation programmes remain in a separate gradient effect family. Their primary effect representation is the prespecified model slope / Fisher-z correlation family appropriate to the frozen design. They are not numerically pooled with direct lnRR programmes.

## H2-v3 — scale-aware bottleneck localization

The Q→E→F chain remains:

`Q = interaction quantity`

`E = effective mating / compatible pollen / realised mating quality`

`F = reproductive function`.

Primary direct contrasts on lnRR:

`Delta_QE = lnRR_Q - lnRR_E`

`Delta_EF = lnRR_E - lnRR_F`

`Delta_QF = lnRR_Q - lnRR_F`.

The previously frozen directional hypothesis `Delta_QE > 0` remains a prospective test of whether effective mating deteriorates proportionally more than raw interaction quantity.

However, a system-specific bottleneck label is promoted beyond exploratory status only if:

- the primary lnRR comparison resolves under the frozen dependence/multiplicity rule;
- the Hedges-g sensitivity does not show a contradictory directional ordering;
- the result is not driven by a single programme in leave-one-programme-out analysis once the cross-programme gate is open.

Statistical significance need not match exactly across scales; contradictory biological ordering must be disclosed and blocks a scale-general bottleneck claim.

## Scale-independent outcomes

Opposite-sign process responses on the same registered representation remain qualitatively interpretable without magnitude ranking. For example, one stage improving while a downstream stage declines is a stronger form of decoupling than a difference in absolute standardized magnitudes.

## No-rescue additions

Do not:

- choose Hedges g instead of lnRR because it gives stronger separation;
- choose lnRR instead of Hedges g because it gives stronger separation;
- change endpoint transformation after viewing Q/E/F ordering;
- label a programme upstream- or downstream-dominant when the two representations give contradictory ordering without explicitly marking the classification scale-specific;
- use current-corpus scale-sensitive systems as fresh confirmation.

## Relation to the current manuscript

The current Journal of Ecology manuscript remains a discovery/synthesis paper. Its lnRR analysis is post hoc sensitivity. This amendment applies only to genuinely fresh validation programmes and prospectively prevents the same scale ambiguity from being hidden in the next study.
