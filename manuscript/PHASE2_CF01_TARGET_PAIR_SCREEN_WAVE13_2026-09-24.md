## Advances

### CFTQ0121 — Plectritis congesta island/meadow connectivity

The source uses 12 focal populations in endangered Garry-oak / maritime-meadow habitat and measures
habitat connectivity within 1 km of each population. Visitor observations and seed production are
measured on the same site programme.

This is retained as a **gradient/generalisation I-F candidate**. The recovery contract freezes:

- independent unit = population/site;
- exposure = source 1-km habitat connectivity, oriented as greater isolation by sign reversal;
- I = total Plectritis floral visitation rate;
- F = direct seed production / maternal female fitness;
- visitor diversity, phylogenetic diversity and visitor functional-group composition = secondary;
- no Vancouver-versus-Gulf-island dichotomy.

No effect has been calculated.

### CFTQ0124 — Cabralea canjerana direct fragmentation I-F

The source samples three fragmented and three continuous Atlantic-forest sites for reproductive
output and directly records pollinator visitation in two fragmented plus two continuous sites.

This is a **direct I-F recovery candidate**, but the paired common frame is prospectively limited to
the four sites with both layers:

- fragmented = F1 + F2;
- reference = C1 + C2;
- I = pollinator visits per 30 min, aggregated to site;
- F = developed fruit number per tree, aggregated to the same sites;
- trees, observation periods, fruits and seeds never become fragmentation n.

The broader 3+3 reproductive sample is not combined with the 2+2 visitation sample as if they shared
one denominator. The tiny four-site covariance gate must pass for admission.

No site-level effect has been calculated.
