## CFTQ0229 — Diekötter red-clover factorial experiment

This is the only wave-23 candidate retained for the quantitative pathway.

The source is a genuine field experiment manipulating:

- habitat area;
- fragmentation / configuration;
- matrix composition;

while measuring both flower-visiting insects and red-clover seed production.

The study therefore has a strong biological I-F chain, but the published response is explicitly
factorial. The abstract itself highlights especially high visitation in small clover patches
surrounded by bare ground compared with larger patches embedded in grass.

EGWEE freezes an estimand gate before numerical extraction:

1. recover the independent experimental habitat units and replicate counts;
2. distinguish source habitat-area, fragmentation/configuration and matrix factors;
3. choose one response-independent fragmentation estimand shared by I and F;
4. retain area/matrix as prespecified blocking/moderator factors where required;
5. do not define the primary fragmented cell from the strongest published response.

Status:
`pending_factorial_fragmentation_estimand_and_independent_unit_recovery`.

Direct I-F increment remains **0** until the factorial effect-unit contract is satisfied.
