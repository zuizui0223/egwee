1. Ottewell et al. (2009), *Biological Conservation* 142:888–898,

The thesis itself is an umbrella source and cannot increment programme K.

## 2009 Biological Conservation estimand

The 2009 paper tests **individual-tree isolation**, expressed as distance to conspecific neighbours,
as a predictor of reproductive success in *Eucalyptus camaldulensis* and *E. leucoxylon*.

Direct F evidence includes:

- seed production;
- germination.

The paper also estimates individual outcrossing rates and discusses increased pollination distances
in agricultural landscapes.

Under the frozen EGWEE layer contract, outcrossing rate is not automatically direct
`C_movement_connectivity`; it is a progeny/mating-state quantity unless a direct donor-movement /
paternity-distance endpoint is recovered on the same independent-tree frame.

The public article surface does not expose a common tree-level direct C marginal and sampling
variance that can be paired with seed production under one isolation-gradient effect representation.

## 2010 Restoration Ecology estimand

The 2010 paper uses a different source contrast:

- paddock trees;
- trees in intact vegetation fragments.

It reports species-specific direct reproductive output:

- *E. leucoxylon* paddock trees have lower reproductive output than fragment trees;
- *E. camaldulensis* paddock trees have higher reproductive output.

It also compares adult genetic diversity and demographic structure.

The publication does **not** provide a direct pollen/seed movement C endpoint on that same
paddock-versus-fragment contrast.

## Why the thesis cannot manufacture C-F

The umbrella thesis contains related reproductive, pollination and mating chapters, but the mapped
primary publications do not provide one synchronized:

`species × exposure × campaign × independent-tree frame`

containing both direct C and F marginals.

In particular:

- the 2009 isolation gradient is not the same estimand as the 2010 paddock-vs-fragment contrast;
- offspring outcrossing rate is not silently promoted to direct C;
- reproductive output from 2010 is not attached to a 2009 mating/movement estimate;
- two species are dependent biological panels, not two independent programmes.

## Terminal status

`linked_primary_publications_no_common_same_exposure_CF_frame`

Direct C-F programme increment: **0**.

The programme remains valuable evidence that isolated paddock trees can retain high outcrossing and
substantial reproductive capacity despite extreme agricultural clearing, while species differ in
the reproductive effect of the paddock-tree context.

## Reopening condition

A direct or gradient multilayer recovery may reopen only within one primary publication/species if an
authoritative dataset exposes:

1. the source isolation/fragmentation exposure;
2. a direct C endpoint such as donor movement, pollen-dispersal distance or mating connectivity;
3. direct F on the same maternal-tree frame;
4. compatible marginal variances at the independent-tree level.

## No rescue

Do not:

- count the thesis as a new programme in addition to its articles;
- treat outcrossing rate as direct C solely because it is genetically estimated;
- combine the 2009 individual-isolation exposure with the 2010 paddock-vs-fragment exposure;
- combine *E. camaldulensis* and *E. leucoxylon* as independent programme K;
- use progeny counts as independent landscape/tree replication;
- convert narrative pollination-distance conclusions into a standardized C effect;
- use the programme to validate a finite EGWE/NEE operator.
