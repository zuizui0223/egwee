## Citation boundary

These references position the manuscript relative to established indicator validation, indicator-suite selection, uncertainty, proxy evaluation and predictor standardisation, and identify the original natural-system data sources. They do not imply that the present four-gate sequence, or any locked system-level result, was previously demonstrated by those studies. All project-computed claims remain governed by the migrated preregistration/result records under `evidence/` and the machine-readable gate registry.
