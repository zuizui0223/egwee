## Citation boundary

These references position the manuscript relative to established indicator validation, indicator-suite selection, uncertainty, proxy evaluation and predictor standardisation. They do not imply that the present four-gate sequence, or any locked system-level result, was previously demonstrated by those studies. System-specific source citations remain attached to their migrated preregistration/result records under `evidence/` and must be included in the final journal-formatted bibliography when those datasets are cited by name in the submitted manuscript.
