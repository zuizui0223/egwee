## Why all eight close for the direct pair

- **IFQ025 Senna didymobotrya:** population-size/Allee study; no habitat-fragmentation treatment.
- **IFQ026 Echinacea angustifolia 2010:** individual isolation and population-size gradients in fragmented prairie.
- **IFQ027 Echinacea angustifolia 2006:** multi-scale isolation/density/population-size gradients.
- **IFQ028 Heliconia tortuosa 2022:** 40 focal-patch landscapes modeled continuously by forest amount and patch size.
- **IFQ029 Verticordia staminosa 2004:** subpopulations nested within one isolated granite outcrop/remnant.
- **IFQ030 Calothamnus quadrifidus 2007:** multiple population fragments varying continuously in size/isolation, without an unfragmented reference.
- **IFQ031 Rutidosis leptorrhynchoides 2010:** seven populations spanning population size; the causal focus is S-allele/mate availability and population size, not a source-defined fragmentation treatment.
- **IFQ032 Solanum rostratum 2015:** six population-size/density populations, while pollinator observations cover only two; no common I-F fragmentation frame.

These records remain in gradient, mechanistic or moderator evidence lanes where appropriate. Direct-pair closure is not study exclusion.
