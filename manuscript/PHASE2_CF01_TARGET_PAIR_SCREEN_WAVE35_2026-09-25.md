## Advances

### CFTQ0344 — Cramer / Duckeodendron

The BDFFP dissertation directly measures both fruit fall and seed dispersal on the same
*Duckeodendron cestroides* adults across three fruit crops.

The source dispersal chapter uses six fragment trees and five continuous-forest trees after finding
no detectable 10-ha versus 100-ha dispersal difference. Fruit production is measured by fruit fall
around those same adult trees.

Status:
`pending_Duckeodendron_11tree_common_CF_values_model_variance_and_forest_nesting_recovery`.

The candidate is real, but repeated years/seeds/distances cannot become new fragmentation units and
tree-level observations must retain their forest-reserve nesting.

### CFTQ0345 — Ottewell paddock-tree thesis

The thesis is an umbrella source for later peer-reviewed paddock-tree studies. Linked primary
publications include the 2009 *Biological Conservation* reproductive-success paper and the 2010
*Restoration Ecology* demographic/reproductive/genetic paper.

Decision:
`linked_thesis_to_primary_publications_pending_common_effect_unit_mapping_no_new_K`.

The thesis itself does not count as a new programme.

### CFTQ0349 — Western Kenya

Bergsdorf's dissertation directly tests fragmentation effects on visitation, primary pollination
success, fruit set and seed set for four plant species in Kakamega main forest and five surrounding
fragments.

Status:
`pending_species_campaign_site_replication_and_common_IF_frames`.

Species and campaigns remain dependent panels until chapter-level site identities establish whether
the main-forest reference and fragments provide valid independent effect units.

### CFTQ0350 — rainforest-isolated crops

Pritchard's thesis links crop isolation from rainforest to pollinator-community depletion, reduced
fruit productivity/yield and pollen limitation.

Status:
`pending_crop_site_isolation_IF_vectors_and_multicrop_identity_recovery`.

The candidate belongs to the gradient/generalisation stream unless a source-defined binary
fragmentation contrast is recovered. Crop and pollinator-guild panels do not create independent K.
