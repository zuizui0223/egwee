## Advances

### CFTQ0052 — calcareous grassland / Phacelia spillover

Eight independent calcareous-grassland fragments span 0.05–9.41 ha. Sentinel Phacelia plots are placed 0–400 m from each fragment edge, and both pollinator visitation and seed production are measured in the same fragment-by-distance experiment.

The design is eligible for the gradient/generalisation family, but full-text audit shows site and sampling round enter hierarchical models while the public appendix provides site metadata/model summaries rather than the raw common I/F plot table needed for cluster-aware reanalysis. The quantitative gate is therefore blocked under the current public route; figures are not digitized post hoc.

### CFTQ0060 — Hedysarum scoparium fragmented/restored experiment

The source directly measures pollinator visitation and seed set in three fragmented plots and three restored plots. Full methods clarify, however, that these plots are nested inside only **two habitat areas**: one fragmented habitat and one corresponding restored habitat separated by about 2 km.

The habitat treatment therefore has one independent habitat per condition. The three plots cannot be promoted to three independent fragmented and three independent restored landscapes. The direct I-F fragmentation programme is closed on treatment-level pseudoreplication. Public data are also author-on-request rather than a recoverable plot-level archive.
