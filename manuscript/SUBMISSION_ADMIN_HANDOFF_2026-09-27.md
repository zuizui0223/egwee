- whether any equal-contribution or senior-author note is required;

### 2. Affiliations

For every author provide the final institutional affiliation(s) used for submission.

Do not copy identity-bearing affiliation text into `MULTILAYER_FRAGMENTATION_META_ANALYSIS.md`, which remains the anonymous reviewer-facing manuscript.

### 3. Corresponding author

Provide the final:

- name;
- postal address;
- e-mail address;
- any journal-required phone/contact field.

These belong on the title page / submission portal / non-anonymous cover letter only.

### 4. Author contributions

Agree the final CRediT-style contribution statement. Suggested roles to allocate as applicable include:

- Conceptualization;
- Data curation;
- Formal analysis;
- Investigation;
- Methodology;
- Software;
- Validation;
- Visualization;
- Writing – original draft;
- Writing – review & editing;
- Supervision / Funding acquisition where applicable.

Do not infer contributions from commit history; obtain author approval.

### 5. Funding, acknowledgements and permits

Confirm:

- funding agency names and grant numbers;
- institutional or infrastructure acknowledgements;
- people to acknowledge who are not authors;
- whether permits/ethical approvals are applicable to this synthesis;
- any wording required by funders.

If no permits are applicable because the manuscript is a secondary synthesis, state that only after author confirmation.

### 6. Conflict of interest

Replace the title-page placeholder with either the approved conflict declaration or the approved no-conflict statement.

### 7. Data and code availability at submission

The reviewer-facing anonymous package is already reproduced in CI.

Before final submission confirm whether the journal requires:

- anonymous private reviewer access only at first submission;
- a DOI-bearing public archive at first submission;
- or public archive only at acceptance.

Do not expose author identity through an archive used for double-anonymous review.

### 8. Exclusivity / prior-publication confirmation

All authors must confirm that the exact manuscript is not published, accepted or under consideration elsewhere, subject to any journal policy on preprints.

### 9. Third-party source/data reuse

Confirm that the submission wording and distributed reviewer package comply with source licences and that no restricted raw source files are redistributed.

The package should contain analysis-ready derived evidence and permitted public-source material only.

### 10. Final all-author approval

Obtain explicit approval of:

- manuscript;
- title page;
- figures/tables;
- cover letter;
- declarations;
- exact submitted version.
