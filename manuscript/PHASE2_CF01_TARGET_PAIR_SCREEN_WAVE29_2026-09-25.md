## CFTQ0283 — Achillea millefolium

This is a strong direct isolation experiment. Plants were transplanted to isolated and non-isolated
islands while resource availability, population size and inter-individual spacing were held
constant. Pollen deposition and fecundity were directly measured.

The biological design passes, but the public source surface does not expose the independent island
replicate count/identity or island-level response variance.

Decision:
`blocked_island_replicate_count_and_fragment_level_IF_variance_not_publicly_recoverable`.

Transplanted individuals cannot become fragmentation n. The reported absence of an isolation effect
is not interpreted as equivalence.

## CFTQ0286 — Calystegia collina

The source compares **16 small versus seven large serpentine outcrops** and directly measures bee
visitation and reproductive success. Public descriptions report **39 plant patches** nested across
the 23 outcrops.

The independent habitat unit is the outcrop, not the plant patch.

The public reporting surface does not expose the 23 outcrop-level paired I/F vectors or certified
between-outcrop dispersion.

Decision:
`blocked_outcrop_level_IF_marginals_and_dispersion_not_publicly_recoverable`.

Local flowering-patch density and nearby compatible-patch availability remain mechanistic/moderator
variables and do not replace the frozen small-versus-large habitat-size contrast.
