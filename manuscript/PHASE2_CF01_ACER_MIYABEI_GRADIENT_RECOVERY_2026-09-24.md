multilayer programme**.

This is not outcome-blind: Table 1 values and the published qualitative conclusions were visible
before the deterministic recovery rule was written. The result is therefore generalisation evidence,
not prospective confirmation.

## Locked variables

- exposure: log distance to nearest forest; higher = greater isolation
- C: negative mean seed kinship; higher = greater gene-flow support
- F: viable seed density = source seed density × source viable-seed proportion

The F endpoint is an EGWEE deterministic derivation from two Table-1 reproductive components; it
was not the source paper's original modelled response and is labelled accordingly.

The interval crosses zero. This programme therefore does **not** resolve a precise C-F separation

## Endpoint-definition sensitivities

On the same nine forests:

- source seed density alone: r = **+0.643**, Fisher z = **+0.763**
- source viable-seed proportion alone: r = **−0.262**, Fisher z = **−0.268**

The two source components move in different directions with isolation. That sensitivity is
scientifically important: a conclusion about "reproductive function" would depend strongly on
whether quantity, viability, or their product were chosen. Neither visible component is promoted
post hoc to replace the deterministic viable-seed-density primary endpoint.
