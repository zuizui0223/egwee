## Most informative closures

### CFTQ0291 — Persoonia mollis paternity study

The source directly measures pollen flow, paternity, fruit set and male/female reproductive success,
but only **within one natural population**. Disturbance and fragmented populations of related taxa
motivate the conservation discussion rather than define the study exposure.

Decision: baseline C/F mating-system evidence; no fragmentation programme.

### CFTQ0292 — Gentianella austriaca

Seven mountain, foothill and lowland populations are compared for standing genetic diversity,
reproductive traits, germination, population size and isolation. The study does not directly estimate
movement/connectivity C, and altitude/population history are entangled with the relict-population
comparison.

Decision: retain G_adult/F conservation evidence; no direct C-F programme.
