# Phase-2 SF05-83 Oenocarpus primary quantitative gate — 2026-09-19

## Decision

Browne & Karubian 2018 (*Oenocarpus bataua*) is **closed for the current prespecified Phase-2 pair families**.

This is an effect-unit and estimand closure. It is not based on effect direction or source significance.

## Source frame

The source sampled 11 distinct forest fragments. Continuous-forest seedlings came from Bilsa Biological Station (BBS), one large continuous-forest reserve.

For plot-level diversity comparisons, spatially proximate continuous-forest seedlings were combined **post hoc** into 10 larger plots to match fragment sampling area and sample size.

Those 10 plots are valid source sampling patches, but they do not create 10 independent continuous-forest landscapes for the EGWEE fragmentation-unit denominator. Treating them as 10 independent reference fragmentation units would overstate reference replication.

## Layer mismatch

The study partitions genetic information in established seedlings into:

- paternal/male gametes;
- maternal/female gametes;
- diploid seedling leaf tissue.

It does not provide an adult-population genetic-state cohort on the same fragment/reference frame.

Therefore it cannot increment the registered `G_adult-G_offspring` pair family.

It also does not provide F, so it cannot increment C-F or G_adult-mean(I,F).

## What remains useful

The study is strong mechanistic evidence that pollen and seed contributions can respond differently to habitat loss and fragmentation. Its 11 fragments also support a within-fragment continuous forest-cover analysis.

That possible gradient/generalisation analysis is not opened here because it is not one of the current pair-specific promotion gates and would require a separately frozen endpoint/exposure contract.
