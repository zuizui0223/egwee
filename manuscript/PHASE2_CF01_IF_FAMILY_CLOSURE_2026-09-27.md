programme, a linked/no-new-K identity state, or a terminal recoverability/estimand/effect-unit

- Sevenello: one programme with three dependent primary species panels; programme-level internal

- Bergsdorf Kakamega: one programme with four dependent recovered species×campaign panels;
  programme-level Bonferroni p = **0.00653539**, driven by the Acanthopale panel.

These programmes raise direct I-F coverage from 1/5 to **3/5**, but do not satisfy the five-programme
analysis-opening threshold and do not alter the frozen Phase-1 five-cluster synthesis.

## Why additional candidates did not become K

Terminal I-F candidates repeatedly failed frozen empirical requirements for reasons including:

- direct I and F were measured in different experiments, chapters, campaigns or site frames;
- the habitat exposure was population size/density rather than a source-defined fragmentation
  variable;
- several correlated landscape variables/radii were available but no unique common estimand could
  be selected without outcome-driven choice;
- response vectors were only graphical and figure digitization was not authorized;
- public reporting lacked fragment/site-level marginal variance at the fragmentation unit;
- lower-level plants, flowers, visits, fruits or observation periods could not be promoted to
  fragmentation n;
- umbrella dissertations duplicated or concatenated primary publications rather than supplying one
  independent common effect unit.

No candidate was closed because its direction was null or because it disagreed with a theoretical
expectation.

## High-information terminal cases

The biological design is strong: three small and three large fragments with equal pollinator
observation effort and natural fruit set.

The public paper does not establish that the published class SDs are between-fragment SDs, and the
six paired fragment-level I/F values are not exposed.

Status: `blocked_fragment_level_IF_dispersion_and_dependence_not_recoverable`.

This is a variance/effect-unit boundary, not a null I-F result.

`blocked_single_fragmentation_estimand_and_common_population_IF_vectors_not_publicly_recoverable`.

## Separate gradient/generalisation evidence

Failure to open the pooled direct I-F family does not mean there is little empirical I-F evidence.

The separate Fisher-z gradient/generalisation registry contains **six programmes / 19 primary
marginal effects**, including:

- Eucalyptus wandoo;
- Zurich phytometer panels;
- Toronto common milkweed;
- Acer miyabei;
- Cardiopetalum calophyllum;
- Pritchard orchard isolation.

Cardiopetalum provides a particularly clear gradient example of interaction persistence alongside
reproductive collapse, but it remains in the gradient stream and is not converted into direct
Hedges-g K.

## Statistical consequence

The direct I-F family does **not** meet its five-programme analysis-opening gate.

Accordingly EGWEE must not report:

- a pooled direct I-F meta-analysis across independent programmes;
- an I-F moderator model;
- a universal direct interaction-versus-function ordering.

The correct statement is that direct pair-specific coverage is **3/5 and search-complete**.

## Biological consequence

The empirical corpus contains both regimes:

- concordant interaction and reproductive deterioration, as in the Aizen–Feinsinger programme;
- strong interaction–function separation, as in the recovered Acanthopale panel and the independent
  Cardiopetalum gradient evidence.

This heterogeneity is itself biologically informative, but the present direct family is too small
for a pooled cross-system law.

## Reopening rule

The direct I-F family may change only if a currently terminal programme's stated missing evidence
becomes available—for example:

- authoritative site/fragment-level I and F vectors;
- certified fragmentation-unit marginal variance;
- a source table that resolves a previously ambiguous common landscape estimand;
- a thesis/publication map proving identical sites and campaign across I and F.

Reopening uses the already-frozen endpoint and effect-unit rules. It does not trigger a new search
merely to reach K=5.

## Theory firewall

This closure is entirely empirical.

The 3/5 coverage state neither validates nor falsifies an EGWE/NEE finite operator. EGWEE estimates
natural fragmentation-response geometry; EGWE owns the finite-model mechanism theory. Agreement
with a theoretical narrative cannot promote a terminal empirical candidate into the quantitative
family.
