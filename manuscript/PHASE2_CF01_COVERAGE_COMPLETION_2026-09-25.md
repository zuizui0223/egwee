## What the completed screen found

The completed search did not simply produce a larger K. It produced several distinct empirical
outcomes:

1. **admissible new programmes**, where independent-unit values and dependence are recoverable;
2. **design-valid but quantitatively blocked programmes**, where biological design is good but
   site/fragment-level values or dispersion are not recoverable;
3. **mechanistic anchors**, where fragmentation clearly alters processes but the candidate does not
   fit the frozen target pair (for example C→D recruitment or movement-compensation cases);
4. **estimand stops**, where several landscape variables/radii/chapters exist and no one common
   response-free exposure can be chosen without post hoc selection;
5. **umbrella/duplicate-source linkages**, where theses or companion papers map to an already defined
   biological programme and therefore do not increase K;
6. **true closures**, including non-plant systems, gymnosperms, reviews, simulations, population-size
   studies without habitat fragmentation, single-reference pseudoreplication and studies missing one
   target layer.

These categories are part of the evidence, not discarded failed searches.

## Search-completion claim ceiling

The completed screen authorizes the following statement:

> The preregistered CF01 target-pair candidate universe has been exhaustively screened under the
> frozen eligibility and effect-unit rules through the queue cutoff.

It does **not** authorize:

- treating every advanced candidate as admitted;
- stopping full-text recovery because a desired K was reached;
- reopening closed studies because an effect would strengthen a pooled result;
- coding missing biological layers as zero;
- counting thesis chapters, species panels, years or linked publications as independent programmes;
- merging Hedges-g and Fisher-z streams;
- claiming a universal layer ordering from the completed search.

## Boundary with EGWE

CF01 queue order, screening, closure, admission, effect recovery and stopping were determined by
empirical search and effect-unit contracts inside EGWEE.

No EGWE/NEE operator, warning rule, reserve variable, finite-model success criterion or desired
theoretical agreement entered the screening denominator or stopping rule.

The allowed relationship remains downstream and asymmetric:

- EGWEE can constrain which natural phenomena a useful theory should explain;
- EGWE can cite a pinned EGWEE result as external consistency evidence;
- EGWEE search completion is **not** validation of a finite EGWE operator sequence.
