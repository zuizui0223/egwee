## CFTQ0155 — Aextoxicon punctatum

This source is biologically strong but does **not** enter the direct C-F effect family.

The study compares three small and three large ancient rain-forest patches and directly quantifies:

- patch-level fruit production / source limitation;
- bird-mediated seed dispersal;
- microsatellite assignment of dispersed seeds to source trees;
- seed immigration between patches;
- establishment after seed addition.

However, the source's movement inference does not use one six-patch C effect unit. The three large
patches have individual immigrant-seed estimates, while the authors explicitly pool S1-S3 for the
small-patch immigration estimate because genetic seed samples are sparse.

EGWEE therefore does not manufacture three equal-precision small-patch C effects from the Table-1
cell counts.

Decision: **close direct C-F on common-effect-unit grounds; retain as a movement-compensation
process anchor**.

The process signal is ecologically important: low local fecundity in small patches coexists with
substantial incoming seed movement. Fragmentation can therefore depress local source function while
movement across the patch network remains active.

Direct C-F programme increment: **0**.

## CFTQ0156 — Schüepp cherry landscape experiment

The source uses **30 spatially separated landscape sectors** with standardized seven-tree cherry
rows. It explicitly separates:

- woody-habitat isolation (fragmentation per se);
- woody-habitat amount;
- cherry-tree isolation;
- cherry abundance;
- local conspecific and heterospecific flower density.

Direct pollinator visitation and direct open fruit set are measured at the same experimental-site
scale. The source omits two sites from fruit-set analysis and three from visitation analysis.

EGWEE retains this as a **gradient/generalisation I-F candidate** under a frozen retrospective
contract:

- independent unit = landscape sector/site;
- primary fragmentation exposure = distance to nearest woody habitat;
- I = all-potential-pollinator visitation rate;
- F = open/control fruit set;
- the common site frame is determined mechanically by source missingness;
- cherry isolation and woody-habitat amount cannot replace the primary exposure because their
  published relationships are stronger.

No EGWEE effect has yet been calculated. Admission now depends only on recoverability of the
site-level common frame.
