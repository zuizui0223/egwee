## Strongest unresolved direct I-F candidates

### CFTQ0312 — urban mangrove Avicennia marina

The source uses replicated large, medium and small mangrove stands inside **two estuaries**, with the
abstract describing three replicate stands per size class per estuary (maximum 18 stands).

Direct pollinator activity and fruit production are both measured. The independent unit is the
**mangrove stand**; estuary is a blocking factor.

Status: `pending_18stand_common_IF_values_and_estuary_block_recovery`.

No binary threshold is invented. If the source is best represented as an ordered stand-size
gradient, it remains in the gradient/generalisation stream.

### CFTQ0315 — Myrtus communis

The source contains **six Large/Small populations** in a fragmented Mediterranean landscape with
visitation/pollen-limitation and direct reproductive output.

A six-population gate now freezes:

- population/site as n;
- broad total visitation as preferred I;
- natural fruit set as preferred F;
- honeybee-hive context as a moderator rather than the fragmentation treatment.

Status: `pending_six_population_IF_values_and_fragment_class_variance_recovery`.

## Other full-text gates

### CFTQ0313 — Banksia caesia linked 2012/2013 programme

The 2013 reproductive/progeny paper and 2012 mating/pollen-dispersal paper are biologically linked,
but C and F cannot be paired until remnant IDs, campaign timing and one common fragmentation
estimand are aligned.

Status: `pending_linked_2012_2013_population_and_campaign_alignment`.

### CFTQ0314 — Kodagu coffee

The source has strong pollinator and fruit-set biology across 41 coffee/forest locations, but
multiple distances are nested inside locations and the F experiment uses a subset. Treating the
141/205 distance-position observations as independent landscapes would pseudoreplicate the 41
highest-level units.

Status: `blocked_common_location_level_IF_effect_not_recoverable_from_published_nested_analysis`.

### CFTQ0316 — Astrocaryum mexicanum

Six forest fragments provide a clean fragment-level pollinator contrast, but the exact 1999
fragment-level fruit-set effect is not textually recoverable without digitizing a figure or deriving
an effect from a p-value bound.

Status: `blocked_exact_fragment_level_F_effect_not_recoverable_without_figure_digitization`.

### CFTQ0317 — Polylepis australis

Twelve independent fragments exist, but response reporting is primarily tree-level and does not
expose a 12-fragment I/F vector or a source hierarchical effect directly convertible to the
registered Fisher-z stream.

Status: `blocked_fragment_level_IF_aggregation_not_recoverable_from_published_tree_level_summary`.
