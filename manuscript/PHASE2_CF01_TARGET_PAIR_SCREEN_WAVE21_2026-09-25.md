## Full-text resolutions

### CFTQ0202 — Bartomeus landscape × invasion programme

The study uses **14 independent riparian sites** and jointly measures Raphanus visitation and fruit
set. It is biologically eligible for a gradient I-F analysis, but the source landscape representation
is not one pre-existing scalar fragmentation variable:

- agricultural, forest and grassland cover are all analysed;
- source-selected radii differ among pollinator guilds;
- before/during Impatiens flowering periods are modelled separately;
- final source models use stepwise reduction.

Decision: retain at
`pending_landscape_estimand_radius_and_common_Raphanus_IF_frame`.

EGWEE will not choose a land-cover variable, radius, pollinator guild or period from the published
significance pattern. A common site-level estimand must be fixed before any effect is calculated.

### CFTQ0204 — Heliconia acuminata seed-limitation programme

The ten-plot BDFFP source frame is strong: four fragment plots and six continuous-forest plots.
The paper directly models seed production, dispersal and recruitment limitation.

However, Table 3's plot-level "estimated seed production" is a potential-production quantity
constructed from inflorescence number with a fixed **20 fruits × 3 seeds per inflorescence**
assumption, rather than observed realised fruit/seed production.

Decision:
`retain_C_D_seed_limitation_close_CF_no_direct_observed_F`.

This programme remains valuable as a fragmentation **C → D / seed-limitation process anchor**.
EGWEE does not promote modelled potential seed supply to direct F merely because it is available on
all ten plots.

### CFTQ0209 — Byrsonima sericea

The source has a particularly clean habitat design:

- three small restinga fragments (0.07–0.28 ha);
- three large fragments (14–99 ha);
- equal 160-min pollinator-observation effort per fragment;
- direct pollinator visit frequency;
- direct natural fruit set.

The public paper reports fragment-class mean±SD, but its statistical methods analyse lower-level
individual observations nested within fragments. The source does not expose the six fragment-level
I/F vectors or establish that the reported SDs are between-fragment SDs.

Decision:
`blocked_fragment_level_IF_dispersion_and_dependence_not_recoverable`.

This is a **design-valid direct I-F candidate**, not an ecological null. Direct I-F coverage is not
incremented because the fragmentation-unit marginal variances are not certified. If six
fragment-level summaries become available, the frozen cluster-robust fallback can handle unknown
within-cluster covariance; covariance is not set to zero by convenience.

### CFTQ0206 — Braun dissertation

The dissertation is an umbrella source containing multiple species/chapter-specific fragment
programmes. Some chapters correspond to later Braun-led primary publications already screened or
discoverable separately.

Decision: `linked_umbrella_source_no_new_K`.

The thesis is retained for chapter/publication identity linkage and source recovery only. It is not
one independent programme.
