### B. Full SF06 passes, non-overlap subset does not

Interpretation:

> The larger source database is consistent with the EGWEE topology, but the evidence cannot be called an independent replication because the robust signal may depend on publications already represented in the frozen EGWEE map or on reduced non-overlap coverage.

Allowed wording: **external-source confirmation with partial source overlap**.

The current 16-programme result remains valid as a frozen within-corpus structural result, but its broader generality is not upgraded.

### C. Full SF06 is non-identifying but influence-sensitive

Interpretation:

Allowed wording: **external existence support, not robust generalization**.

### D. Full SF06 fails the coverage gate

Interpretation:

No external generality conclusion. Do not treat a sparse sign map as validation.

### E. Full SF06 is identifying

Interpretation:

The external source does not reproduce the EGWEE many-to-many sign topology under the preregistered pairing rule.

The manuscript must then keep translation non-identifiability explicitly limited to the frozen EGWEE evidence universe. Do not redefine signs, pairing, exposure or source inclusion to restore the result.

## Lane 2 — compatibility as a translation modifier

Primary representation-specific model:

`d_F = alpha + beta*d_I + gamma*SC + error`

### M1. gamma_SC > 0 and 95% CI excludes zero

Interpretation:

Compatibility explains residual female-fitness response after conditioning on observed pollination response on the source Hedges-d scale.

If the non-overlap sensitivity is directionally concordant, this provides external support for **compatibility as one translation modifier**.

It still does not establish measured effective reproductive assurance.

### M2. gamma_SC > 0 but CI includes zero

Interpretation:

Directionally consistent but unresolved. Keep effective reproductive assurance as a prospective hypothesis.

### M3. gamma_SC <= 0

Interpretation:

The compatibility translation prediction is not supported in SF06. Do not replace compatibility with another moderator after seeing the result.

The broader topology result, if supported, remains logically independent of this mechanism failure.

No compatibility mechanism promotion is allowed when the sign itself reverses under publication balance.
