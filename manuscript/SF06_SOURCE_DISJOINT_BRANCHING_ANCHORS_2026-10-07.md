# Source-disjoint external branching anchors — 2026-10-07

## Purpose

The corrected SF06 public-S1 topology generalizes interaction→function non-identifiability after deleting every publication already used in the frozen EGWEE I–F map. This note asks whether the external mismatches include source-level biological examples of both mismatch directions, rather than only small sign fluctuations.

These examples are interpretive anchors, not new independent prevalence trials.

## Buffering direction — Samanea saman

Source: Cascante et al. (2002), Conservation Biology 16:137–147, doi:10.1046/j.1523-1739.2002.00317.x.

Corrected SF06 pair:

- pollination d = -0.936;
- female-fitness d = +0.553;
- both absolute effects exceed 0.5;
- marginal directional support is approximately 95.2% for the pollination sign and 97.3% for the female-fitness sign.

The source biology matches a buffering interpretation:

- flowers on isolated trees were much less likely to receive enough pollen tubes to produce mature fruit;
- total stigma pollen loads were similar between isolated and continuous trees;
- seed number, seed abortion and undamaged-seed output were maintained;
- isolated-tree progeny nevertheless showed greater relatedness/inbreeding and reduced vigor.

Thus a loss in effective pollination need not appear immediately as reduced seed output, and apparent short-term reproductive buffering can coexist with downstream quality costs.

## False-reassurance direction — Tristerix corymbosus

Source: Magrach, Santamaría & Larrinaga (2013), Journal of Ecology 101:713–721, doi:10.1111/1365-2745.12083.

Corrected SF06 pair:

- pollination d = +0.176;
- female-fitness d = -1.609.

The pollination sign is only moderately resolved in the meta-analytic row, so this is not promoted as an individually resolved sign reversal. However, the source-level process geometry is unusually explicit:

- visitation by the dominant hummingbird pollinator was unaffected by forest edges;
- pollen-tube formation increased near edges;
- fruit set was broadly comparable across the edge gradient;
- fruit removal by the sole seed disperser declined strongly near edges;
- estimated overall reproductive success therefore declined sharply near edges.

The source authors explicitly concluded that successive life-cycle interactions responded in opposite directions and that seed dispersal, not pollination, dictated the final edge effect on reproductive performance.

This is a direct example of **parallel-pathway override**: an apparently intact or enhanced upstream mutualism does not diagnose final reproductive performance because a different downstream mutualism becomes limiting.

## Why the pair is useful

Samanea and Tristerix are both absent from the frozen EGWEE I–F map used to define the original sentinel result.

Together they show two biologically distinct source-disjoint routes to the same statistical non-identifiability:

- **buffering:** upstream deterioration with temporarily retained reproductive output;
- **false reassurance:** retained/improved upstream interaction with downstream reproductive loss.

The two directions therefore do not require one shared hidden mechanism.

## General interpretation

The external generalization is best understood as a **branching life-cycle problem**.

Fragmentation can alter:

- interaction quantity;
- donor provenance / compatible mating;
- plant resource state and post-transfer viability;
- compensatory reproductive routes;
- later mutualisms such as seed dispersal.

Reproductive function integrates these branches. A pollination response can therefore be a genuine disturbance response and still be an insufficient stand-alone sentinel of final reproductive condition.

## Claim ceiling

Allowed:

- Samanea and Tristerix provide source-disjoint biological examples of opposite translation failures;
- the external many-to-many topology is compatible with multiple biological mechanisms;
- Tristerix directly illustrates a parallel downstream mutualism overriding pollination as a sentinel.

Not allowed:

- these two species estimate the prevalence of buffering versus false reassurance;
- the SF06 Tristerix pollination sign is individually two-sided-95%-resolved;
- all mismatches share the same life-cycle mechanism;
- seed dispersal is the dominant hidden branch across the full corpus.
