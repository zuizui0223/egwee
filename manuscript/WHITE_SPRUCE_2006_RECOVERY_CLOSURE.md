# ML017 / PS022 Picea glauca recovery closure

## Decision

ML017 is **not admitted** to the primary multilayer Hedges-g stream from the currently recoverable public evidence.

Status: `primary_F_stand_vector_or_pairing_not_reconstructable`.

## Passed gates

- Independent system relative to ML001–ML003 and ML014.
- Source-defined replicated stand-size exposure with 23 stands and a prelocked small (`1 to <10` trees) versus large (`>=100`) direct contrast.
- Stand, rather than tree/family/cone/seed/embryo/locus, is a defensible fragmentation unit.
- The linked 2006 reproductive and mating-system papers are correctly treated as one dependent programme/cluster rather than two independent studies.
- The mating-system paper publicly exposes the complete stand-level multilocus outcrossing (`t_m`) vector for all 23 stands, so the locked G endpoint is recoverable at the correct unit.

## Blocking gate

The reproductive-success source reports that mean filled seeds per cone was analysed against stand size across the same 23 stands and that small stands produced substantially fewer filled seeds than large stands. However, the publicly recoverable article material exposes the stand-level F response as a scatterplot/regression rather than as a numeric table keyed to stand IDs.

That is insufficient for the predeclared primary cluster because ML017 requires:

1. one exact filled-seeds-per-cone value per stand;
2. the exact stand ID for each F value;
3. alignment of that F vector to the public stand-level `t_m` vector; and
4. a within-cluster sampling covariance reconstructed from the paired stand frame.

Several stands share the same stand-size category or similar/identical tree counts. Therefore a plot point cannot be assumed to identify a specific A–W stand merely from x-position. Digitising the figure after the state-separation direction is already known would also require a separately frozen and independently validated digitisation-error method under the ML017 contract; no such amendment was made.

Tree-level reproductive observations (102 trees, cones nested below trees) are not substituted as fragmentation replicates, and the published regression or class-level summaries are not converted into a pseudo stand-level covariance merely to fill the fifth-cluster slot.

## Reopening condition

Reopen ML017 only if a source table/raw dataset supplies the 23 stand-level filled-seeds-per-cone values with stand identifiers (or an equivalent exact source representation that preserves stand replication and permits pairing with `t_m`).

No hypothetical ML017 cluster p-value is inserted into the Fisher synthesis. The canonical omit-ML001 result therefore remains unchanged until another independent same-effect-family primary cluster is actually admitted.
