# Natural-data programme: four empirical state-measurement gates

**Publication status:** deferred empirical programme, explicitly outside the active warning-validity and model state-validity submissions. The four lines below preserve their locked results and STOP decisions without treating heterogeneous systems as validation of one synthetic mechanism. Journal and final paper structure are intentionally undecided until the four lines are assessed as an independent empirical package.

## Shared empirical question

When a natural system is used to represent a future-relevant ecological state, where can the inference fail before residual habitat, origin, or history is interpreted? The existing audits resolve four logically different outcomes. They must remain separate because they imply different next measurements and different claim ceilings.

The common ordered logic is:

`measurement adequacy -> representation/information preservation -> residual origin/history`.

Datasets remain at their own ecological resolutions and are never pooled merely to create a larger sample. Whole sites, gardens, maternal plants, arrays, populations, or orchards remain the declared holdout units. Archive-access failures, metadata mismatches, failed predictive gates, and non-identifiability outcomes remain visible and are not repaired after observing their consequences.

## Line 1 — residual context adds no transferable gain after an informative partial state

### Honshu–Izu

The functional partial state `TM_z + FDQ + FEve` had held-out MSE `1.08774`. Adding mainland distance increased it to `1.13209`, a **4.08% worsening**, and improved only **3/8** held-out sites. The result supports no detected transferable distance gain after that partial state; it does not prove state completeness.

### Zurich

Across six fixed reproductive endpoints, **0/6** met the preregistered positive residual-context rule after the source-defined function-specific pollinator interaction state was supplied.

### Toronto

Adding urban cover and green-space edge density after the locked partial pollination/floral state increased total held-out NLL by `4932.9195`, with a garden-bootstrap 95% interval `[603.9654,10953.6611]`.

**Claim ceiling:** these are residual-context negative results under their own held-out designs. They do not establish that distance, urbanisation, or habitat context is biologically irrelevant, and they do not establish complete state measurement.

## Line 2 — a process coordinate remains missing after the current state is measured

### *Oenothera harringtonii*

Adding maternal spatial isolation after pollinator treatment reduced leave-one-maternal-plant-out MSE from `0.11619` to `0.09187`, a **20.93% improvement**; the locked permutation test gave `p=0.00130`.

**Interpretation:** spatial mating opportunity remained a missing contemporary mating-state coordinate. This is evidence that the preceding state representation was incomplete for the declared endpoint, not a direct functional-loss result and not a general spatial-isolation law.

## Line 3 — a plausible process proxy fails endpoint-relevant measurement adequacy

### *Eschscholzia californica*

The primary seed-function endpoint remained `multi_endpoint_not_identifiable` after the exact metadata gate found `Fallow ground` versus `Fallow graound`. A prospective one-key sensitivity then found the same unexpected mismatch at a second array and stopped before model fitting. No repaired F estimate exists. For independently estimable mating and pollen-movement endpoints, the preregistered pan-trap count/mean-ITD state did not show reproducible held-out gain.

### Mallorca carob

Two non-identical deposited pollinator-abundance representations were both mandatory. Their fruit-production gains were `-0.10195 [-3.12202,3.61919]` and `-0.09919 [-3.14415,3.66453]`. Neither earned process-measurement adequacy, so **the residual-context gate was not opened**.

**Claim ceiling:** failed adequacy means the candidate proxy did not earn state status for the declared endpoint under the frozen design. It does not imply pollinator irrelevance and does not authorise endpoint switching, proxy substitution, or post-result repair.

## Line 4 — preprocessing can erase the mechanistic information a measurement was meant to add

### *Campanula americana*

None of the fixed interaction representations improved leave-one-population-out prediction of pollen limitation over the mean baseline. A response-firewalled diagnostic showed that the effective-transfer coordinates were constant positive rescalings of their phase-visitation coordinates; after feature-wise standardisation the paired predictors differed by at most `8.88e-16`. The representation had erased the efficiency multiplier that made the mechanistic measurement distinct. No alternative scaling or endpoint rerun was opened.

**Interpretation:** biological measurement and analytical representation are separate gates. A mechanistically richer raw measurement cannot improve identification if preprocessing projects it onto the same coordinate as the simpler proxy.

## Cross-origin synthesis remains not identifiable

Honshu–Izu and Zurich use different biological coordinates and responses, and each origin is represented by one study. Origin is therefore confounded with study, taxa, protocol, and response construction. The locked decision remains `cross_origin_convergence_not_identifiable_from_existing_archives`; no pooled outcome model is authorised. A subsequent four-archive minimal bridge stopped at automated archive access before schema mapping or outcome modelling.

This STOP is retained as a design boundary. It is not evidence for or against a shared urban–island law.

## Why these four lines are separated from EGWE warning validity

None of the four empirical lines estimates the sensitivity, specificity, discrimination, or full-denominator validity of the frozen genetic warning rules. Conversely, the warning-validity result does not validate any natural-system state proxy. Keeping the programmes separate prevents temporal-precedence results from borrowing ecological externality and prevents heterogeneous natural data from being presented as validation of a warning statistic.

## Next publication gate

Before selecting a journal or deciding whether these four lines form one empirical synthesis or multiple papers, assess whether a common contribution survives without pooling incompatible endpoints. The minimum shared contribution is methodological: natural state claims pass through four empirically distinguishable failure modes—residual-context redundancy, missing coordinates, inadequate proxies, and information-erasing representation. Support, non-support, `not_identifiable`, `not_estimable`, and access STOPs must remain explicit outcomes.
