## Ecological information from the negative screen

This is not simply “no studies found.” The screen shows a structural separation in the literature:

- fragmentation-genetics papers often quantify pollen/seed movement, mating and cohort genetic state in detail;
- reproductive-function studies are often separate publications/programmes;
- therefore direct within-programme **movement/connectivity → reproductive function** geometry is much rarer than either layer alone.

That pattern supports keeping C-F as a distinct estimand rather than constructing cross-publication pseudo-pairs.
