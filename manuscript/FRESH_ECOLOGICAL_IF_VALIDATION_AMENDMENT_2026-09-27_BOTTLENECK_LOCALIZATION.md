# Fresh ecological I–F validation amendment — bottleneck localization

**Frozen:** 2026-09-27

## Why an amendment is needed

The original fresh-validation preregistration proposed that effective-mating-quality measurements should tend to recouple interaction and reproductive function relative to quantity-only interaction measures.

After that preregistration was written, a secondary audit was completed using **already burned discovery programmes only**: ML001 *Serapias*, ML002 *Brosimum*, ML014 *Eucalyptus socialis* and P2_CF01_ACER_MIYABEI_2014.

That audit showed that movement/mating support can itself deteriorate more strongly than F. *Serapias* resolves this direction, while *Brosimum* and *Eucalyptus socialis* point the same way but remain unresolved. Acer is unresolved in the opposite point direction.

No fresh post-cutoff programme or prospective field outcome has been opened. The amendment is therefore made **before fresh validation data** and records the discovery evidence that motivated the change.

## Amendment to H2

### Superseded narrow wording

> Effective-mating-quality interaction measurements should show smaller I–F mismatch than quantity-only interaction measurements.

This remains one possible outcome but is no longer the only confirmatory geometry.

### H2 v2 — effective mating localizes the bottleneck

In fresh programmes that measure interaction quantity, effective mating quality and reproductive function on the same independent units, the effective-mating response should identify where the quantity–function mismatch arises.

Define oriented effects so more negative means stronger deterioration under fragmentation:

`Q = effect(I_quantity)`

`E = effect(I_effective)`

`F = effect(F_reproductive)`

and contrasts:

`Delta_QF = Q - F`

`Delta_QE = Q - E`

`Delta_EF = E - F`.

For a quantity-buffered F-dominant mismatch, `Delta_QF > 0`.

The primary H2-v2 prediction is:

> **If effective mating is the missing bottleneck between interaction quantity and reproduction, then E should be more negative than Q (`Delta_QE > 0`).**

The sign and magnitude of `Delta_EF` then distinguish ecological sub-regimes rather than serving as a mandatory recoupling criterion.

## Prespecified bottleneck regimes

### B1 — direct mating-quality propagation

- `Delta_QF > 0`;
- `Delta_QE > 0`;
- `Delta_EF` near zero within uncertainty.

Interpretation: quantity is buffered, effective mating deteriorates, and F tracks effective mating.

### B2 — mating bottleneck with downstream compensation

- `Delta_QF > 0`;
- `Delta_QE > 0`;
- `Delta_EF < 0` (E more negative than F).

Interpretation: effective mating is strongly impaired, but reproductive output is partially buffered downstream.

### B3 — post-mating / post-pollination bottleneck

- `Delta_QF > 0`;
- `Delta_QE` near zero;
- `Delta_EF > 0` (F more negative than E).

Interpretation: quantity and effective mating are comparatively retained, while a later reproductive filter deteriorates.

### B4 — coupled chain deterioration

- Q, E and F all deteriorate;
- pairwise contrasts are unresolved or biologically small.

Interpretation: no detectable decoupling along the measured chain.

Other geometries remain reportable and are not forced into these four states.

## Confirmatory opening rule

H2-v2 is tested only in genuinely fresh programmes with Q, E and F measured on the same independent fragmentation units and exposure window.

Cross-programme inference opens separately within an effect family only after at least **5 fresh Q–E–F programmes**.

Primary cross-programme estimands:

1. mean `Delta_QE` — primary bottleneck-localization estimand;
2. mean `Delta_QF` — quantity–function decoupling estimand;
3. mean `Delta_EF` — secondary localization/compensation estimand.

The primary directional prediction is mean `Delta_QE > 0` among fresh programmes meeting the Q–E–F gate.

No Hedges-g / Fisher-z cross-family pooling is allowed.

## Pollen-supplementation rescue

Where experimentally feasible, hand cross-pollination or pollen supplementation remains an independent mechanism check.

Rescue of F under supplementation strengthens a pollen/mating limitation interpretation, but supplementation is not required to classify Q–E–F geometry.

## Discovery firewall

The following discovery systems cannot count toward H2-v2 confirmation:

- all eight current I–F census programmes;
- ML001 *Serapias*;
- ML002 *Brosimum*;
- ML014 *Eucalyptus socialis*;
- P2_CF01_ACER_MIYABEI_2014.

These systems may be discussed only as motivation or external context.
