# CFTQ0155 Aextoxicon process anchor — 2026-09-24

## Why this is retained but not admitted as direct C-F

Núñez-Ávila et al. (2013), doi:10.1111/1365-2745.12148, compare three small and three large ancient
rain-forest patches and quantify fecundity, seed dispersal and establishment.

The reproductive-function layer is genuinely patch-level. Table 1 reports total fruit production
per hectare separately for S1, S2, S3, L1, L2 and L3.

The movement/connectivity layer is not source-estimated on the same six-patch effect unit. For seed
immigration, the authors report L1, L2 and L3 individually but explicitly pool the genotyped seeds
from S1-S3 because small-patch seed samples were too sparse.

Therefore EGWEE does **not** turn Table-1 small-patch cell counts into three equally precise C
replicates when the source itself pooled them for inference.

Decision: `close_no_common_patch_level_C_effect_unit`.

Direct C-F programme increment: **0**.

## Ecological process signal retained

The source pattern is nevertheless important process evidence:

- source-level fecundity is much lower in small than large patches;
- the pooled small-patch immigrant-seed proportion is reported as 40%;
- large-patch immigrant-seed proportions are reported as 14.3%, 6.5% and 16%;
- bird-mediated dispersal can therefore connect fragments even where local seed production is
  strongly source-limited.

This is retained as a **movement-compensation / source-limitation anchor**, not as a meta-analytic
C-F effect.

The ecological interpretation is that low local reproductive output and high incoming movement can
coexist. A single "fragmentation deterioration state" would miss that process geometry.

## No rescue

Do not:

- treat the one- and two-seed small-patch genetic samples as equal-precision patch C effects;
- use Table-1 mismatch variants to select the largest C-F separation;
- combine pooled-small C with patch-level F as if they shared one six-patch covariance matrix;
- use seed traps or genotyped seeds as independent fragmentation units;
- convert the process anchor into validation of an EGWE/NEE mechanism.
