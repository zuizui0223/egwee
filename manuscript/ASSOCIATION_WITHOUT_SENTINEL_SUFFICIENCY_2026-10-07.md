# Association without sentinel sufficiency: a branching-pathway interpretation — 2026-10-07

## Empirical pattern

Three results now coexist.

1. **Shared directional response to fragmentation is real.**  
   All 17 primary direct effects are negative on both oriented Hedges g and lnRR.

2. **Pollination and female-fitness effects are associated on average.**  
   Aguilar et al. report a positive cross-species association, and the corrected SF06 fragmentation-only model retains a positive pollination coefficient (beta=+0.190, p=0.0053).

3. **Pollination state is still non-identifying for reproductive state.**  
   The corrected SF06 sign table is many-to-many, survives source-publication and species deletion, and provides zero incremental binary classification gain over the fragmentation-context baseline in the full and source-disjoint subsets.

These statements are not contradictory.

## Biological interpretation: fragmentation is a branching driver, not a single cascade

A simple monitoring picture is:

fragmentation → interaction quantity → reproduction.

The empirical evidence is better represented by a branching structure:

fragmentation → interaction quantity  
fragmentation → transfer provenance / effective mating  
fragmentation → post-transfer viability / plant condition  
fragmentation → compensatory routes / reproductive assurance  
all of these → reproductive function.

Under this structure, interaction quantity can remain correlated with function because both respond to the same disturbance and because interaction quantity genuinely contributes to reproduction. But it need not be a sufficient state variable because other branches can change independently.

## The two mismatch directions have different biological meanings

### False reassurance: interaction retained, function impaired

Observed I-nonlower / F-lower states can arise when fragmentation affects a branch not represented by interaction quantity:

- compatible-pollen fraction or donor provenance declines;
- post-pollination viability declines;
- plant condition/resource limitation reduces fruit or seed maturation;
- interaction counts are locally concentrated but mating quality deteriorates.

Wandoo provides a mechanistic clue: substantial pollen-tube arrival can coexist with poorer effective mating and reduced seed production.

### Apparent over-warning / buffering: interaction impaired, function retained

Observed I-lower / F-nonlower states can arise when alternative routes compensate:

- wind pollination supplements insect visitation;
- ambophily or apomixis bypasses part of the focal interaction route;
- selfing/geitonogamy provides short-term reproductive assurance;
- other pollinator guilds or rewiring maintain function.

Haloxylon, Lithraea and Rhododendron provide source-supported examples of such routes, although their long-term fitness consequences differ.

## Why average coupling can coexist with zero sentinel gain

If fragmentation raises the prior probability of reproductive impairment through several parallel routes, then female fitness can be lower whether the observed interaction indicator is lower or not.

That is exactly the corrected SF06 sign table:

- P(F lower) = 42/54 = 77.8%;
- P(F lower | I lower) = 35/44 = 79.5%;
- P(F lower | I nonlower) = 7/10 = 70.0%.

The upstream sign changes the conditional probability only modestly, so the optimal binary rule remains “predict F lower” for both I states.

This explains how a significant continuous association can coexist with zero incremental binary state-diagnostic gain.

## General ecological implication

The useful distinction is not “correlated versus uncorrelated”. It is:

- **response indicator:** a variable that tends to change with disturbance or function;
- **state sentinel:** a variable whose observed state materially changes the diagnosis of downstream functional impairment.

A response indicator can be valid without being a sufficient state sentinel.

For fragmentation monitoring, this means that demonstrating a significant pollination–fitness relationship is not enough to justify pollination quantity as a stand-alone indicator of reproductive condition.

## What is new here and what is not

Not new:

- flower visitation can be a poor proxy for pollinator effectiveness;
- plant reproduction is jointly pollen- and resource-limited;
- reproductive assurance and alternative pollination routes exist;
- ecological indicators require validation against what they are intended to represent.

The narrower empirical contribution is the conjunction:

> across matched and external fragmentation evidence, positive average coupling, recurrent bidirectional state mismatches, and zero incremental sign-level sentinel gain occur together.

That combination turns a familiar “proxy imperfection” warning into a directly auditable monitoring-sufficiency result.

## Claim ceiling

This is an interpretation of the empirical topology, not a causal mediation analysis.

The current data do not identify the relative causal contribution of provenance, viability, resource limitation or reproductive assurance across systems. The branching model therefore generates the next synchronized measurement design rather than establishing one universal mechanism.
