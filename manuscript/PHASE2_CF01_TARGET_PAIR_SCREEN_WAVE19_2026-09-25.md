## CFTQ0187 — Myrmecophila christinae

This is the only wave-19 candidate retained for the quantitative pathway.

The source surveys **14 orchid populations** in a fragmented coastal-shrub landscape and jointly
reports:

- fragment-size / habitat-disturbance context;
- pollen limitation from hand-pollination experiments;
- fruit production and fruit set;
- population structure and recruitment.

Under the frozen EGWEE definitions, pollen limitation is an eligible interaction-layer response and
fruit production / fruit set are reproductive-function responses.

The programme is **not** yet admitted, because the public abstract exposes two source habitat axes:

1. fragment area/size;
2. habitat disturbance / affectation.

Different reproductive responses are reported against those axes. EGWEE therefore freezes an
estimand gate before any numerical extraction:

- independent unit = population/site;
- maximum programme frame = 14 populations;
- choose one response-free fragmentation variable from full-text methods, not from effect strength;
- I = population-level pollen limitation under the source pollination experiment;
- F = one direct fruit/seed reproductive endpoint on the same population/exposure frame;
- individual flowers, plants, fruits and seeds remain nested below population.

No effect has been calculated.
