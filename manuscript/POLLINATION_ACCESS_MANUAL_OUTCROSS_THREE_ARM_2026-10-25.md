# Empirical EGWEE: open pollinator access versus manual outcross sufficiency

**2026-10-25 | EXPLORATORY, post hoc, same-source ecological extension.**
This report belongs to **egwee** (empirical, source-backed fragmentation/pollination research), NOT theoretical **egwe/NEE**. It neither supersedes the frozen manuscript nor increments its five direct / eight matched I–F / sixteen mixed-tier fragmentation programmes. The source results of GloPL (2018) and Sevenello et al. (2026) were known before this reanalysis; effects here were *not* prospectively preregistered.

## Central ecological question

**Is the reproductive output of a flower with pollinator access equivalent to the output of a flower from which pollinators are excluded but to which humans transfer outcross pollen?**

A negative result for additional pollen under open access (OPSupp−OP near zero) is **not equivalent to pollinator independence** (OP−Bag near zero). Nor does giving pollen to bagged flowers necessarily reproduce the outcomes of natural animal visitation. It is an empirical and conceptual three-arm question:

- **N**: unbagged, naturally pollinated control.
- **S**: unbagged naturally pollinated plus *hand-supplemented* pollen.
- **B**: experimentally bagged plus manually administered *outcross* pollen.

GloPL labels these \`Natural\`, \`Sup\` and \`Bagout\`; Sevenello labels the analogous (but **not identical**) conditions \`OP\`, \`OPSupp\` and \`BagSup\`. Sevenello additionally contains the *bagged without hand pollen* (\`Bag\`) condition.

The GloPL authors themselves define manual outcross of bagged flowers and note that bagging can alter flower microclimate. Sevenello originally reported decreases under insect exclusion and no significant overall natural-open versus pollen-supplemented-open difference. Consequently the **principle** of bagging artefacts, pollen limitation, and pollinator dependence is *prior art*, not an EGWEE discovery. See Bennett et al. (2018), [Scientific Data DOI 10.1038/sdata.2018.249](https://doi.org/10.1038/sdata.2018.249), and Sevenello, Walker & Mayfield (2026), [Landscape Ecology DOI 10.1007/s10980-026-02305-2](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10980-026-02305-2).

## 1. Global direction frequencies, independent publications explicitly separated

From **2,969 literature-defined GloPL cases / 927 publications / 1,265 species**, recover only experiments with published **Natural and Bagout means of the *same reproductive endpoint***. Positive below means **bagged manual outcross produced a larger *published mean*** than natural open pollination; not a statistically certified difference or an individual-level treatment counterfactual.

| Reproductive endpoint | Matched cases | Publications | Bagout > Natural | Natural > Bagout | Equal published means | Equal-publication fraction Bagout > Natural |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| **FS** Fruit set | **811** | **413** | **577** | **214** | 20 | **70.4%** (publication bootstrap [66.3%,74.5%]) |
| SO Seeds per ovule | 159 | 72 | 120 | 39 | 0 | 81.1% |
| **SPFR** Seeds per existing fruit | **193** | **109** | **130** | **63** | 0 | **65.5%** |
| **SPFL** Seeds per flower | **51** | **25** | **44** | **6** | 1 | **78.4%** (publication bootstrap [62.3%,92.0%]) |
| SPP Seeds per plant | 14 | 6 | 10 | 4 | 0 | 88.9% |

All five response types come from overlapping, response-selected parts of the original source dataset. It is invalid to treat them as independent experiments or to compare their percentages as if case/site/population denominators were constant.

For FS, the bagged-outcross-higher direction is seen in **315 of 413 publications**, while the natural-higher direction occurs in **137 publications** (these publication groups overlap when one paper reports several experiments). Even for SPFL, **6 original cases** have Natural > Bagout. Thus **neither intervention can be assumed to universally dominate the other**. The dominant GloPL direction is not evidence of a universal mechanism; studies with hand outcrossing are a selected and heterogeneous collection.

**Measurement robustness only:** A naive independent-arm mean/SD/N screen in the 811 FS cases has 783 available non-zero SEs, 640 nominal |z|≥1.96: 468 Bagout-higher and 172 Natural-higher, spanning multiple publications. These are not valid simultaneously resolved treatment differences; within-plant arm covariance, binomial boundedness, sampling units and study-level dependence are not identified by source table marginal summaries. Do not promote 172 cases as certified biological inhibition by hand pollen or bagging.

## 2. Within *the same GloPL experiment*: complete three-way rankings

**Fruit-set FS** permits the N, S and B published means to be compared within **45 cases from 20 publications**, not the whole set of 811 cases.

| Strict order, from lowest to highest mean fruit set | Cases |
|---|---:|
| Natural < Sup < Bagout | **16** |
| Natural < Bagout < Sup | **12** |
| Bagout < Natural < Sup | **4** |
| Bagout < Sup < Natural | **4** |
| Sup < Natural < Bagout | **5** |
| Sup < Bagout < Natural | **0** |
| At least one tie | **4** |

The **Natural > Bagout** ordering in this full three-arm sample is present in **11/45 cases across 4 different source publications and 8 plant families**; it is not confined to the Sevenello landscape. Most three-arm GloPL source experiments instead favour bagged manual outcross, so the sign cannot be exported from one habitat to another.

Seeds per **existing fruit** (SPFR) permit a different three-arm comparison, **27 cases from 12 publications**. **All six strict rankings are represented**:

- \`N<S<B\` 12 cases, \`S<N<B\` 8, \`B<N<S\` 2, \`S<B<N\` 2, \`B<S<N\` 1, \`N<B<S\` 1; one tie.

The six-order result is a topology of **observed source means**, not six statistically established reproductive strategies. When one strict order is represented by one case, sampling error can easily alter the apparent ranking.

## 3. The key interaction: delivery method × measured reproductive stage

In **22 same-source GloPL cases from eight publications**, all **six means** are available: fruit-set FS and seeds-per-existing-fruit SPFR, each under N, S and B. Assess whether the sign of the treatment contrast versus Natural is *identical for both reproductive stages*:

| Case classifications | Cases |
|---|---:|
| FS and SPFR concordant for both bagged and open-supplemented contrasts | 7 |
| Discordant for both contrasts | 2 |
| Discordant **only for bagged manual** (B−N) | **4** |
| Discordant **only for open supplemental** (S−N) | **9** |

**13/22 cases** (from seven publications, eight families) change the sign-concordance classification depending on *how* artificial pollen is administered. A diagnostic excluding **all four source-mean zero ties** leaves **10/18** discordant in only one pollen-delivery method (six publications, five families). This is a useful **intervention-by-stage ecological hypothesis** grounded in fully matched observations; it is not an identified causal interaction effect because FS and SPFR are different biological denominators, the source pairs share maternal sampling, and the published means lack covariance and causal treatment equivalence. The study sample is far too small for a general incidence claim.

## 4. Independent 2026 Sevenello field experiment points the other way

Sevenello consists of 520 quadrats with **four distinct treatment plants each**. Its source-verified complete four-arm per-flower comparison is possible for 381 quadrats covering three taxa, averaged within transects, then Edge/Core × crop, and then giving each **physical reserve** equal weight:

| Species | Four-arm, measured-flower quadrats | Distinct physical reserves | Natural open − bagged manual seeds **per flower** | Reserves with Natural > BagSup |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| *Lawrencella rosea* | 156 | 6 | **+3.229** | 6/6 |
| *Podolepis aristata* | 111 | 4 | **+17.774** | 4/4 |
| *Podotheca gnaphaloides* | 114 | 6 | **+24.271** | 6/6 |

All **16 species×reserve means** are positive. *Goodenia rosea* was not entered in the per-flower comparison because original source flower denominators are unavailable; no imputation.

Contrast this **single independent source's 16 non-independent species×reserve cells** with the different-source GloPL SPFL result, where bagged manual outcross has higher mean seeds/flower in **44/51 cases from 25 publications**. The opposing directional topologies are real in their respective *recorded mean data*, but cannot be pooled, because pollen donor identity, dose/frequency, microclimate/physical bagging, source ecology and design differ. Sevenello applies supplemental conspecific pollen to bagged plants during flowering; it does not experimentally guarantee the same compatible pollen receipt/timing as open pollinator visits.

The Sevenello source's original published results **already showed** natural-open reproductive advantage under insect exclusion: the incremental EGWEE contribution is the equal-reserve and individual-flower-denominator audit, and its comparison against a genuinely separate multistudy source. This is a counterexample to **universally treating bagged hand pollination as a perfect substitute for access to natural pollinators**—not proof that beneficial fauna supply an additional necessary nonpollen service.

## 5. Why this is potentially important for general island/fragmentation ecology

The registered EGWEE result is that upstream pollination **quantity** does not universally identify female reproductive output in fragmented populations. The two source-external experimental programmes refine the *mechanism candidates* rather than confirm a single one:

1. **Pollen amount/sufficiency** is not the same estimand as **access to pollinators**; zero incremental supplementation response can coexist with a large exclusion penalty.
2. **Delivered pollen provenance/compatibility** and pollination visit sequence may differ between human and animal transfer, but **none of the pooled GloPL or Sevenello datasets directly prove pollen provenance as the active mediator**.
3. **Bag microclimate, temporal dose, developmental resources, and floral display** are mutually plausible alternatives. The comparison of **a few source-mean endpoints** must not be presented as factorial mechanistic identification.
4. **The identity and timing of the measured reproductive stage** changes the apparent ranking even within the same source experiment. This is worth testing with signed *synchronized* I, mating quality/provenance and F endpoints before inferring non-equivalence of causal mechanism.

**Genuinely discriminating future field experiment:** replicate independent island/mainland or fragment/core units; assign flower branches to natural-open, open+standardized known-donor pollen, bag+standardized donor, bag+sham handling or breathable sham bag, and bag-only; record flower production, target pollen-tube counts, compatible pollen, genetic outcross paternity, fruit initiation and seeds/flower with repeated staging. Randomize treatment at appropriate independent maternal unit; synchronize donor, dose and application timing across open+hand and bag+hand. Check whether a source-specific Natural−BaggedHand reproductive gap remains after effective pollen and microclimate are controlled.
