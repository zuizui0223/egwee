# CFTQ0315 Myrtus communis population-level I-F recovery stop — 2026-09-25

## Frozen candidate

Programme: `P2_CF01_MYRTUS_2009`.

Primary source: González-Varo et al. (2009), *Biological Conservation* 142:1058–1065,
doi:10.1016/j.biocon.2009.01.017.

The source compares six populations in a fragmented Mediterranean-forest landscape:

- Large: DBL, CHP, DHY;
- Small: GBL, CRB, PTR.

Independent unit = **population/site**, n=6 maximum.

## I layer is recoverable

Table 2 reports total insect visitation rates for all six populations:

- DBL = 30.5 visits h⁻¹;
- CHP = 41.0;
- DHY = 27.6;
- GBL = 63.5;
- CRB = 86.6;
- PTR = 26.4.

These are population-level source summaries and satisfy the frozen broad-I endpoint better than
honeybee-only or native-bee-only alternatives.

## F / pollen-limitation effect-unit problem

Natural fruit set and pollen limitation were measured on individual shrubs.

The public source reports:

- natural fruit set in Large patches = 53% ± 19%, n=21 plants;
- natural fruit set in Small patches = 58% ± 19%, n=19 plants;
- pollen-limitation indices calculated for individual plants from control versus xenogamy treatments;
- GLMMs with patch size as fixed effect and population as random effect;
- Figure 4 as Large-versus-Small aggregate pollen-limitation bars;
- Figure 5 as shrub-level pollen-limitation versus visitation relationships.

It does **not** expose:

- six population-level natural fruit-set values;
- six population-level pollen-limitation values;
- population-level F/PL dispersion for the three Large versus three Small populations.

Therefore the published plant-level n and SD cannot be attached to an n=3 versus n=3 fragmentation
effect.

## Decision

Status:
`blocked_population_level_F_or_PL_vector_not_publicly_recoverable`.

The broad I endpoint is source-recoverable, but the second layer is not effect-unit-valid at the
population level. No direct I-F Hedges-g programme is admitted.

This is a recoverability limitation, not evidence of I-F equivalence or absence of fragmentation
effects.

## Reopening condition

Reopen only if an authoritative source or author-provided dataset supplies, for the same six
populations:

1. natural fruit set or another already-permitted direct F quantity at population level; or
2. a source-wide population-level pollen-limitation endpoint under a frozen layer rule;
3. population-level dispersion or paired population values sufficient for valid marginal effects.

## No rescue

Do not:

- use 39–42 individual plants as fragmentation n;
- use plant-level SD with n=3 populations per class;
- digitize Figure 4 or Figure 5 to manufacture population values;
- replace broad total visitation with honeybee visitation because the Large/Small contrast is larger;
- choose natural fruit set, brood size or seed mass by significance;
- interpret the small class-level fruit-set difference as equivalence;
- treat this programme as validation of an EGWE/NEE finite operator.

All terminal outcomes are retained.
