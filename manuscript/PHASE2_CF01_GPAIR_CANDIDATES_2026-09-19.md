# CF01 G_adult–G_offspring candidate register — 2026-09-19

## CF01_GPAIR_001 — Afrocarpus gracilior

Source: Abate et al. 2024, *Forest Ecology and Management* 572:122256, doi:10.1016/j.foreco.2024.122256.

This candidate was found through citation expansion after the G_adult–G_offspring family became the nearest preregistered Phase-2 opening gate.

### Design evidence

- 11 populations/sites were sampled genetically.
- Adult and progeny samples are both available at 9 of those sites.
- The study uses source-defined population-size/fragmentation classes: large/intact, intermediate, and small/isolated.
- The linked 2025 paper confirms that individual-population genetic values are reported in the 2024 companion study.
- The current repository contains no prior Afrocarpus programme, so this is not a duplicate of ML001–ML020.

### Current gate

The candidate is **registered but not quantitatively opened**.

Before calculating any effect:

1. recover the exact nine common adult/progeny population identities;
2. recover population-specific adult and progeny uHe on the same frame;
3. retain population as the independent unit;
4. freeze the direct fragmentation contrast or gradient from the source exposure geometry before calculating Hedges-g/Fisher-z;
5. reconstruct within-programme G_adult/G_offspring covariance from paired population values;
6. reject rather than rescue if there are fewer than two independent units in a required direct-comparison group or if a gradient has insufficient finite sampling variance.

The 2025 category-level means are **not** used as independent replicates.

### Why this matters

If Brosimum ML002 is successfully upgraded into the G_adult–G_offspring family, current coverage would become 4/5. Afrocarpus is therefore a prospective fifth-programme candidate, but it is not counted until its population-level effect-unit gate passes.
