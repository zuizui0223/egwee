## Myrtus audit

*Myrtus communis* remains an excellent **design** example.

The source-defined six-population frame is:

- Large: DBL, CHP, DHY;
- Small: GBL, CRB, PTR.

Population-level total visitation is tabulated for all six populations. Natural/control fruit set, however, is reported in text as habitat-level plant summaries, while exact 2007 population-specific fruit-set values are only shown graphically in the companion material.

The current contract therefore forbids two shortcuts:

1. treating plant-level SD/SE as between-population dispersion while using `n=3` patches per group;
2. digitizing plotted bars opportunistically after seeing the candidate.

Myrtus is consequently **closed for direct quantitative I-F admission under the current source-recovery route**, while remaining in the systematic/design evidence ledger.

## Caragana and Haloxylon audit

Both studies explicitly replicate six fragmented and six natural plots. That solves the independent-unit **count**, but not automatically the standardising dispersion.

Until the accessible source establishes that the reported SD/SE for the locked I and F endpoints is dispersion across those six plots, no Hedges-g effect is calculated. Nested flowers, plants, visits, fruits or repeated observations cannot be combined with `n=6` as if they were plot-level SD.
