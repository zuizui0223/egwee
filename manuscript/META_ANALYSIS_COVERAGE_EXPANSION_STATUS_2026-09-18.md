> **from “is there evidence against complete layer exchangeability?” to “how large are repeated cross-layer contrasts, and which biological conditions structure concordant versus decoupled fragmentation responses?”**

Primary development outputs are:

- layer-specific fragmentation effects;
- repeated (I-F), (C-F), (G_{adult}-G_{offspring}), and conditional (G_{adult}-mean(I,F)) contrasts;
- cohort/history lag where replicated;
- process–function coupling where replicated;
- predeclared moderator interactions only after coverage gates are met.

## Quantitative gates

- pair-specific meta-analysis: >=5 independent programme/study clusters for that pair;
- categorical moderator interaction: >=10 independent clusters overall and >=4 per modeled category;
- adult-vs-offspring cohort-lag meta-analysis: >=5 same-exposure clusters;
- continuous moderator interaction: >=10 independent clusters plus dispersion safeguards.

Until a gate is met, the corresponding hypothesis stays descriptive.
