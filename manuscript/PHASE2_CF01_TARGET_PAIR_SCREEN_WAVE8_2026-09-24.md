## Advance

### CFTQ0078 — Banksia nivea subsp. uliginosa

The source explicitly investigates fragmentation and reproductive biology in nine populations differing in population size and habitat context. Pollinator observations and direct reproductive-function measurements occur within the same population programme.

Full-text admission is not automatic. The next gate must establish:

1. the exact source-defined fragmentation/habitat-context exposure before any outcome calculation;
2. whether population is the independent fragmentation unit;
3. whether pollinator visitation and fruit set can form a matched I-F pair on that same unit/frame;
4. whether any C endpoint is actually measured in this paper rather than imported from prior genetic work;
5. recoverable summary dispersion or raw data sufficient for an EGWEE effect.

No numerical EGWEE effect has been calculated.

## Wave-8 closures

- **CFTQ0071 Foetidia:** strong gene-flow/mating-system evidence in fragmented forest, but no direct fecundity F endpoint and no common replicated fragmentation pair frame.
- **CFTQ0072 reintroduction experiment:** connected/unconnected habitat is the experimental exposure, not a biological C response; F/demography is measured but C-F is not.
- **CFTQ0073 Himantoglossum:** the causal treatment is pollen transfer/outbreeding, while fragmentation/isolation is background.
- **CFTQ0074 Gomortega:** reproduction and dispersal are characterized within one fragmented landscape without a common habitat-fragmentation contrast across C and F.
- **CFTQ0075 Atlantic-forest fruit removal:** source-defined forest-amount gradient and direct dispersal response, but no direct fecundity F endpoint.
- **CFTQ0076 Camellia pubipetala:** two isolated populations provide reproductive-ecology evidence but no replicated fragmentation severity/reference frame.
- **CFTQ0077 bird-pollination review:** non-primary review.
- **CFTQ0079 Erythrophleum:** adult genetic structure/diversity only; no direct I-F or C-F pair.
- **CFTQ0080 Trillium:** recruitment/age-structure study in secondary forest without a source-defined fragmentation exposure.
