## Advance

### CFTQ0135 — Comarum palustre multilevel spatial structure

The source follows 14 Belgian wetland-fragment populations for three years and jointly measures:

- source-defined population/landscape spatial structure;
- pollinator abundance and visitation;
- pollen limitation;
- open-pollinated viable seed set.

EGWEE retains this as a **gradient/generalisation I-F candidate**. The frozen contract fixes:

- independent unit = population/site, not the 15 insect-observation plots within each population;
- primary exposure = the source 500-m landscape isolation / woody-area-cover variable;
- primary I = total direct floral visitation rate;
- primary F = open-pollinated viable seed set;
- repeated years aggregated within population rather than counted as new fragmentation replicates;
- population surface, edge closure, flower density and taxon-specific pollinator responses remain
  secondary and cannot replace the primary variables after outcomes are inspected.

No EGWEE effect has been calculated.
