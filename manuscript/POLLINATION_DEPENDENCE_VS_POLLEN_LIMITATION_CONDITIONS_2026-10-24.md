The new raw-data source-level reanalyses below **do not alter** the frozen 5 direct fragmentation clusters, 8 quantitative I–F programmes, 16 mixed-tier translation programmes, or SF06 source denominators. ## Ecological question

Under what **measurable ecological conditions** is a quantity-like pollination indicator insufficient for reproductive function? Distinguish four concepts that cannot be equated simply because they all involve pollen:

1. **Pollinator access/dependence:** flowers exposed to natural visiting pollinators versus experimentally bagged flowers.
2. **Supplementary pollen response:** adding hand-delivered pollen when flowers already have free natural pollinator access.
3. **Bagged hand-supplementation potential:** hand-delivering pollen to pollinator-excluded plants, retaining a bagging effect.
4. **Reproductive-stage readout:** fruit set, seeds per fruit, seeds per flower or plant, fruit mass, viability and final recruitment.

The same intervention may have different consequences on these axes without invoking a universal unobserved post-pollination bottleneck.

## 1. Sevenello: pollinator access versus additional pollen within the same quadrats

**Original source:** Sevenello, Walker & Mayfield (2026), [*Landscape Ecology*, doi:10.1007/s10980-026-02305-2](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10980-026-02305-2), supplementary public Figshare [10.6084/m9.figshare.31072189](https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.31072189) (CC BY 4.0).

**Prior art ceiling:** The paper explicitly reported that bagged treatments had lower seed output than open treatments in all four species; it also reported that open versus open+supplemented treatment means were not significantly different at the species level. This pattern itself was discovered by the source authors. Our additional question is *whether the treatment-response topology persists across distinct reserves and after normalizing by the treated plant's own flowering display*, not a claim to have discovered pollinator dependence.

**Original experiment and hierarchical unit:** Source 2,080 separate treatment plants in 520 quadrats, four **different plants** (OP, OPSupp, Bag, BagSup) per quadrat. The study covered nine reserves / eleven independently sampled remnant areas, with two reserves providing independent transect pairs adjacent to two crops. Those two crop boundaries are **not two physically independent reserves**; give the source \`site\` unit equal weight after averaging Edge/Core transects, then distinct crop-side blocks within a reserve. These are repeated species or plots within **one experiment and landscape**, not 21 independent replication studies.

For the four-arm complete case analysis there are **499 quadrats**: GORO 117, LARO 156, POAR 112, POGN 114. Only those with **all four seed totals** are used, while the earlier preregistered OP vs OPSupp primary pair sample remains unchanged. Source \`BagSup\` involved supplementary pollen applied every four to five days, not unlimited pollen supply or a standardized pollen-compatibility dose.

### Source-consistent total-seed response

The units below are equal-*physical reserve* means of seeds **per treated plant**. Letters are original author codes: **GORO *Goodenia rosea***, **LARO *Lawrencella rosea***, **POAR *Podolepis aristata***, **POGN *Podotheca gnaphaloides***.

| Species | Number of reserves | OP−Bag | BagSup−Bag | OP−BagSup | OPSupp−OP |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| GORO | 5 | **+22.258** | +9.691 | +12.567 | +9.022 |
| LARO | 6 | **+17.471** | +8.701 | +8.770 | +1.771 |
| POAR | 4 | **+372.668** | +79.060 | +293.608 | +10.499 |
| POGN | 6 | **+188.026** | +54.533 | +133.493 | +42.665 |

Every one of the **21 observed species × physical-reserve cells** has positive OP−Bag, BagSup−Bag and OP−BagSup *mean contrasts* (21/21 three times). This establishes a descriptive rank order of source treatment means under the explicitly declared reserve weighting:

**Open natural pollination > Bagged + manual pollen > Bagged, at all observed species×reserve cells.**

By comparison, in the same complete-case reserve analysis, further hand-supplementing **already open** plants (OPSupp−OP) changes sign among reserves for GORO (3 positive/2 negative), LARO (4/2), POAR (3/1); POGN has 6/6 positive. Species-specific small-reserve exact sign-flip p values for OP−Bag are 0.0625, 0.03125, 0.125, 0.03125; **not four statistically independent confirmed ecological laws**, and no multiplicity-adjusted pooled cross-species p is licensed.

The algebraic identity **OP−Bag = (OP−BagSup)+(BagSup−Bag)** holds separately at every complete quadrat and under the site averaging. In this particular study, supplemental pollen and pollinator access explain different observable treatment contrasts. No externally universal contribution of pollinators beyond pollen itself can be inferred: bagging may change temperature/humidity; plant vigour/flower number is not randomized at the exact individual-counterfactual level; repeated rubbing/manual pollen may change damage or timing and may not reproduce animal pollen provenance or quantity.

### Critical flower-denominator sensitivity

One can confuse higher individual seed output with higher pollen fertilization per flower when *different treatment plants* have different flowering displays. We therefore repeated the **entire treatment hierarchy within each source quadrat using seeds per each plant's own positive observed flower count**. Complete four-arm flower-denominator cases: **LARO 156, POAR 111, POGN 114**, totaling **381**. GORO does **not** have valid per-flower counts due to explosive dehiscence, and is not silently treated as zero.

| Species | Physical reserves | OP−Bag (seeds/flower) | BagSup−Bag | OP−BagSup | Number of reserves with all three positive |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| LARO | 6 | **+7.425** | +4.196 | +3.229 | **6/6** |
| POAR | 4 | **+24.775** | +7.002 | +17.774 | **4/4** |
| POGN | 6 | **+36.558** | +12.286 | +24.271 | **6/6** |

Thus the ordering persists in **16/16** eligible species×reserve cells when the denominator is observed flower count, rather than only total individual display. This rules out a *purely total-flower-count arithmetic explanation* for the observed ordering, **not** a bag microclimate, time/dose, fertility or treatment-plant-confounding explanation. Pollen quality/provenance is not measured; the experiment does not determine which of those competing mechanisms matters.

The nuanced ecological result for EGWEE is therefore **standing pollinator-access dependence ≠ incremental pollen limitation under open visitation**. A small extra-pollen benefit cannot certify negligible pollinator dependence, while increased pollen delivery cannot by itself certify the availability of the full open-pollination reproductive context. This narrower paired-comparison statement is useful, even though the individual source findings are already prior art.

## 2. Global GloPL and crop PolLimCrop: natural fruit set is not an independent ecological moderator

Separate source-cited reanalysis used [GloPL](https://doi.org/10.5061/dryad.dt437), wild source-level FS (fruit set) vs SPFR (seeds per existing fruit), **313 eligible paired nonzero cases / 84 publications**, and [PolLimCrop](https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.24625299.v1), crop FS vs reported SN (seed number), **300 cases / 72 article codes**. These are **not identical biological denominators and must not be pooled**. The original compilation articles documented endpoint dependence; that concept is prior art.

Post hoc moderator test: within the **same source publication** (source-centered unweighted linear-probability slope), relate *natural-treatment baseline fruit set* to (a) whether added-pollen fruit set itself declines, and (b) whether fruit-set and downstream seed-response signs disagree.

| Same-case source study frame | Baseline-FS slope for a decline in FS after supplementation, per 0→1 FS | Baseline-FS slope for two-stage disagreement, per 0→1 FS |
|---|---:|---:|
| GloPL wild | +0.487 | +0.352 |
| PolLimCrop crop | +0.490 | +0.067 |
| Crop without *Malus domestica* | +0.611 | −0.385 |
| Crop without Rosaceae | +0.635 | −0.400 |

The wild stage-disagreement slope has a **borderline positive** publication bootstrap 95% interval **[+0.006,+0.695]** under the fixed computation seed, but its flower-only and whole-plant supplementation strata individually have intervals spanning zero. Crop stage-disagreement slope interval **[−0.520,+0.647]**, and the crop point sign reverses on removal of heavily represented apple/Rosaceae. Thus a generally transferable baseline-FS stage-disagreement moderator is **not** supported.

Moreover, **natural fruit-set baseline enters the definition of the observed fruit-set response difference itself**, \(\Delta FS = FS_{\rm supplemented}-FS_{\rm natural}\). Higher control fruit set mechanically leaves less room for positive supplement response, and even a control-value measurement error can induce a negative relationship between baseline and change. A deliberately artificial *within-publication shuffling of supplemental-FS source case pairings* creates a much steeper apparent FS-decline/baseline relationship than the actual paired data; this is a falsification of interpreting the simple positive slope as a new ecological saturation mechanism, **not** a randomization test of field pollen effects. PolLimCrop includes some source FS records ≤1 on an otherwise 0–100 reported scale, so an explicit separate exclusion checks ambiguous percentage/proportion representations.

### Source-level endpoint co-movement null

Fix the original FS and downstream positive/negative marginal sign counts **within each source publication** and randomly shuffle the identity of the downstream sign among that publication's cases. This tests whether the particular cases with fruit-set decline are also especially likely to show downstream change, *beyond that publication's sign mix*. It does **not** randomize plant or experiment treatments.

| Fixed source-level frame | Actual discordant cases | Expected by within-publication sign re-pairing |
|---|---:|---:|
| Wild FS/SPFR | **81/313 (25.9%)** | **90.16/313 (28.8%)** |
| Crop FS/SN | **75/300 (25.0%)** | **86.50/300 (28.8%)** |

In both sources, actual cases are somewhat more concordant than independent sign pairings within the same publication would predict. However, publication-equal bootstrap **gain** intervals straddle zero, so it is premature to claim a general strong law of reproductive-stage synchrony; the crop permutation signal is concentrated in cases from apple-rich sources and weakens after omission. Published positive average cross-endpoint correlations already constitute relevant prior art.

The more precise EGWEE state is: **reproductive indicators can disagree, but raw discordance is not necessarily a resource-limitation phenotype; endpoint boundedness, measurement error and study composition require explicit competing diagnostics**.

## 3. Research consequence and evidence hierarchy

1. **Strongest existing fragmentation-specific result (unchanged):** 3 independent landscape programmes with downstream interaction–function mismatch; no established single cross-system cause.
2. **New exploratory internal treatment topology (one source):** 4-plant × 4-treatment Sevenello comparison shows robust site-level ordering OP > BagSup > Bag, including per-flower in all 16 eligible site×species cells; diagnostic of how different pollination questions lead to different reproductive answers, not new pollinator-dependence discovery.
3. **External generality (two source compilations):** 81/313 wild and 75/300 crop mean-sign discrepancies persist across multiple publications, with compatible relative frequencies but non-equivalent outcome denominators and uncertain effect-sign resolution. Natural baseline FS does **not** provide a source-independent transferable causal moderator.
4. **Prospective mechanistic test:** independent remnant landscapes with sham bags matching bag microclimate/handling, standardized supplemental pollen dose and provenance, baseline floral display, pollen tubes/compatible pollen/outcross parentage, resource context, fruit establishment, seeds per fruit and viable recruits. Test **intervention × environment × reproductive stage** with reserve-level replication. Predefine exposure orientation, denominator, effect scale and dependence covariance before observing outcomes.

Neither the global case counts nor the 21 cells are independent new quantitative confirmations for the registered fragmentation meta-analysis. These analyses do not justify replacing the submission paper's scale-robust headline or modifying its five/eight/sixteen source denominators.
