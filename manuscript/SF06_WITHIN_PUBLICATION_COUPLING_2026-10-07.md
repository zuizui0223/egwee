# SF06 within-publication coupling audit — 2026-10-07

## Status

Post-exposure descriptive robustness audit. Only two source publications contribute more than one habitat-fragmentation pollination–female-fitness species stratum, so this is not a broad inferential test.

## Question

Could the positive pollination–female-fitness relationship arise only from differences **between** source publications?

The corrected public-S1 habitat-fragmentation subset contains 55 paired strata from 33 publications. Only two publications contain multiple paired species:

- Aguilar (2005) PhD thesis — 9 species;
- Aizen & Feinsinger (1994) — 15 species.

For those publications we estimate the simple within-publication slope after subtracting each publication's mean pollination and female-fitness effect.

## Result

### Aguilar (2005)

- n = 9 species;
- within-publication slope = **+0.596**;
- within-publication Pearson correlation = **+0.414**.

### Aizen & Feinsinger (1994)

- n = 15 species;
- within-publication slope = **+0.157**;
- within-publication Pearson correlation = **+0.319**.

### Pooled within-publication de-meaned slope

Across the 24 species in those two multi-species publications:

- pooled within-publication slope = **+0.239**.

For comparison:

- simple slope across all 55 fragmentation pairs = **+0.220**;
- slope among the 33 publication means = **+0.243**.

## Interpretation

The positive coupling in the external source is not obviously produced only by between-publication differences: both available multi-species publications show positive within-publication relationships, and the pooled within slope is close to the overall and between-publication slopes.

This strengthens the “tracks” part of the paper's title while leaving the sentinel conclusion unchanged. Positive within-publication coupling can coexist with many-to-many sign translation and weak incremental prediction.

## Claim ceiling

Allowed:

- positive coupling is visible within both multi-species publications available in the paired public-S1 subset;
- the overall positive relationship is not obviously a pure between-publication artifact.

Not allowed:

- two publications establish a universal within-study coupling law;
- the within slope is a cluster-robust confirmatory estimate;
- within-publication demeaning proves a causal pollination effect on female fitness.
