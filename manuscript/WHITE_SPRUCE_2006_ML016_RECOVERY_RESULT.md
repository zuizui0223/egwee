# ML016 / PS021 white spruce 2006 recovery result

## Terminal state

`reproductive_stand_vector_not_reconstructable`

ML016 is **not admitted** as a fifth primary multilayer cluster. The two 2006 papers do share the same 1994, 23-stand programme and the mating layer is recoverable at the stand level, but the locked reproductive-function endpoint is not available in an effect-unit-valid numerical representation.

## Source-programme identity

The two source papers use the same northern-Ontario fragmented agricultural landscape and the same cone collection:

- 23 natural white-spruce stands;
- 104 maternal trees;
- cones collected August–September 1994;
- source-defined stand-size classes: 11 small (`1–<10` trees), 6 medium (`10–<100`), 6 large (`>=100`).

The recovery contract fixed small stands as fragmented and large stands as reference before stand-level reproductive values were opened. Medium stands remain source context only.

## Recovered G_mating layer

Heredity Table 1 exposes one stand-level multilocus outcrossing estimate `t_m` for every stand. On the locked small-versus-large frame:

- small: `n=11`, mean `90.36363636`, SD `0.77106774`;
- large: `n=6`, mean `91.31666667`, SD `1.02453241`;
- fragmented-minus-reference Hedges `g = -1.04712811`;
- canonical metafor-LS variance `0.28982509`.

Higher `t_m` means greater mating/outcrossing support, so no sign reversal is required. The negative effect indicates somewhat lower stand-level outcrossing support in the small stands.

This effect is retained as **descriptive single-layer evidence only** because the prospectively locked F companion layer failed its representation gate.

## Locked F layer and blocker

The reproductive-success paper reports that:

- filled seeds per cone increase with stand size;
- trees from small stands set about 38% fewer filled seeds per cone than trees from large stands;
- Figure 2 plots the mean number of filled seeds per cone for the 23 stands against stand size.

However, the accessible public representation does not provide the exact 23 stand-level filled-seed values or a compatible small-versus-large stand-level mean and dispersion from which the frozen Hedges-g estimand can be reconstructed.

The paper's fitted regression equation and Figure 2 are **not** substitutes for observed stand values. The recovery therefore stops rather than:

- digitizing the figure;
- generating pseudo-observations from the regression;
- using 102/104 maternal trees as fragmentation replicates;
- switching to total seeds, empty-seed percentage, germination or another easier endpoint.

## Dependence consequence

Because the F stand vector is unavailable, the predeclared group-centered G_mating/F stand-level covariance cannot be reconstructed. Covariance is not set to zero and no fifth cluster is manufactured from one recoverable layer.
