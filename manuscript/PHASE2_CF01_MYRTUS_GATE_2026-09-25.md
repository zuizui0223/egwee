# CFTQ0315 Myrtus communis direct I-F gate — 2026-09-25

## Programme

`P2_CF01_MYRTUS_2009`.

Primary source: González-Varo, Arroyo & Aparicio et al. (2009), *Biological Conservation*
142:1058–1065, doi:10.1016/j.biocon.2009.01.017.

The study contains **six source populations** classified as Large versus Small in a highly fragmented
Mediterranean-forest landscape.

## Independent unit

Independent unit = **population/site**, maximum n=6.

Plants, visitor observations, flowers, pollen-addition treatments, fruits and seeds are nested below
population.

## Locked exposure

Primary direct contrast candidate = source Large versus Small population/patch class.

The full text must confirm the class definition and whether population size and habitat patch size
are the same source variable or separate correlated descriptors. Honeybee-hive presence is context/
moderator and cannot be used to redefine fragmentation after results are seen.

## Locked I

Primary I is frozen prospectively for recovery as the **broad population-level total visitation rate**
if the six values are available.

If only pollen-limitation is recoverable at population level, it may serve as the direct I endpoint
only under a documented source-wide rule fixed before calculating its effect.

Honeybee-only or native-bee-only responses are sensitivities, not selectable primary endpoints.

## Locked F

Primary F = natural reproductive function on the same six populations.

Preference order fixed before numeric recovery:

1. natural fruit set if population-level values are available;
2. otherwise the source primary seed-production/brood-size response on the common frame.

Seed mass remains secondary.

## Full-source recovery audit

The open manuscript makes the interaction layer unusually transparent.

### I is recoverable at population level

Table 2 reports total insect visitation rate (`V/H`, visits/hour) separately for all six source
populations:

- Large: DBL, CHP, DHY;
- Small: GBL, CRB, PTR.

The source analysis treats population as a random effect for the Large-versus-Small visitation
comparison. This is compatible with the population/site independent-unit contract.

### F is not exposed on the same numeric frame

The reproductive analyses remain nested below population:

- pollen limitation uses 6–8 plants per population and natural versus xenogamous flowers within
  plant;
- seed production samples 12–14 plants per population and 20 fruits per plant;
- Figure 4 reports Large/Small pollen-limitation summaries;
- Figure 5 shows the six population pollen-limitation points against visitation, but pollen
  limitation is an I-layer response rather than direct natural F;
- Figure 6 reports Large/Small brood-size and seed-mass summaries.

The public manuscript does not provide a table of six population-level natural fruit-set or
brood-size values with population-level marginal dispersion.

Therefore a valid direct F marginal cannot be constructed without either digitizing figures or
pairing lower-level plant/model uncertainty with population `n=3+3`.

## Decision

Status:
`blocked_population_level_F_or_PL_vector_not_publicly_recoverable`.

The programme remains biologically valuable: pollinator assemblages and visitation shift strongly
between Large and Small patches, and visitation covaries with pollen limitation. But the frozen
direct I-F family requires both I and F marginals at the same legitimate population effect unit.

Direct I-F programme increment: **0**.

## Reopening condition

Reopen only if an authoritative public or author-provided source supplies population-level natural
fruit set or the source primary seed-production response for the same six populations together with
population-level marginal variance or paired raw population values.

## Admission

Admit only if both marginal effects correspond to the six population units and their variance is
population-level. If paired population values exist, reconstruct covariance; otherwise the
pre-existing cluster-robust fallback may be used only after both marginals are effect-unit-valid.

## No rescue

Do not:

- use flowers or plants as n;
- choose honeybee visitation because its fragmentation contrast is larger;
- choose brood size versus fruit set by significance;
- treat beekeeping as the fragmentation treatment;
- count multiple I/F endpoints as separate programmes.

All terminal outcomes are retained.
