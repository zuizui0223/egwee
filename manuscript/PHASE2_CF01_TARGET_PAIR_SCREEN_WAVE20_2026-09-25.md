## Most informative closure

### CFTQ0196 — fragmentation, seed dispersal and seedling establishment

Herrera & García quantify a clear fragmentation pathway in two ornithochorous trees:

- habitat fragmentation alters seed-deposition probability;
- bird-mediated seed deposition is a direct movement/connectivity process;
- seedling establishment depends on seed arrival for one species and is obscured by post-dispersal mortality in the other.

This is strong **C → D** evidence: movement/connectivity feeds recruitment/demography.

It is not a C-F pair under the frozen EGWEE layer contract because seedling establishment is
`D_resource_demography`, not `F_reproductive_function`. EGWEE therefore does not relabel
recruitment as reproductive function merely to enlarge C-F coverage.

Decision: retain as a stage-coupling/process anchor; direct C-F increment = **0**.
