## Advances

### CFTQ0084 — western-European landscape configuration

The source samples 229 agricultural landscapes across four western-European regions and uses source-defined field-border density as configurational heterogeneity. Wild-pollinator abundance/diversity and sentinel radish seed set are measured in the same landscape programme.

This is a candidate for the registered **gradient/generalisation I-F stream**, not a binary fragmented-versus-reference Hedges-g programme. Full-text/data recovery must lock:

1. the independent landscape unit and country/region hierarchy;
2. one I endpoint before numerical extraction;
3. radish seed set as F;
4. the field-border-density exposure;
5. a common landscape frame and covariance-aware representation.

Associated data are registered at Figshare (collection DOI 10.6084/m9.figshare.c.3994269). No numerical EGWEE effect has been calculated.

### CFTQ0088 — Aloe thraskii population isolation

The source measures bird/bee visitation and fecundity across multiple populations varying in population size, density and isolation distance.

The unresolved question is whether the source's isolation metric represents an eligible habitat/remnant-fragmentation exposure or only the spacing of transient coastal-dune populations. Under the ambiguity rule it advances to full-text clarification rather than being admitted or excluded from the gradient stream.

## Wave-9 closures

- **CFTQ0081 Echinacea pollinator effectiveness:** pollinator taxon/effectiveness and pollen movement are the causal frame; fragmentation is background.
- **CFTQ0082 Aechmea mariae-reginae:** reproductive biology and the six-population genetic survey do not provide a common measured fragmentation I-F frame.
- **CFTQ0083 Dicliptera:** one forest fragment only.
- **CFTQ0085 Anogeissus:** the source-defined predictor is within-population conspecific density/distribution, not habitat fragmentation.
- **CFTQ0086 Los Tuxtlas connectivity indicators:** integrative landscape synthesis drawing on prior process/species studies, not one new primary common I-F effect unit.
- **CFTQ0087 Cycas megacarpa:** broad historical-fragmentation C/G/demographic evidence but no direct reproductive-function F endpoint for C-F.
- **CFTQ0089 Echinacea phenology:** source-defined axis is flowering date/season; remnant fragmentation is background.
- **CFTQ0090 Bomarea salsilla:** one site per habitat category; plants and factorial exclusion treatments are nested below site and cannot create independent fragmentation replication.
