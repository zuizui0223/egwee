## Question

Does the complete 12-programme process–function census retain evidence for both resolved mismatch directions after omitting any one programme?

## Full census

The complete census contains 12 independent programmes. Eleven are pair-testable and four resolve a process–F mismatch:

- downstream F-dominant: 3;
- upstream process-dominant: 1 (*Serapias lingua*).

Thus the full census contains resolved mismatches in both directions.

## Leave-one-programme-out result

The two-direction result is **not leave-one-programme-out robust**.

- Omitting **ML001 *Serapias lingua*** removes the only resolved upstream process-dominant programme.
- After omitting ML001, 3 resolved programmes remain and all 3 are downstream F-dominant.
- Omitting any one of the other 11 programmes retains at least one resolved downstream programme and the *Serapias* upstream programme, so both directions remain represented.

Therefore:

> **Evidence for an upstream resolved bottleneck direction in the current corpus is ML001-dependent.**

This mirrors the broader influence result in the five-cluster direct synthesis, where ML001 is also the system whose omission removes the global Fisher rejection.

## Ecological claim ceiling

Allowed:

- the current audited corpus contains one resolved upstream process-dominant programme and three resolved downstream function-dominant programmes;
- current evidence is compatible with variable bottleneck position across systems;
- the upstream resolved direction is presently represented by one influential system;
- further independent movement/mating–F systems are required before claiming that upstream bottlenecks recur.

Not allowed:

- variable bottleneck position is leave-one-system-out robust;
- upstream bottlenecks are common;
- downstream and upstream bottlenecks occur at estimable relative frequencies;
- the 3:1 resolved split is a significance test or prevalence estimate.

## Consequence for title and abstract

The manuscript should use language such as **evidence for variable life-cycle bottleneck positions** rather than imply that recurrent bidirectional bottleneck variation is already established.

## Fresh validation consequence

The fresh Q → E → F programme remains necessary. It should accumulate genuinely new programmes before asking whether upstream and downstream bottleneck positions recur across independent systems.
