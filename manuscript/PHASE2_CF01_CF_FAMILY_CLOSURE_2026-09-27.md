# Phase-2 CF01 C-F family closure — 2026-09-27

## Closure state

The CF01 systematic target-pair search is complete:

- deterministic target-pair queue: **360 / 360 screened**;
- pending title/abstract/method screen: **0**;
- full-text-gated records originating from a C-F target family: **24**;
- C-F full-text records with a remaining `pending_*` quantitative status: **0**.

The direct same-system C-F coverage ledger remains:

- **2 / 5 independent programmes**;
- admitted systems: **ML001; ML002**.

The preregistered `5_independent_programmes` threshold is an **analysis-opening gate**, not a target
that authorizes additional searching or relaxed admission.

Therefore the direct C-F pair-specific analysis remains closed.

## What 2/5 means

This is no longer a search-in-progress denominator.

CF01 exhausted its frozen candidate queue and resolved every C-F-targeted full-text record to a
terminal state. Phase-2 did not add a new direct C-F programme because candidate studies failed one
or more frozen empirical requirements:

- no direct reproductive-function F on the same exposure;
- C and F measured on different campaigns, populations or independent-unit frames;
- lower-level trees, flowers, seeds or observations could not be promoted to fragmentation n;
- marginal sampling variance was not recoverable at the fragmentation unit;
- public raw-data bytes or matching source tables were inaccessible;
- an apparent C endpoint was actually mating/genetic state, interaction context or demographic
  recruitment;
- umbrella theses duplicated primary publications rather than supplying new independent K.

No candidate was excluded because its direction was null, inconvenient or inconsistent with a
theory.

## High-information terminal cases

### BDFFP seed rain — CFTQ0065

The design is unusually strong: eleven 1-ha plots span 1-ha, 10-ha and 100-ha fragments plus
continuous forest, with seed traps pooled to plot.

The expected Dryad workbooks are publicly indexed, but the deposited file bytes remain
unreproducible through the audited public routes.

Status: `blocked_public_dryad_file_bytes_not_recoverable`.

This is an access boundary, not a biological null.

### Aextoxicon punctatum — CFTQ0155

Fecundity is available at patch level, but the small-patch seed-immigration C estimate is pooled
across small patches while large patches are reported separately.

Status: `close_no_common_patch_level_C_effect_unit`.

The programme remains a movement-compensation / source-limitation anchor.

### Attalea humilis — CFTQ0171

The linked publications supply a valid four-fragment common biological frame, but complete
fragment-level C and F vectors are not publicly recoverable.

Status: `blocked_linked_four_fragment_CF_values_not_recoverable`.

The source directions show a reproduction–movement sign reversal, retained as mechanism evidence.

### Leptonychia usambarensis — CFTQ0215

The 2009 public appendix resolves the source landscape as fragments 2, 9, 16 and 31 ha plus
continuous-forest localities 520 and 3500 ha. Its tabular fragment-level data are rodent/dove
abundance, not direct seed movement, and realised fecundity is not exposed as a matching six-unit F
vector.

Status: `leptonychia_public_data_no_common_CF_frame`.

### Spondias mombin — CFTQ0301

Population-level pollen immigration C is extremely high in small islands, while reproductive
function is lower, but fruit production is reported from individual-tree/DBH analyses rather than as
a population-level F marginal with a compatible variance.

Status: `spondias_mombin_population_F_not_recoverable`.

This is retained as movement persistence alongside reproductive decline, not promoted to C-F K.

### Banksia sphaerocarpa var. caesia — CFTQ0313

The 2012 mating/connectivity dataset is publicly deposited, but the linked 2013 reproductive-output
paper does not expose the matched remnant-level F marginal required for a direct pair.

Status: `blocked_linked_2013_population_F_marginals_not_publicly_recoverable`.

### Duckeodendron cestroides — CFTQ0344

Fruit fall and direct seed dispersal were measured around the same focal-tree programme, but public
reporting supplies heterogeneous mixed-model summaries rather than a common focal-tree C/F table and
canonical marginal sampling variances; higher-level reserve nesting is also not recoverable.

Status:
`blocked_Duckeodendron_common_tree_CF_marginals_and_forest_nesting_not_recoverable`.

## Thesis / linked-publication identity closures

Umbrella theses were not counted as new programmes.

Chapter/publication mapping resolved several apparently promising C-F candidates without creating
new K:

- *Salix nigra*: within-one-population pollen gene flow; fragmentation is an implication, not the
  source exposure;
- *Eucalyptus aggregata*: 18-population hybrid-state/reproduction study plus a distinct three-site
  adult gene-flow campaign; hybrid state is not direct C;
- *Eucalyptus camaldulensis / E. leucoxylon*: the 2009 isolation-gradient and 2010
  paddock-versus-fragment publications use different estimands and direct-layer content.

Four remaining umbrella-source rows are explicitly `linked_umbrella_source_no_new_K`; they are
identity/provenance objects, not unresolved candidate programmes.

## Statistical consequence

The direct C-F family does **not** meet its five-programme analysis-opening gate.

Accordingly EGWEE must not report:

- a pooled direct C-F meta-analysis;
- a C-F moderator model;
- a direct C-F cross-system ordering;
- an inferred absence of movement–function decoupling from failure to reach K=5.

The absence of an opened pooled analysis is a **coverage/effect-unit limitation**, not evidence that C
and F are exchangeable.

## Biological consequence

Although the direct C-F formal family remains closed, several independent systems repeatedly show a
mechanistically important pattern in source-level evidence:

> movement/connectivity can remain substantial, or be supplied by immigrants, while local
> reproductive output or recruitment support is reduced.

Examples include *Spondias mombin*, *Aextoxicon punctatum*, *Attalea humilis* and *Leptonychia
usambarensis*.

These systems are retained as **mechanistic anchors**, not converted into effect rows that violate
the frozen independent-unit or covariance contracts.

This pattern can motivate future sampling and interpretation, but it is not yet a pooled C-F
meta-analytic result.

## Reopening rule

The direct C-F family may change only when a currently terminal programme's stated missing evidence
becomes available—for example:

- previously inaccessible raw files become reproducibly public;
- an authoritative fragment/population-level F or C table is released;
- a linked-campaign source explicitly resolves the common independent-unit frame and dependence.

Reopening uses the already-frozen endpoint and effect-unit rules. It does not trigger a new search
for additional programmes merely to reach K=5.

## Theory firewall

This closure is entirely empirical.

Failure to open the direct C-F meta-analysis neither supports nor falsifies an EGWE/NEE operator.
Likewise, agreement with a theoretical compensation narrative cannot be used to promote a
mechanistic anchor into the quantitative family.

EGWEE remains the natural-data synthesis; EGWE remains the finite-model theory programme.
