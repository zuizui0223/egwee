# ML009 / PS013 *Pistacia lentiscus* reference audit

## Decision

Terminal state: `single_landscape_per_condition_no_fragmentation_replication`.

No quantitative primary effect is admitted to the cross-system multilayer synthesis.

The 2012 highly fragmented landscape and the previously studied large continuous stand provide useful **programme-level comparative evidence**, but they do not provide replicated fragmentation units. Mothers, seeds, males, loci and distance classes are nested biological observations and cannot be promoted to independent fragmented/reference landscapes.

## Why the earlier "cross-campaign" shorthand is insufficient

The fragmented study collected fruits in October 2007. The continuous study analysed two consecutive flowering seasons, 2006 and 2007. Therefore the continuous 2007 correlated-paternity estimate is temporally aligned with the fragmented 2007 fruit cohort well enough for a descriptive matched-season comparison.

The blocker is not simply calendar mismatch. The blocker is the exposure grain:

- fragmented condition: one highly disturbed landscape/site;
- continuous reference: one large dense continuous stand;
- fragmentation-level replication: `1 vs 1`.

Accordingly, the 29 fragmented mothers and 30 continuous mothers are not fragmentation replicates.

## Source-harmonized comparisons retained as descriptive evidence

### Mating / pollen-cloud diversity

The 2012 fragmented study reports within-mother correlated paternity `r_p = 0.231`.

The continuous reference reports strong temporal variation:

- 2006: `r_p = 0.030`;
- 2007: `r_p = 0.085`.

The source interprets the fragmented value as substantially higher than the continuous stand. For EGWEE, the 2007 continuous value is the primary descriptive season match; the 2006 value is retained as temporal sensitivity. Neither comparison is converted into Hedges g or Fisher z with mother-level n because the exposure is assigned at landscape level.

### Adult spatial genetic structure

The fragmented study reports `S_p = 0.0122`. The 2012 paper recomputes the continuous-reference value as `S_p = 0.0022` and explicitly notes the stronger fine-scale spatial genetic structure in the fragmented landscape.

This is the best harmonized cross-landscape metric in the programme, but it is still one fragmented landscape versus one continuous landscape. It is descriptive comparative evidence, not an independent replicated fragmentation effect.

### Pollen-dispersal kernel

Do not manufacture a C-layer kernel contrast. The 2012 source explicitly notes that the analogous continuous-population pollen-dispersal comparison is not available in a directly comparable form. Therefore extensive pollen movement in the fragmented landscape remains process context, not a synchronized quantitative fragmented-vs-continuous effect.

## Cluster boundary

ML009 targets `C`, `G_mating`, and `G_adult`, but admits zero primary effects because:

1. only one landscape exists per exposure condition;
2. mother/seed/locus observations are nested below that exposure;
3. a within-cluster C/G sampling covariance at the fragmentation-unit level is not identifiable;
4. the strongest source-harmonized comparisons come from two publications/programme components rather than replicated landscape contrasts.

The programme can be cited as evidence that severe anthropogenic fragmentation coincides with higher correlated paternity and stronger adult SGS while overall pollen movement and genetic diversity can remain high. That biological interpretation must stay separate from the covariance-aware cross-system denominator.

## Reopen condition

Reopen ML009 only if a source provides additional independent fragmented/continuous landscapes under compatible mating/SGS definitions, or a prespecified hierarchical design yields valid landscape-level uncertainty and cross-layer dependence without treating mothers or individuals as independent fragmentation replicates.

Do not rescue the cluster by:

- using mother count as landscape n;
- averaging the 2006 and 2007 continuous seasons and treating them as separate control landscapes;
- using loci or distance classes as replication;
- converting the source's qualitative fragmented/continuous comparison into an invented sampling variance;
- treating the recomputed continuous `S_p` as a second independent study.
