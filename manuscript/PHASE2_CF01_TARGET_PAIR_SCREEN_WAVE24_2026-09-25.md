## CFTQ0235 — Artz–Waddington Asclepias landscape experiment

This is the only wave-24 candidate retained for full-text resolution.

The source has strong I-F biology:

- 5 small and 6 large Everglades tree islands;
- repeated sampling positions at 25, 100, 300 and 700–1000 m from the same islands;
- direct insect visits to potted *Asclepias lanceolata*;
- direct fruit and seed production on the same island-distance architecture.

The study shows that pollinator activity and reproductive output decline with increasing distance
from tree islands.

However, the public reporting does not preserve a valid common independent-unit table:

- direct visitation observations are pooled by distance class within small/large island strata;
- fruit/seed analyses retain repeated distance positions nested within the same islands;
- reported seed-production regressions use roughly 40 and 48 distance-site observations around only
  5 and 6 source islands.

The **tree island**, not island×distance position, is the highest independent landscape unit.
EGWEE therefore refuses to count 40/48 repeated sampling positions as 40/48 independent
fragmentation units.

Decision:
`blocked_repeated_island_IF_effect_unit_not_recoverable`.

This is a quantitative effect-unit limitation, not an ecological null.
