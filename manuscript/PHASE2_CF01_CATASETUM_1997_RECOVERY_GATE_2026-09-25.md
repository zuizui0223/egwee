# CFTQ0278 Catasetum viridiflavum 1997 I-F recovery gate — 2026-09-25

## Frozen target

Programme: `P2_CF01_CATASETUM_2002`.

The pre-audit recovery contract fixed the direct I-F candidate to the 1997 common-year design:

- fragmented = island forest;
- reference = mainland large/continuous forest;
- I = site-level *Eulaema cingulata* abundance, bees trapped/day;
- F = 1997 fruit set;
- required common frame = the same five island + five mainland sites;
- independent unit = site/population.

No other year may replace 1997 after source outcomes are inspected.

## Full-source audit

The open Journal of Ecology article establishes the following.

### Pollinator frame

The 1997 scent-bait survey sampled:

- **five island sites**;
- **five mainland sites**;
- each on three non-consecutive days.

The paper reports forest-type bee-abundance summaries and a nested ANOVA with site inside forest
type.

However, the text does **not identify which five island sites** from the ten-island study network
were used for the bait survey.

### Reproductive frame

Female reproductive output was followed over the broader study network. The article describes the
1997 reproductive survey within the 15-site programme, but its 1997 nested fruit-set model reports
`Site (forest type)` numerator d.f. = 11, showing that the effective analysed F frame is not
transparently recoverable as the same named ten sites used for bee abundance.

### Site map

Figure 1 labels all 15 study sites with letters. It does not mark the five island + five mainland
subset used in the 1997 bait survey.

Therefore the map cannot be used to infer the I-F common frame.

## Decision

Status:
`blocked_1997_common_site_identity_and_site_level_F_vector_not_recoverable`.

This is an **effect-unit/recoverability STOP**, not evidence that pollinator and reproductive
responses are equal under fragmentation.

No Hedges-g I or F effect is admitted from this programme and direct I-F programme coverage does not
increase.

## Why group summaries do not rescue the programme

The article reports island/mainland bee-abundance means and standard deviations, but the frozen
multilayer contract requires valid marginal effects on the same independent-unit frame. The
reproductive layer does not expose a matching ten-site fruit-set vector or certified site-level
dispersion.

EGWEE therefore does not:

- guess which island labels formed the bait-survey subset;
- infer site membership from Figure 1 geometry;
- infer the common frame from ANOVA degrees of freedom;
- pair forest-type bee summaries with lower-level fruit-set variation;
- use 1996 or 1998 fruit set to obtain a stronger contrast.

## Ecological interpretation retained

The source remains strong mechanism evidence:

- *E. cingulata* reached island fragments despite not being resident there;
- bee abundance was similar between island and mainland forest types in 1997;
- reproductive performance varied strongly among sites and across years;
- pollinator movement from mainland forest can maintain reproduction in fragments.

Those observations are retained as fragmentation-pollination connectivity evidence without
manufacturing a quantitative paired I-F cluster.

2. 1997 site-level bee abundance for those sites;

4. site-level marginal dispersion or paired raw values sufficient for the frozen dependence rule.

All terminal outcomes remain retained.
