## CFTQ0215 — Leptonychia usambarensis

This is the only wave-22 candidate retained for the quantitative pathway.

The ecological programme is unusually strong:

- Cordeiro & Howe (2003) directly compared a c.3500-ha continuous forest with four small fragments
  of about 2, 9, 13 and 31 ha;
- the earlier study documented reduced disperser visitation / seed removal and poorer recruitment in
  fragments;
- Cordeiro et al. (2009) explicitly extends the same system and tests whether diminished fecundity,
  seed quality, seed predation or adverse post-dispersal conditions could explain the recruitment
  deficit independently of seed dispersal.

The biological logic therefore supports a possible C-F programme, but the two papers are separated
in time.

EGWEE freezes a **linked-campaign gate** before effect extraction:

1. recover the exact 2009 fragment/site identities;
2. verify overlap with the four 2003 fragments and continuous-forest comparator;
3. recover direct fecundity at the same fragmentation-level unit;
4. establish whether C and F belong to a defensibly synchronized campaign or must remain temporally
   linked but analytically separate;
5. never use trees, observation hours, seeds or seedlings as forest-fragment n.

Status:
`pending_linked_2003_2009_site_and_campaign_alignment`.

Direct C-F increment remains **0** until the common independent-unit/campaign contract is satisfied.
