## 2008 Journal of Ecology programme

The source samples **18 E. aggregata populations** and tests effects of:

- absolute population size;
- **relative population size** compared with sympatric congeners;

on:

- hybrid seed production;
- genetic diversity / inbreeding;
- direct seed production;
- germination and seedling performance.

Relative parental abundance strongly predicts hybrid production and reproductive performance.

Under the frozen EGWEE layers, however, **hybrid seed production is offspring mating/genetic state**
(`G_offspring`), not direct movement/connectivity C.

The primary predictor is also relative parental abundance/population size, not one direct
habitat-area/isolation fragmentation variable.

## 2011 Heredity programme

The later paper directly studies asymmetrical interspecific gene flow, but on **three adult hybrid
zones**.

That adult three-site campaign is not the same independent-unit/cohort frame as the 18-population
open-pollinated seed/reproduction study.
