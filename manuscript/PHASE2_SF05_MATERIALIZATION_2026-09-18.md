# Phase-2 SF05 materialization — 2026-09-18

## Current state

SF05 is the 2023 AoB PLANTS FSGS synthesis (doi:10.1093/aobpla/plad019). Its public Supporting Table S1 has been materialized without spreadsheet recalculation and reduced to a study-level bibliographic screening universe.

The workbook contains 177 population rows belonging to 65 source-selected systematic-review papers; 31 of those papers are marked by the source as entering its quantitative meta-analysis.

## Outcome-blind denominator warning

The published article reports the flow 343 -> 243 nonduplicate -> 200 source-eligible -> 74 with habitat status + Sp -> 65 systematic-review papers. The final step is outcome-dependent: six studies with non-significant FSGS and three studies with outlier Sp were removed before Table S1.

Therefore the 65 materialized identities are not EGWEE's outcome-blind SF05 denominator. They are a recoverable seed subset. At minimum, the nine outcome-excluded identities must be restored to candidate screening if they can be recovered; ideally the broader 243/200 record frame is reconstructed through the registered reproducible search route.

No non-significant FSGS study will be coded as zero, and no source outlier exclusion is inherited as an EGWEE eligibility rule.

## What has and has not been opened

Materialized now:
- citation/DOI/species and source study membership;
- disturbance, cohort, life-form, mating-system, pollination/dispersal metadata already present in Table S1;
- title/method screening hints used only to prioritize full-text multilayer screening;
- links to already registered EGWEE studies when a DOI matches.

Not opened for Phase 2:
- no new C/I/F/G-offspring effect size;
- no new layer-pair contrast;
- no candidate is admitted because of effect direction, significance or apparent support for state separation.

## Next screening

Screen title/method candidates for additional same-system layers, with particular attention to fragmentation papers that also measured pollen/gene dispersal, mating, cohorts, density or reproductive function. Every candidate receives the same same-exposure and independent-unit gate before quantitative extraction.
