## Advances

### CFTQ0065 — Central Amazon fragmented-forest seed rain

The BDFFP study uses 1-ha sampling plots in 1-ha, 10-ha and 100-ha forest fragments plus continuous-forest controls and reports a replicated plot-level design. The same seed-trap framework distinguishes dispersed and undispersed seeds.

This is the strongest wave-7 C-F candidate, but admission is not automatic. Full-text/data audit must first lock:

1. whether the dispersed-seed quantity satisfies the frozen movement/connectivity (C) definition;
2. whether the undispersed/local seed quantity is a direct reproductive-function (F) endpoint rather than only a seed-rain origin category;
3. the fragment/site/plot hierarchy and true independent fragmentation unit;
4. a common effect representation and sampling dispersion without promoting traps to landscape replicates.

No numerical effect has been calculated.

### CFTQ0069 — Ophrys balearica population configuration and fitness

Seven populations are characterized by spatial patch properties, and male pollination fitness and female fruit-set fitness are measured in the same population framework.

The unresolved design question is whether source-defined patch size/shape represents an eligible habitat-fragmentation exposure or only conspecific population geometry/density. The ambiguity rule therefore advances this record to methods clarification rather than closing or admitting it.

### CFTQ0070 — Brazil nut forest-degradation gradient

The source defines replicated forest-degradation categories and measures contemporary pollen-mediated gene flow plus offspring genetic state. This is a strong C/G landscape-degradation programme.

The C-F pair remains unconfirmed. Full text must establish whether fruit/seed production or fecundity is measured as a response at the study-site level. Fruits collected as genetic material do not automatically constitute an F endpoint.

## Wave-7 closures

- **CFTQ0061 snapdragon:** C and migrant reproductive success are studied within one fragmented landscape, but no common fragmentation-severity/reference axis is defined across patches.
- **CFTQ0062 fynbos bird pollination:** direct pollination response, but no direct reproductive-function F endpoint.
- **CFTQ0063 Sidalcea–Rubus:** the causal exposure is invasive-plant context, not habitat fragmentation.
- **CFTQ0064 forest orchids:** dispersal/gene-flow biology is measured in a fragmented landscape, but no common source-defined fragmentation axis for paired C-F response is established.
- **CFTQ0066 Paphiopedilum:** one focal fragmented population; flowers, plants and years cannot create independent fragmentation units.
- **CFTQ0067 Sinocalycanthus:** pollen supplementation/mate distance are the causal treatments; fragmentation is background.
- **CFTQ0068 Magnolia patungensis:** strong C/G fragmented-forest evidence, but no direct F endpoint and no common fragmentation-severity/reference axis.
