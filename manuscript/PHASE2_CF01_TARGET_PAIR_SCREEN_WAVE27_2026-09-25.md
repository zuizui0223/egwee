## CFTQ0263 — Brunsvigia radulosa

The source jointly measures direct seed production and pollen limitation across fragmented grassland
populations and records three related predictors:

- habitat fragment area;
- population isolation;
- population size.

This is a legitimate I-F candidate, but population size is not automatically an EGWEE habitat-
fragmentation estimand. Published results emphasize population size, so choosing that predictor
after seeing the outcome would violate the frozen exposure firewall.

Decision: retain at
`pending_fragment_area_or_isolation_estimand_and_common_population_IF_frame`.

No effect is calculated until one source habitat variable — fragment area or isolation — and a
common population-level pollen-limitation / seed-production frame are recovered. Population size is
retained as a separate demographic / mate-availability moderator.

## CFTQ0267 — Quesada bombacaceous trees

This paper directly evaluates fragmentation effects on bat visitation and reproductive success in
*Ceiba aesculifolia*, *C. grandiflora* and *C. pentandra*.

It is a strong direct I-F candidate, but the source response frames are heterogeneous:

- visitation is reported from filmed flowers / inflorescences;
- reproductive success is measured on trees;
- species differ in geography and habitat sampling;
- not every species clearly has the same I/F endpoint set.

EGWEE therefore freezes a species-specific effect-unit gate:

- reconstruct tree/local-context marginal I and F effects;
- preserve species-specific forest/fragment frames;
- do not promote filmed flowers to independent fragmentation n;
- keep all eligible species as dependent panels inside **one publication programme**;
- if valid marginal effects exist but covariance is unrecoverable, use the prespecified cluster-
  robust fallback rather than setting covariance to zero.

Status:
`pending_species_specific_common_IF_effect_unit_and_dependence_recovery`.

Direct I-F increment remains **0** until at least one species panel satisfies the effect-unit contract.
