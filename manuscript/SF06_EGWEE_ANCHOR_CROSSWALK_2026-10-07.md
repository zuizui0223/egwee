# SF06 × EGWEE false-reassurance anchor crosswalk — corrected 2026-10-07

## Purpose

Clarify whether the external SF06 generalization merely reuses the three strongest quantitative EGWEE false-reassurance anchors.

## EGWEE anchors

1. *Eucalyptus wandoo* — interaction/pollen-tube response positive, reproductive function negative.
2. *Cardiopetalum calophyllum* — both responses decline, but reproductive function declines much more strongly than pollinator abundance.
3. Kakamega *Acanthopale pubescens* — interaction/visit occurrence positive, reproductive function negative.

## Overlap with accessible SF06 public S1

- *Eucalyptus wandoo*: absent.
- *Cardiopetalum calophyllum*: present as SF06PAIR035.
- *Acanthopale pubescens*: absent.

Thus only one of the three quantitative anchors is represented in the external paired source.

The overlapping case, *Cardiopetalum*, is primarily a same-sign **severity mismatch** in EGWEE rather than one of the two clearest sign reversals. Corrected SF06 parsing recovers the same source direction: female-fitness d = -1.733 and pollination d = -0.048.

## Source-disjoint external generalization

More importantly, the SF06 robustness analysis deletes **every publication** already represented in the frozen EGWEE I–F map, not just the three anchors.

After that deletion:

- 31 constituent-consensus habitat-fragmentation pairs remain;
- 20 are I−F−;
- 6 are I−F+;
- 4 are I+F−;
- 1 is I+F+;
- minimum deterministic mismatches = 7;
- publication-LOO minimum = 5;
- species-LOO minimum = 6.

Therefore the external many-to-many topology does **not** depend on Wandoo, Cardiopetalum, Acanthopale, or any other publication already used in the frozen EGWEE translation map.

## Consequence

The evidence hierarchy is now:

1. three matched-programme quantitative anchors provide the strongest individually resolved evidence for concrete downstream severity/false-reassurance failures;
2. the frozen 16-programme EGWEE map shows many-to-many interaction→function states across mixed quantitative and source-explicit evidence;
3. the source-publication-disjoint SF06 public-S1 subset independently generalizes the same sign-level non-identifiability across 31 additional paired units.

This is stronger than saying that the external source merely contains one familiar counterexample.

## Claim ceiling

Allowed:

- the external generalization persists after deleting all EGWEE-overlapping publications;
- the strongest matched-programme anchors and the external source-disjoint topology are complementary evidence;
- Cardiopetalum independently reproduces its strong same-sign amplitude contrast in SF06 after corrected sign parsing.

Not allowed:

- the 31 external pairs are a random sample of nature;
- the 7 mismatches are prevalence or prediction-error estimates;
- the external point-sign mismatches are all individually resolved;
- absence of Wandoo/Acanthopale from SF06 implies publication bias.
