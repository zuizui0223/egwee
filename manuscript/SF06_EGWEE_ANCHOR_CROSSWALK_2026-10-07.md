# SF06 × EGWEE false-reassurance anchor crosswalk — 2026-10-07

## Purpose

Clarify what the accessible SF06 public-S1 sign analysis does and does not test relative to the three representation-stable quantitative EGWEE false-reassurance anchors.

## Result

The three EGWEE anchors are:

1. *Eucalyptus wandoo* — interaction/pollen-tube response positive, reproductive function negative;
2. *Cardiopetalum calophyllum* — both responses negative, but reproductive function declines much more strongly than pollinator abundance;
3. Kakamega *Acanthopale pubescens* — interaction/visit occurrence positive, reproductive function negative.

Crosswalking those sources against the frozen SF06 exact-pair manifest gives:

- *Eucalyptus wandoo*: **absent** from the accessible SF06 pollination–female-fitness paired subset;
- *Cardiopetalum calophyllum*: **present** as `SF06PAIR035`;
- *Acanthopale pubescens*: **absent**.

Thus the accessible SF06 sign analysis directly overlaps only one of the three quantitative false-reassurance anchors.

Crucially, the overlapping anchor is *Cardiopetalum*, whose robust EGWEE failure is an **amplitude/severity mismatch**, not a sign reversal: pollinator abundance and fruit set both decline, but fruit set declines much more strongly.

## Consequence

A deterministic pollination-sign → female-fitness-sign lookup in SF06 does **not** test the strongest EGWEE sign-discordant anchors, because those two anchors are absent from the accessible paired source.

Nor can a sign lookup detect the core *Cardiopetalum* result, because its two endpoints share the same sign.

The external and internal results therefore address different levels of sentinel sufficiency:

- **directional sufficiency:** can pollination response direction identify female-fitness response direction?
- **severity sufficiency:** can the magnitude of pollination response calibrate the magnitude of reproductive impairment?
- **local state sufficiency:** does an apparently retained interaction quantity imply that reproductive function is intact in a focal matched system?

The accessible SF06 result can speak mainly to the first question. The strongest quantitative EGWEE anchors concern the second and third.

## Emerging synthesis

The evidence is increasingly consistent with:

> **pollination can be directionally informative in aggregate while remaining quantitatively and locally insufficient as a sentinel of reproductive function.**

This is narrower than a claim of universal sign-level non-identifiability and is better aligned with both the published Aguilar et al. cross-species coupling and the matched-programme EGWEE results.

## Claim ceiling

Allowed:

- SF06 sign determinism does not directly refute the strongest EGWEE false-reassurance anchors;
- two of the three quantitative anchors are absent from the accessible SF06 paired subset;
- the one overlapping anchor is a same-sign amplitude mismatch;
- external directional coupling and local quantitative translation failure can coexist.

Not allowed:

- absence from SF06 proves publication bias;
- SF06 would necessarily reproduce Wandoo or Acanthopale if those studies were included;
- sign agreement demonstrates accurate severity prediction;
- the crosswalk establishes grain dependence causally.
