## Design closures

### CFTQ0094 — Zygophyllum xanthoxylum

This is the strongest apparent I-F hit in the wave: pollinator visitation and seed-set/pollen-limitation
responses are measured under a source-defined fragmented-versus-control design.

Full methods show, however, that the treatment consists of only **one fragmented habitat patch and one
control habitat patch**, separated by about 300 m. Each contains three plots. Those plots are nested
within the two habitat patches and cannot be promoted to three independent fragmented and three
independent control landscapes.

The programme therefore closes on habitat-level pseudoreplication, not on effect direction.

### Other wave-10 closures

- **CFTQ0091 Oenocarpus fruit removal:** continuous rainforest with fine-scale habitat-quality variation; fruit removal is seed-dispersal C, not fecundity F, and there is no fragmentation exposure.
- **CFTQ0092 Nothocalais:** pollination/reproductive biology in one hill-prairie population.
- **CFTQ0093 Gomortega:** historical microsatellite differentiation; no direct F and no common contemporary fragmentation contrast.
- **CFTQ0095 Brazil nut dispersal:** pollen/seed dispersal in two natural populations; fragmentation is a projected consequence rather than the measured exposure and no direct F response is analysed.
- **CFTQ0096 Eschweilera:** edge/interior phenology and floral visitors within one forest; reproductive phenology is not direct F.
- **CFTQ0097 restoration plantations:** adult genetic-diversity study without direct I/F fragmentation responses.
- **CFTQ0098 Hesperidanthus:** road-dust/distance exposure and reproductive output; no direct fragmentation-response I endpoint.
- **CFTQ0099 Espírito Santo defaunation:** non-primary synthesis/considerations paper.
- **CFTQ0100 Platanthera:** self-versus-cross hand-pollination experiment; fragmented grassland is ecological context rather than the causal exposure.
