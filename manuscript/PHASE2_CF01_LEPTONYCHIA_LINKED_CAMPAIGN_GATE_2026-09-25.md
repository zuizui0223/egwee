# CFTQ0215 Leptonychia linked-campaign C-F gate — 2026-09-25

## Programme identity

Programme: `P2_CF01_LEPTONYCHIA_2003_2009`.

Primary publications:

- Cordeiro & Howe (2003), *PNAS*, forest fragmentation and seed-disperser mutualism;
- Cordeiro et al. (2009), *Ecology*, disperser limitation and recruitment.

The 2009 paper explicitly treats the 2003 dispersal result as prior evidence and asks whether other
processes—especially fecundity, seed quality, predation or abiotic recruitment limitation—can
explain the same fragmentation-associated recruitment failure.

## What is already source-explicit

The 2003 study used:

- one large continuous forest (about 3500 ha);
- four small forest fragments of approximately **2, 9, 13 and 31 ha**;
- direct bird-visitation / seed-removal measurements;
- seedling/juvenile recruitment spatial responses.

This establishes a strong C / movement-disruption programme.

The 2009 paper reports extended disperser observations and explicitly tests **diminished fecundity**
as an alternative explanation for lower recruitment in fragments.

## Why C-F is not yet admissible

The two papers are separated in time and the public 2009 surface available to the audit does not yet
establish that:

1. the exact four 2003 fragments and continuous-forest unit are the same independent habitat units
   for the 2009 fecundity comparison;
2. the C and F observations belong to a defensibly synchronized campaign/window;
3. direct fecundity values are recoverable at the same habitat-unit level as seed dispersal;
4. any within-programme dependence can be reconstructed or assigned the frozen cluster-robust
   fallback without silently concatenating campaigns.

Same species + same region + linked citations are not sufficient to create a paired multilayer
cluster.

## Frozen gate

No C-F effect is opened until the following are recovered from authoritative sources:

- 2009 site/fragment names or uniquely identifying sizes;
- 2009 continuous-forest comparator identity;
- timing of dispersal and fecundity measurements;
- direct habitat-unit fecundity summaries or raw values;
- explicit overlap with the 2003 C frame.

## Permitted terminal outcomes

- `leptonychia_linked_direct_CF_cluster_robust`;
- `leptonychia_linked_direct_CF_covariance_aware`;
- `leptonychia_shared_sites_but_temporally_distinct_mechanism_programme`;
- `leptonychia_site_overlap_not_recoverable`;
- `leptonychia_fragment_level_F_not_recoverable`.

## No rescue

Do not:

- pair 2003 C with 2009 F merely because the species and landscape are the same;
- treat trees, observation hours, seeds, seedlings or juveniles as independent forest fragments;
- use recruitment as F;
- interpret absence of a fecundity effect in 2009 as equivalence with C;
- combine campaign-specific effects without a linked-publication dependence contract;
- count the 2003 and 2009 papers as two independent programmes.

Until the gate is resolved, the programme is retained as strong **dispersal-limitation / mechanism
evidence** with direct C-F increment = 0.
