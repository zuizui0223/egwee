# Cover letter draft — Ecological Indicators

Dear Editors,

Please consider our manuscript, **“Test the state before interpreting the residual: four empirical gates for ecological state indicators,”** for publication as an Original Research Paper in *Ecological Indicators*.

Ecological studies often treat a biological state, proxy, or indicator as adequate before asking whether geography, habitat, origin, or history explains residual variation. Our manuscript addresses the prior inferential question: when has the proposed state actually earned the right to support that downstream interpretation?

We develop a fail-closed four-gate sequence—endpoint-relevant measurement adequacy, information-preserving representation, residual-context testing, and cross-study identifiability—and apply it to seven locked natural-data analyses. The systems deliberately do not estimate one common ecological effect. Instead, they occupy distinct branches of the same interpretation workflow: three residual-context tests show no detected transferable gain after their locked partial states; *Oenothera harringtonii* identifies a missing mating-opportunity coordinate; *Eschscholzia californica* and Mallorca carob stop at measurement adequacy; *Campanula americana* shows a representation collapse caused by feature-wise standardisation; and a requested urban–island synthesis remains non-identifiable.

The contribution is therefore methodological and indicator-focused rather than a collection of descriptive case studies. The component practices—indicator validation, held-out predictive testing, uncertainty assessment, proxy evaluation, and standardisation—are established. The new contribution is their ordered use as a rule for **what may be interpreted next**, with STOP, failed adequacy, and non-identifiability retained as scientific outcomes rather than repaired after results are known.

This framing matches *Ecological Indicators* particularly well because the manuscript concerns indicator development, testing, and interpretation rather than simple monitoring. No heterogeneous effect sizes are pooled, and none of the natural systems is presented as external validation of the separate synthetic eco-genetic model or genetic-warning work.

All project-computed endpoints, candidate states, holdout units, stop rules, and claim ceilings are locked in machine-readable registries and migrated preregistration/result records. The submission package is built deterministically from those records. Third-party datasets are cited to their original repositories and are not redistributed where licensing does not permit it.

The manuscript has not been submitted elsewhere. Author order, affiliations, CRediT roles, funding, acknowledgements, competing interests, and the final automated-tool disclosure remain subject to explicit author approval before portal submission.
