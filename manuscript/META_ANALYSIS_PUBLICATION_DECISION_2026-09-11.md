# Publication decision — EGWEE becomes the empirical meta-analysis counterpart to NEE

**Decision date:** 2026-09-11

## Decision

The active EGWEE publication is no longer the four-gate ecological-indicator methods paper.

The active direction is a **systematic review and multilevel meta-analysis of natural fragmentation effects across biological layers in flowering plants**.

NEE remains the theory/mechanism paper. EGWEE asks whether the core state-separation prediction is visible across natural systems.

## Two linked empirical questions

1. **State separation in nature:** under the same fragmentation contrast, do resource/demographic support, pollinator interaction, movement/gene flow, reproductive function, adult genetics and offspring genetics show equivalent effects?
2. **Structured discordance:** when layers diverge, is that divergence associated with movement/connectivity, reproductive assurance, pollination vector, cohort identity and fragmentation history?

## What changes

- `MULTILAYER_FRAGMENTATION_META_ANALYSIS.md` becomes the active manuscript spine.
- A protocol and effect-size schema are frozen before synthesis.
- Existing large meta-analyses become search/data seeds rather than conclusions to be copied.
- Original primary studies are deduplicated and re-extracted where possible.
- The four-gate EGWEE work becomes QC/provenance material for admissibility and interpretation.
- The former Ecological Indicators submission metadata is historical, not active.

## What does not change

The seven previous locked analyses are not deleted or reinterpreted. Their endpoints, holdouts, STOP rules, negative results and claim ceilings remain fixed. They simply no longer define the headline paper.

The four pre-existing natural mechanism anchors also remain valuable:

- *Crepis sancta* — interaction limitation;
- Miyake *Camellia–Zosterops* — movement compensation;
- *Conospermum undulatum* — cohort/history lag;
- *Spondias purpurea* — joint interaction/movement/function/offspring-genetic deterioration.

They become illustrative anchor systems inside a larger quantitative synthesis rather than the sample itself.

## Novelty boundary

The paper must not claim novelty for showing that fragmentation has negative average effects on pollination, reproduction, genetics or pollinator abundance; those are established by prior meta-analyses.

The novelty target is **cross-layer concordance/discordance under shared fragmentation contrasts**: existing syntheses usually aggregate one response domain at a time, whereas EGWEE will explicitly link multiple biological layers within studies/species and test whether they behave as one deterioration axis.
