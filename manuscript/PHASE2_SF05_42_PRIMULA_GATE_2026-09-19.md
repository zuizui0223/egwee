# Phase-2 SF05-42 Primula primary quantitative gate — 2026-09-19

## Decision

Van Rossum & Triest 2003 (*Primula vulgaris*) is **closed for the current prespecified Phase-2 pair families**.

The study remains highly informative for layer-specific genetic state, reproductive function, fragmentation context and moderator coding. The closure is only about the preregistered paired estimands.

## Source geometry

The study covers 41 populations in a fragmented agricultural landscape and measures:

- adult genetic variation/structure with 27 allozyme loci;
- reproductive success, including fruit set;
- spatial genetic structure used to infer current gene flow.

The source also compares two continuous populations with two groups of fragmented populations of similar distance classes for spatial-autocorrelation slopes.

## Why it does not increment a registered pair

The population network supplies **G_adult + F**, but `G_adult-F` is not one of the preregistered Phase-2 pair families.

The registered pair families are:

- I-F;
- C-F;
- G_adult-G_offspring;
- G_adult-mean(I,F).

No G_offspring cohort is reported.

The gene-flow evidence is based on spatial autocorrelation and grouped distance-class comparisons rather than a population-level C endpoint on the same 41-population frame as fruit set. It therefore cannot be paired with F as a common-frame C-F effect without changing the source estimand after inspection.

## Retained use

Primula remains eligible for:

- layer-specific G_adult and F coverage if exact population-level source values become recoverable under the systematic expansion;
- fragmentation/moderator metadata;
- mechanistic discussion of genetic connectivity, reproductive success and stepping-stone population networks.

It does not change any pair-specific programme count.
