# ML019 / PS024 Pithecellobium elegans recovery closure

## Decision

ML019 is **not admitted** to the primary multilayer Hedges-g stream from the currently recoverable public evidence.

Status: `population_level_F_vector_or_G_F_pairing_not_reconstructable`.

## Passed gates

- Independent species/system relative to the four canonical primary clusters.
- Source-defined replicated landscape design: two continuous populations in a 1500-ha reserve and six fragmented populations separated by up to 75 km.
- Population is the defensible fragmentation unit.
- Adult allozyme diversity is reported on the eight-population design; secondary synthesis gives the two continuous-population H values as 0.14 and 0.14 and the six fragmented-population range as 0.12–0.15.
- The same source reports reproductive consequences (fruit/seed set) in the same fragmentation programme and qualitatively associates fragmented/low-flowering-density conditions with lower seed crops or failure to set fruit.

## Blocking gate

The prospectively fixed F endpoint is not recoverable as an exact population-level numeric vector aligned to the eight population IDs.

The accessible primary abstract and independent review syntheses establish the biological direction, but they do not expose one seed-set/seed-crop or fruit-set value for each of the two continuous and six fragmented populations. The reproductive result is reported as poor seed crops / failure to fruit under low flowering density and as lower seed set in fragments, rather than as an eight-row population table that can be paired to adult expected heterozygosity.

Therefore the frozen primary requirements fail:

1. the exact G/F common population frame cannot be materialized numerically;
2. a population-level F Hedges-g effect and variance cannot be reconstructed without lower-level tree/fruit/seed pseudo-replication;
3. G and F cannot be aligned by exact population ID; and
4. the required paired-population covariance cannot be reconstructed.

Qualitative evidence that both genetic diversity and reproductive function deteriorate with fragmentation is retained as background evidence but is not converted into a primary cluster statistic.

## Reopening condition

Reopen ML019 only if the original source tables/raw data expose population-specific reproductive values for the same eight (or an explicitly overlapping subset of) source populations, with exact population identities and sufficient information for population-level Hedges-g effects and G–F dependence.

No hypothetical ML019 cluster p-value is inserted into the Fisher synthesis. The canonical omit-ML001 diagnostic remains unchanged until a genuinely admissible fifth same-effect-family cluster is recovered.
