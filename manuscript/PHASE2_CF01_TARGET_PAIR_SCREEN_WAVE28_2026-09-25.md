## CFTQ0272 — Coffea canephora forest-distance gradient

The source samples **15 independent coffee agroforestry systems** in Central Sulawesi and jointly
measures:

- distance to nearest old-growth rainforest / forest margin;
- coffee flower-visiting bees;
- open-pollinated fruit set;
- local shade and floral-resource covariates.

The retrospective recovery contract now freezes:

- independent unit = agroforestry system/site;
- primary landscape exposure = distance to forest;
- I = broad total coffee flower-visiting bee abundance/visitation;
- F = open-pollinated fruit set;
- light intensity, coffee/noncoffee blossom cover and flowering-plant richness = covariates;
- social versus solitary bee guilds = sensitivities/mechanistic descriptors, not selectable primary
  endpoints.

This avoids choosing the bee guild or local-management variable with the strongest published
association.

No EGWEE Fisher-z effect has yet been calculated. Admission requires a recoverable site-level common
distance/I/F frame.

## CFTQ0278 — Catasetum viridiflavum direct I-F candidate

The full study follows **10 Panama Canal islands and five mainland forest sites for three years**.

However, the replicated direct pollinator-abundance survey is narrower: in 1997 the author sampled
**five island and five mainland sites** on three non-consecutive days. Fruit set was also measured in
1997.

The direct I-F contract therefore fixes the common programme frame to those same **10 sites in
1997**, rather than mixing the 15-site reproductive census or the stronger 1996/1998 fruit-set
differences into the I effect.

Frozen candidate:

- fragmented = island site;
- reference = mainland forest site;
- independent unit = site/population;
- I = 1997 site-level abundance of the sole euglossine pollinator *Eulaema cingulata*;
- F = 1997 fruit set on the same ten sites;
- 1996 and 1998 = temporal context/sensitivity only.

No Hedges-g effect has yet been admitted. The source article reports group and nested-model results,
but the exact ten-site paired I/F vectors still need recovery.
