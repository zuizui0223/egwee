# Phase-2 SF05 multilayer screen status — 2026-09-19

## Current state

The SF05 source-selected seed subset contains 65 studies, of which 25 were flagged from bibliographic/title-method metadata for possible additional EGWEE layers. All 25 flagged studies have now completed design-only screening against the same-exposure and independent-unit rules.

No candidate was retained or closed because of effect direction, statistical significance, effect magnitude, or a candidate-specific p-value. No new quantitative effect or cluster has been admitted.

Machine-readable decisions are in `evidence/meta_extraction/phase2_sf05_multilayer_screen_v1.csv`.

## Screening result

### Advance to quantitative full-text screening — 6

These studies already have a source-defined fragmentation contrast or replicated fragmentation landscape design that could support a quantitative EGWEE effect if the exact common frame, endpoints and uncertainty are recoverable.

- **SF05-42 — Van Rossum & Triest 2003, Primula vulgaris:** 41-population fragmented landscape with genetic and reproductive-success layers; exact common exposure/endpoint frame still needs source verification.
- **SF05-4 — Bizoux et al. 2009, Milicia excelsa:** four populations including open-habitat and continuous-forest populations; verify that the source treatment definition is an admissible fragmentation exposure.
- **SF05-32 — Wang et al. 2011, Castanopsis sclerophylla:** populations span fragmentation severity with pre- and post-fragmentation cohorts; strong candidate for cohort genetic and process-layer recovery.
- **SF05-71 — Côrtes et al. 2013, Heliconia acuminata:** two continuous-forest sites and three 1-ha fragments with parentage/propagule-dispersal responses.
- **SF05-83 — Browne & Karubian 2018, Oenocarpus bataua:** forest fragments versus continuous forest with pollen- and seed-mediated genetic contributions.
- **SF05-93 — Lompo et al. 2020, Parkia biglobosa:** two higher-fragmentation cotton populations and two lower-fragmentation non-cotton populations, with adult/embryo genetics and mating/gene-dispersal processes.

### Advance to exposure/layer verification — 4

These studies have replicated populations and potentially useful response geometry, but the present source frame does not yet establish that the predictor is an admissible source-defined fragmentation exposure for EGWEE.

- **SF05-11 — Duminil et al. 2016, Erythrophleum suaveolens:** three populations contrasting adult density in a logging context.
- **SF05-20 — Lemos et al. 2015, Schinus molle:** nine populations across the Brazilian Pampa; exact area/connectivity exposure needs recovery.
- **SF05-76 — Ismail et al. 2012, Dysoxylum malabaricum:** fragmented agro-forest landscape with low/high-density contexts and adult/juvenile genetic plus pollen/parentage responses; independent patch/stand replication must be verified.
- **SF05-86 — Raabová et al. 2015, Menyanthes trifoliata:** nine remnant populations varying in population size; whether that source predictor belongs in the registered fragmentation-severity stream must be decided before effect extraction.

### Closed on design geometry — 15

The remaining 15 flagged studies lack an admissible replicated fragmentation exposure, have only one fragmented treatment unit, represent a non-fragmentation disturbance/exposure, or duplicate an already structurally closed programme.

This group is retained as background/single-system evidence where useful, but nested trees, maternal families, offspring, loci, life stages or local neighborhoods are not promoted to fragmentation replicates.

## Quantitative priority

The next quantitative recovery order is fixed from design informativeness, not expected results:

1. **SF05-93 Parkia biglobosa** — replicated 2-vs-2 fragmentation contrast plus adult/embryo and process layers.
2. **SF05-32 Castanopsis sclerophylla** — replicated fragmentation gradient plus pre/post-fragmentation cohorts.
3. **SF05-71 Heliconia acuminata** — replicated 3-fragment / 2-continuous design with direct dispersal process.
4. **SF05-83 Oenocarpus bataua** — explicit fragment / continuous-forest design with seed- versus pollen-mediated components.
5. **SF05-42 Primula vulgaris** — unusually broad population network and genetic + reproductive function, pending exact common-frame recovery.
6. **SF05-4 Milicia excelsa** — replicated habitat populations, pending confirmation that open habitat is a source-defined fragmentation contrast.

The four exposure-verification studies remain behind this queue until their predictor definition passes the protocol firewall.

## Denominator constraint remains active

SF05 is still not a complete outcome-blind source frame. The source flow indicates at least nine identities were removed before the published 65-study table using source-outcome criteria. Those identities must be restored where recoverable, and the broader registered search frame should be reconstructed before SF05 screening can be declared complete.

Completing the 25-study design screen therefore removes one bottleneck but does not satisfy the systematic-frame completion gate. The frozen five-cluster Journal of Ecology baseline remains the submission-valid version until the preregistered promotion rule is met.
