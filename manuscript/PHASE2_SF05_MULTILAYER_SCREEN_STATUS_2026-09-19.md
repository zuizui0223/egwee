# Phase-2 SF05 multilayer screen status — 2026-09-19

## Current state

The SF05 source-selected seed subset contains 65 studies, of which 25 were flagged from bibliographic/title-method metadata for possible additional EGWEE layers. This file records the first design-only screening wave.

No effect direction, significance, effect magnitude, or candidate-specific p-value was used to retain or close a paper. No new quantitative effect has been opened or admitted.

## Wave 1

Four of the 25 flagged studies have been screened against the same-exposure and independent-unit requirements.

- **SF05-2 — Berens et al. 2014, Prunus africana:** closed for direct Phase-2 admission. The paper follows five life stages within Kakamega Forest and does not itself define a fragmented-versus-reference exposure contrast. It remains useful as life-cycle genetic background.
- **SF05-66 — Jin et al. 2012, Sinocalycanthus chinensis:** closed for direct Phase-2 admission. The source compares age classes within two populations described as fragmented, without a source-defined unfragmented/reference exposure.
- **SF05-96 — Ayre et al. 2009, Persoonia mollis ssp. nectens:** closed for direct Phase-2 admission. The design follows adult and seedling cohorts in one isolated population across repeated wildfires, not independent fragmentation treatment/reference units.
- **SF05-42 — Van Rossum & Triest 2003, Primula vulgaris:** advances to full-text quantitative screening. The paper jointly addresses population genetic variation/structure and reproductive success across a 41-population landscape with fragmentation explicitly in the study question. Admission is not claimed: the exact exposure coding, common population frame, independent unit counts, endpoints, uncertainty and covariance must still be verified from the full source.

Machine-readable decisions are in `evidence/meta_extraction/phase2_sf05_multilayer_screen_v1.csv`.

## Denominator constraint remains active

SF05 is still not a complete outcome-blind source frame. The source flow indicates at least nine identities were removed before the published 65-study table using source-outcome criteria. Those identities must be restored where recoverable, and the broader registered search frame should be reconstructed before SF05 screening can be declared complete.

Therefore this wave advances recoverability work only. It does not satisfy the systematic-frame completion gate and cannot supersede the frozen five-cluster Journal of Ecology baseline.
